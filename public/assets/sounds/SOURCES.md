# Sound sources

Copied from the TowerDefense repo (`public/assets/sounds/`), where they were
gathered; each file's original upload, by its name here. The creator prefixes
and ids look like Pixabay downloads; those without one were named by hand.
One-shots were peak-normalized to -1 dBFS; the loop was loudness-matched
(-20 LUFS), and the game cross-fades its end into its start.
