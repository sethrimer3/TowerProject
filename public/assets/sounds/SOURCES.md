# Sound sources

Every file here is Ogg Vorbis (quality 4), each one's original listed by its name here.
The `battle/` clips (but `slime-impact.ogg`), `ui/button-thud.ogg` and `ambience/wind-forest.ogg` came from the TowerDefense repo (`public/assets/sounds/`), where they were gathered; the creator prefixes and ids look like Pixabay downloads, and those without one were named by hand.
They were peak-normalized to -1 dBFS (the loop loudness-matched to -20 LUFS) there, and re-encoded from those MP3s as they were.
The rest came from `assets/UNSORTED SFX/` (our own, including `chestCreak.ogg` and `key.ogg`): one-shots made mono and peak-normalized to -1 dBFS, loops kept stereo and loudness-matched to -20 LUFS.
The game cross-fades each loop's end into its start.
