# Tower generation

How a Tower floor is built (`src/tower/`): the planning pipeline, door and key schedules, forks, embedding, the enemy schedule and stage, bosses, early-floor rules, and their tests.

**Tower** (`src/tower/`, entered via `RoomWorld` in `tower/room-world.ts`, which caches each floor's torches; `rollUnguardedLoot` lives in `tower/index.ts`): each floor is a self-contained 17×17 board (15×15 interior), deterministic for `(seed, room)`.
Pipeline in `tower/index.ts`: `strategic-graph.ts` plans a floor archetype, a main route of gated hubs to the stairs, and optional branches built from the declarative micro-puzzles in `patterns.ts` (enemy guards key, blue door holds several yellow keys, key chains, choice rooms, temptations…) → `forks.ts` turns some gated edges into forks: two or three parallel lanes (one to three tiles of gates, sometimes with an item between) into the same region, from the declarative `FORK_PATTERNS`, priced on one scale (`GATE_VALUE`) near the gate they replace, never into a region with nothing worth reaching;
each forked region carries its forks best first and its single gate as the last fallback → `door-quota.ts`, the door stage, places the floor's blue, red and Heart Doors (no other table offers them: `withoutQuotaDoors`; patterns built round one and forks holding one are left out of ordinary planning): `towerDoorHundredths` in `key-schedule.ts` gives each door's rate by tower and floor (`TOWER_DOOR_SCHEDULE`: blue from floor 51, red from 101 and Heart Doors from 201 in Tower I, 5, 10 and 10 floors earlier each later tower, each starting at a tenth of a door a floor and growing a hundredth every five floors for blue and every ten for red and hearts, blue and red until floor 1,000, hearts up to one a floor), the stage places doors until what they count for meets it (in hundredths of a door; a fraction left owed placed at its chance, `owesDoor`), and each door upgrades a gate, combines with a single door already there, never a run (a gate's `also` colours: one tile taking a key of each, `doorKeys`, drawn as the Amber + Azure … Triune doors; for hearts, a gate's `heart`, a door rule's `heart` flag, which drains HP to 1 after the keys, `drainsHp` in `doors.ts`, its art the keys' door with a heart crest, `door_bh.png` …), adds a pattern built round it, lengthens a single blue, red or Heart Door into a **door run** (a gate's `run`, `DoorRun`: two or three of the same door in a row, each counting; `runsWith`) or becomes a fork holding it, whose fallback gate is the door itself, which is what it counts as (`quotaFork` on the node; on the way to the stairs holding no other quota door; on a branch others may stand as extra doors; `redDoorOrElite`, a `towerOnly` fork pattern the Delve's `forksWorth` call leaves out) (in Tower I blue and red only off the way to the stairs);
what is still owed (below 0, over) is recorded as `dropped` in the graph's `doorQuota`;
`placeYellowRuns` makes some plain yellow doors off the way to the stairs yellow runs from floor 6 (`YELLOW_RUNS`, `yellowRun` in `key-schedule.ts`), a key sink;
then `placeWoodenDoors` makes `towerWoodPercent` (`TOWER_WOOD_SCHEDULE`) of the floor's yellow locks Wooden Doors (a `wood` gate and stairs guard; the `wood` door rule, `{ type: "wood", durability }`, durability from `woodDurability` in `enemy-curves.ts`, twice the floor's normal, balanced enemy's ATK, which `tierTile` multiplies like enemy stats: any one key opens it, cheapest first, and with none `doorCost` lets the hero break it down for that much HP, DEF not reducing it, unless that would fell the hero; `resolveStep` takes the HP, `Game`'s `openDoor` raises it as a heart, and it never costs an area's mastery; the Delve's steel gates are Wooden Doors too) → `resource-planner.ts` gives doors plausible key sources with depth-dependent probability, without enforcing parity, aiming for each colour's keys per lock (`towerKeyRatio`, `TOWER_KEY_RATIO` in `key-schedule.ts`: yellow from 1.3, blue from 1.2 and red from 1.1 on each colour's first floor, falling by tower and floor, half as fast on the way to the stairs): `thinToAim` keeps the floor's pattern keys of a colour at the chance that leaves the aim's worth for its locks (a room left with no item gets a potion), `coverDoor` rolls for key sources while short, at the chance of how short, never past the floor's aim for the colour (`floorAim`), and unguarded loot keeps a key at the aim (`keepsKey`);
`lockColors` counts a forked region as its single gate, a door run each of its doors and a yellow run once;
the first floor a blue or red key can appear on in a tower lays one in the start hall;
`tests/key-supply.test.ts` → `embedder.ts` tiles the interior with chambers joined by one-tile gate doorways (no corridors), lays each fork's lanes side by side in a shared wall (a lane deeper than one tile runs through a band of rows taken from one of the two chambers; the first fork that fits wins, else the single gate), then, on its own stream (`settling`, so every tier draws the rest alike), settles what each fork that fits changes (a fork of k lanes counts 1/k of each lane's doors, `forkCredit`): `settleQuota` lengthens or shortens door runs by the quota doors owed or over, and `settleKeys` adds or takes away room keys by the keys its lanes ask for against its gate's, places shortcuts and stairs, and trims chambers to their contents (`FloorBuilder`), then `furnisher.ts` (`enemyTile`, which the enemy stage also uses) arranges each chamber (clear walking lanes, enemy rings, guarded niches, item rows, mirrored pillars; `grid.ts` holds the shared rectangle helpers), and `layRuns` lays each door run's other doors on the tile just inside the region it opens and, for three, just outside in the parent's chamber, where they keep the chamber whole (a door with no tile stays owed; `tests/door-runs.test.ts`) → `geometryProblems()` check → `analyzer.ts` reports doors, key sources, branches, dead ends, density.
`census.ts` averages what floors hold (doors by kind, combined ones and those standing in built forks apart, each quota door's scheduled rate and drops, keys and keys per lock, enemies by strength against the enemy schedule's shares and count, with their weight on the forks' `GATE_VALUE` scale, items) by tower and band of floors, for tuning the door and key schedule in `docs/DOOR_AND_KEY_SCHEDULE.md`, whose section 5 explains its options and columns (`tests/tower-census.test.ts`), and the enemy schedule.
The **enemy schedule** (`src/enemy-schedule.ts`, `ENEMY_SCHEDULE`; `docs/ENEMY_SCHEDULE.md`, which holds its build order) sets, by tower and floor in whole percents, each strength's share of a floor's enemies, bosses left out (`enemyShare`: weak fading, strong and elite from their first floors, `enemyFirstFloor`, earlier each tower, rising to their caps, normal the rest), each profile's (`profileShare`: balanced fading from 60% by floor and tower to 20%, attack- and defense-heavy splitting the rest; any strength may wear any profile, bosses balanced) and its count in percent of what generation places (`enemyCountPercent`: a point every 30 floors, to 300% from floor 6,000);
the Tower's **enemy stage** (`tower/enemy-stage.ts`, `placeEnemies`, run by `generateTowerFloor` after the boss and before the unguarded loot, on its own stream) places by it: it adds `extraEnemies` on random plain floor tiles (never the way in, the tile just inside, beside the stairs or in a fork's lane), then re-deals every enemy but bosses and fork lanes' the shares' strengths, weakest asked weakest (`rankStrengths`, in `src/enemy-stage.ts`, the rules the Delve shares), and the profile shares' profiles (`dealProfiles`), recording `enemyCount` (baseline, added, dropped) on the graph;
the Delve runs it too, last in `region()`, each of an area's ten equivalent floors by the tower of its number (`placeEnemies` in `delve/labyrinth.ts`: never the way in or beside it, near the milestone gate or its boss, or in a fork's lane; `Region.enemyCount`);
`tests/enemy-schedule.test.ts`, `tests/enemy-stage.test.ts`.
`towerDebug()` is available in the browser console.
Floors come in sections of `TOWER_SECTION` (10): a section's first floor is sealed below, and every other floor has stairs down.
A section's last floor (`isBossFloor`) gets its boss after embedding, on the one tile beside the stairs (replacing any stairs guard), so it must be beaten to climb; the Delve puts one in each milestone gate's shaft.
Otherwise the stairs pocket's `stairsGuard` (on that same tile) is a normal or strong enemy, a yellow door (half the unguarded stairs; the resource planner covers it like a main-route door), or nothing.
The first floors follow their own rules (`openFirstFloor` and `keyedFloor` in `patterns.ts`), kept by the minimal fallback floor too: floor 1's main route and stairs stand open, so the starting hand reaches the stairs without a fight; floors 2 to 5 always have the yellow stairs door, a yellow key in the start hall, a key for each key every door takes lying behind it (`keyEveryDoor` in `resource-planner.ts`), their main-route doors always paid for, no shortcuts and no forks over a locked gate or with locked lanes.
On the first tower's first ten floors (`earlyPotions` in `strategic-graph.ts`), about 35% of the doorways on the way to the stairs hold a potion (a `potion` gate) in place of an enemy: it takes `earlyPotionShare` of each main-route gate table's weight out of the enemies', and the doorways those floors force open (floor 1's, the stairs pocket's on floors 2 to 5) roll the same chance; a potion gate never forks.
Generation places no strong enemy below floor 11 and no elite below floor 41 in Tower I, each earlier in later towers (`strengthOnFloor` by tower and `STRENGTH_FROM_FLOOR` in `scaling.ts`, the enemy schedule's `enemyFirstFloor`: one asked for stands one strength lower, the furnisher's `enemyTile` and the Delve's `gateTile`, by equivalent floor and the delve's number, alike), and no Heart Door below its first floor (by the door stage's schedule, `towerDoorFirstFloor`, the Delve's by equivalent floor), so no enemy on floors 1 to 10 but the boss beats a new hero in one fight.
`tests/floor-schedule.test.ts` covers these schedules, and `tests/door-quota.test.ts` the door stage.
`tests/early-floors.test.ts` checks all of these, playing the base hand up floors 2 to 5.
A boss is the `boss` strength: a multiplier on its own floor's enemy curve (`enemy-curves.ts`: in the Tower ×4 HP, ×1.75 ATK and ×1.3 DEF, a long fight that DEF beats; in the Delve twice a strong enemy's HP and ATK), so a boss placed on any floor takes that floor's value.
A floor's secret is the `greaterBoss` strength (`tower/greater-boss.ts`, Tower only): once every torch on the floor is out (`callsGreaterBoss`, checked by `Game.land` on every step but onto stairs, since undo brings back the run but not the torches), one appears on the plain floor tile nearest the stairs by walk (`greaterBossSpot`, never the hero's or the Gem's), written into `run.changes` (the one enemy a saved change may hold) with the floor recorded in `run.summoned` so it comes once a floor a run; `game.summoned` has the renderer play `poof.ts`'s one-second puff of smoke over it.
It has `GREATER_BOSS_OVER_BOSS` (2) times a boss's HP, ATK and DEF, twice a boss's Gold, Silver and XP, a violet rim and four chevrons, and opens no tier.
`tests/greater-boss.test.ts` covers it.
`tests/bosses.test.ts` checks both modes' bosses block the only way up.
`tests/tower-layout.test.ts` hashes whole floors (tiles and embeddings) for a spread of seeds and depths, plus raw `embed()` retries, against `tests/fixtures/tower-layout.golden.json`.
A change there changes saved maps, so it also needs a `LAYOUT_VERSION` bump; regenerate with `UPDATE_GOLDEN=1` only when the layout change is intended.
`tests/tower-planning.test.ts` hashes strategic graphs (the main route, branches, shortcuts, and the resource planner's key sources and notes) over many more seeds, depths and budget cuts, `analyzeFloor` and its summary text for real floors, and `keyEconomy` and `asciiMap` on seeded synthetic boards against `tests/fixtures/tower-planning.golden.json`; regenerate it with `UPDATE_GOLDEN=1` only for an intended planning or analysis change.
`tests/tower-forks.test.ts` checks that every fork pattern's lanes cost about the same with none cheaper in every way, how keys found in a lane pay for later doors, and that built forks lead in by every lane and never into an empty dead end.
