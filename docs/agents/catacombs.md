# Catacombs

The Catacombs' engine (`src/catacombs/`), an idle side area where the hero, as a Magician, fights undead a turn a second.
The design and build plan is `docs/CATACOMBS.md`; this doc covers what is built.

Built so far: step 1, the engine; step 2, the unlock and the save; step 3, the Mausoleum and the Catacomb button; step 4, the Catacomb screen; step 5, its Stats menu (now Skills); step 6, the time away and the currencies table; step 7, the Codex; step 8, the spawn table and set halls, Magic skills, Drops, MCrits, crosshairs and potions, abilities and Spirit upgrades.
The engine is pure and knows nothing of the DOM, `Game`, the board drawing or the clock: the game layer (step 3 on) passes it the wall clock, runs the turns it returns, and credits the Lore it reports.
The screen (`ui/catacomb-view.ts`) runs the engine on the wall clock and credits the Lore it reports.

## Unlock and save

- Tower VI's floor 20 Goal, Catacombs (`GoalUnlock` `catacombs`), reveals the Inspiration skill Reveal Catacombs (`catacombs`, 40 Inspiration; the node's `revealGoal`, progression.md).
- `open.ts`: `catacombsOpen(save)`, the skill owned or Access to Everything on.
- `save.catacombs` is a `CatacombSave`: `defaultCatacombs()` in `defaults()`, `decodeCatacombs` in `decode()`; the profile file keeps it in `towers` with its run cleared (`exportProfile`).

## The way in

- `OutsideWorld`'s seventh flag, `mausoleum` (set by `Game.forestWorld` from `catacombsOpen`; the Tower's forest only), raises the Mausoleum at `MAUSOLEUM` (`outside.ts`: by the entrance column, `dx` 7, rows 1 to 3, a 3 × 3 block of walls) in a yard one tile wider that the trees and grass leave clear (`forestProtected`, `Roads.mausoleum` in the grass's cache key, the cloud shadows' key).
  `drawMausoleum` draws the native 72-pixel `forest-mausoleum` PNG; `Renderer` draws it after the Tournament Hall.
- `Game.atMausoleum(x, y)` is what `BoardOverlay`'s tap tests (its sixth callback); `Game.reshapeForest()` rebuilds the forest when a building opens, which the Upgrades page calls on buying Reveal Catacombs.
- `game/catacomb-desk.ts`: `CatacombDesk` (`game.catacombs`): `open`, `run`, `number`, `wouldLeave(number)` (the number and hall a run of another number past Hall 1 would leave) and `enter(number)` (refused while shut).
- `ui/catacombs-entry.ts`: `enterFromMausoleum` enters the forest's `save.tower.tier`, confirming first when `wouldLeave` says so, then opens the Catacomb screen.
- The menu's `#catacombs-button` (`ui/shell.ts`) shows once the Catacombs are open and a run exists (`renderRunMenu`); a Proving Grounds trial's cloned save has no Catacomb run (`TrialSession`), so it never shows there.

## The Catacomb screen

- `catacombs/player.ts`: `CatacombPlayer.step(save, wallNow, frameNow)` runs the turns `tick` says are due, at most `DRAWN_PER_SECOND` (15) times a second of frame time, so at ×100 a step takes six or seven turns and only the last is drawn; it credits their Lore to `save.cells` (`earn`) and returns the clock's verdict, the gains and the last turn's event.
- `FrameLoopHost.background` runs every frame on every page (`frame-loop.ts`): `main.ts` passes `CatacombView.frame`, so the Catacombs play on behind any page, and a hidden tab's gap goes to the bank (`BANK_GAP_MS`).
- `ui/catacomb-view.ts`: `CatacombView` opens on the run under way (the Mausoleum's tap, the menu's Catacomb button) and closes with Switch Back or Escape.
  While open it owns the board through `FrameLoopHost.boardFrame` (after a trial, which comes first), so no Tower or Delve turn, route or fight runs behind it, and its capture listeners keep the board's taps and the game's keys from the run.
  `html.catacombs` hides what the run shows and the Catacomb has no use for (`style.css`: the hand, Training bar, auto and speed, End Climb, the Shop, keys, Silver, shield, shroud, boosts, inspect and route boxes, the tabs' icons); `.catacomb-only` pieces show only then, made once on first open (`build`): Lore in the purse, the magic skill points in the portrait's top-right corner (the level row has no room for them beside the MXP bar), Switch Back (an arrow back over its name) under the hamburger where the run's Shop sits, the Spirit grid in the Gold bonus's place (`#catacomb-spirit`, `spiritGridHtml`: two columns of three, Common to Mythic, or Uncommon to Ancestral once any Ancestral is held, `shownSpirits`), and the bank over the board's top right (`#catacomb-bank`: the clock, `bankTime`, the ×0 (Pause) ×1 ×2 ×5 ×10 ×25 ×100 `select`, `hasteLabel`, always shown, since pausing needs nothing banked).
  Closing the screen while paused sets the haste to ×1 (`unpause`), and so does `frame` for a save paused when the app closed: a Catacomb nobody watches plays on, or spends its bank at the haste chosen.
  A restart flashes "The hero rises and tries again..." centred over the hall's upper part (`flashRestart`, `#catacomb-flash` in `#board-frame`, the free levels' box at the status line's size), shown 2.5 seconds and fading over the last half.
  `decorate`, called by `main.ts`'s `update` after the HUD renders and after each step, writes MHP, MAtk and MDef into the HP, ATK and DEF slots, MAGICIAN, the Magic level and MXP (the bar purple), the hall in the floor's place, the Spirit held in the Gold bonus's (`#gold-bonus` hidden), the Wisp's stats here in the base enemy box, the heading and a status line per turn (`turnLine`).
  The board's heading is marked `catacombs`, so the run's heading is drawn again on Switch Back.
- `ui/catacomb-odds.ts`: `catacombOddsHtml(hall, number)`, the base enemy box's table on the Catacomb screen, laid out as the run's enemy table: each type's rank chevrons (`strengthMarks`) and picture (`undeadIconUrl`) by its name, its chance a slot, its MHP, MAtk and MDef there, MXP and Spirit (the columns headed by icons: the run's heart, sword and shield, `mxpIcon`, a Spirit icon), and the ranked last slot's, then the Lore bonus (Tower N's Gold bonus); it follows the hero to each new hall, and `refreshEnemyOddsBox` leaves it alone.
- `ui/catacomb-menus.ts`: `CatacombMenus`, laid out as a run's hand over its tabs (mounted on first open): Skills, Spirit, Forge and Academy as tabs in the tab bar itself (`#catacomb-tabs` in `nav`, its own buttons, so every UI theme's tab bar dresses them; the page tabs and the hand hidden), and over the bar `#catacomb-menus`, the abilities over the open menu's panel, the panel always as tall (scrolling what doesn't fit) so nothing moves as the tabs change.
  Skills (`menuPanelHtml`) shows the table button, then a box per `MAGIC_SKILL_IDS` entry (`skillBoxHtml`: its name and points of its cap; tapped, it widens to − and + buttons, each disabled when it can't be pressed, and a line per effect, `skillLines`: a point's worth and the total, percent effects with their fraction), then the points free.
  `movePoint` moves a point; the view's `decorate` shows it on the HUD at once, and the engine counts it from the next turn.
  The table button opens `catacombStatRows` (`catacombs/summary.ts`: MHP, MAtk, MDef, MCrit chance and factor, Armor Penetration, Spirit and MXP gain, Speed Drop attacks left, each Drop's chance and effect, the Gold Crosshair chance, Magic level, MXP to next, points, the Catacomb, hall, deepest hall, Lore bonus, Lore, every Spirit and Ingot), put away by a tap anywhere.
  Spirit shows the Spirit held and an `upgradeHtml` per Spirit upgrade (level, what a level does, a buy button with its price as the rarity's icon and amount, disabled while short, MAX once maxed: Spirit isn't Gems, so the Gem rule doesn't apply); Forge and Academy say what they will do and show what they will spend (`LATER`).
  The ability row (`abilitiesHtml`, `#catacomb-abilities`, hidden until one is unlocked) is a slot an unlocked ability: a slim Auto toggle (`aria-pressed`), the cast button (disabled without a charge, the buff's attacks left under its name) and the turns to its next charge or *Ready*; a mouse hover, or a touch held `HOLD_MS`, shows `.catacomb-tip`, and the press that showed it casts nothing.
  The view re-renders the panel and the ability row in `decorate` only when their HTML changes.
- `render/catacomb-board.ts`: `CatacombBoard` draws the hall on `#world` at its own size: crypt stone from the Tower's tiles (`paintTile`, Tower III's look, addressed bottom up since `paintTile` reads north as y + 1), the stairs, the torches standing on the floor (each treasure room's, and the hall's up and right of the Lore), the Lore tablet and Ingot bars not yet taken, potions (the red potion of a Tower or Delve run, `paintContents`), the seven native undead sprites (`sprites/catacombs.ts`) with rank chevrons, a run's HP bar over each one struck (`paintHpBar`, draining to each strike and fading once it is banished, behind the Enemy HP bars setting), a native icon per Drop (MXP crystal, Loot pouch, Speed boot, MHP heart) in a row centred over the enemy above its bar, each with a light green one-pixel rim (`drawPixelOutline`'s `green`), and a pulsing crosshair (red, or gold) turning once every 6 seconds (still under Reduce motion), the Lore tablet and Ingots with the gold rim a run's pickups wear (both rims drawn over the dark falloff), the hero tweened to each turn's tile and leaning into each strike (the undead leaning back as it answers), a dark falloff from the hero, and what rises off the board through a run's own `BoardPopups` (render.md): each strike's number (a crit's glow, a gold crosshair's gold ring), the hero's damage, a potion's or MHP Drop's heal, a pickup rising from its tile (Lore's tablet, an Ingot after its amount) and a kill's MXP and Spirit as drops (`Paid`, which `defeat` returns and the round, Barrage and crosshair events carry).
  A tapped crosshair calls down a Thunderbolt's bolt and light (`paintThunderbolt`, `paintThunderlight`), its number rising and the undead's bar draining as it lands.
  It plays a run's cues (`playAfter` in `sound.ts`): `punch` for the hero's strike and the undead's answer, `bodyFall` for a kill or a fall, `lightningZap` for a crosshair, `potion`, `key` for Lore and Ingots (until they have their own), and `stoneSteps` down the stairs; the view passes the Settings' hit-number, HP-gain and enemy-bar rows (`settings`).
- The view's capture `pointerdown` on `#world` maps the tap to a hall tile (`tap`): a slot's column on the corridor or the wall just above or below it taps that slot's crosshair (`tapCrosshair`), any other tile a potion (`takePotion`); either draws its popup, writes `tapLine` and saves.
  Every tap also shows the tile's box (`#catacomb-inspect`, a run's `inspect-box`, with `#catacomb-highlight` on the tile; above the tile, or below it with no room): `catacombTileInfo` (`ui/catacomb-tile-info.ts`) gives an undead's MHP, MAtk with its strike back, MDef with the Magician's strike, MXP and Spirit (tile-info's `stat` lines, gains and Drops counted), its Drops and crosshair, and always the forecast (`forecastFight`), in Damage Prediction's and Combat Forecast's words; the Lore and Ingots still to take, the stairs, a potion, torches, walls and floor.
  A crosshair's tap shows its undead's box (none once it is banished, nor for a potion drunk); `decorate` rewrites the box each turn and puts it away once its undead or pickup is gone; a second tap on the tile puts it away; a wall's, floor's or entrance's fades after 3 s (`plainTile`).
- The board draws each undead with the glow and black outline a run's enemy of its rank wears (`paintRingedSprite` in `render/tile-painters.ts`, sharing `bakeEnemy`'s rings).

## Time away and currencies

- `bankGap(clock, now)` (`clock.ts`) banks a gap over `BANK_GAP_MS` since the clock's last turn; `tick` uses it for a hidden tab, and `CatacombDesk.arrive(now)` for the app starting (only while the Catacombs are open and a run is under way).
- `main.ts` calls `arrive` right after `arriveOffline`, before anything saves, and the Offline dialog (`ui/offline-gold-dialog.ts`) adds `catacombLine` (*Catacombs: +10,800 banked turns (03h:00m)*); with no Offline Gold or Gems owed it opens for that line alone, headed *The Catacombs*.
- The currencies table (`currencyRows`) lists the seven Spirits and four Ingots after Lore once the Catacombs are open, their icons `spiritIcon` and `ingotIcon` (`ui/dom.ts`, drawn in CSS until their PNGs).

## Modules

- `catalog.ts`: the data.
  `UNDEAD` (Wisp to Demon, each its rarity, MHP, MAtk, MDef, MXP and first hall, the hall `SPAWN_TABLE` first gives it odds), `UNDEAD_OF` (the undead of each rarity), `SPIRIT_RARITIES` (common to ancestral, its own list, though it matches `RarityId`), `INGOTS` and `INGOT_EVERY`, `STRENGTHS` (normal, strong ×1.25, elite ×1.5, boss MHP ×3.5, MAtk ×1.3, MDef ×1; rewards ×1, ×2, ×4, ×10), `hallStrength` (a hall's final enemy: Boss every 25th, else Elite every 10th, else Strong every 5th), `DEEP_HALLS` and `deepFactors` (MHP and MDef compounding from the hall after each listed one), `SPAWN_TABLE` and `spawnOdds` (the designer's odds a slot by hall band, lowest rarity first, gated by each type's first hall), `FIXED_HALLS` and `fixedHall` (the set halls' six, lowest rarity first), `undeadStats` (base × `tierStats(number)` × strength × deep halls, on the `snap` grid), `undeadRewards`, the Lore tile's amount (`loreOnTile`: `tierGold(number, LORE_PER_TILE)`, 1 before the bonus), and `DROPS` (each Drop's name, range and effect line).
- `hall.ts`: a hall's 17 × 17 layout.
  `hallCell(hall, x, y)` is what a tile is before anyone acts (the corridor rows 7 to 9, every slot's column walled above and below, the treasure rooms of `ROOMS` on halls that have them, each Ingot and its room's torch on the room's far edge, and every hall's torch up and right of the Lore, `LORE_TORCH`, which `SIDE_TILES` leaves out so no potion lies there); `hallTargets(hall, number, slots)` lists the hero's targets in order (E1 to E3, the Lore, the Ingots brass, steel, iron, nickel, E4 to E6, the stairs; empty and beaten slots left out); `rollSlots(hall, number, rng)` rolls the six slots, one draw each, the final slot of a ranked hall a second draw weighted among the open types, and one Wisp on the last slot of a hall that rolled none, while a set hall draws nothing and stands as `fixedHall` says, E6 at the hall's strength; `ingotAmount` is its nth room times the number.
  A `Slot` carries its Drops' amounts (`drops`) and its crosshair (`mark`: `crosshair` or `gold`); `SIDE_TILES` are the corridor's rows 7 and 9 bar the slots' walls and the Lore column, where potions lie.
  The `Board` that draws a hall comes with the Catacomb screen (step 4).
- `magic.ts`: Magic levels and skills.
  `mxpToNext(level)` is 200 × 1.2^(level − 1), rounded, its powers multiplied out in order; `mxpForLevel` totals them (level 2 at 200, 3 at 440); `magicLevel`, `pointsEarned` (a point a level past the first); `MAGIC_SKILLS` (Might, Weaving, Resonance, Focus, Fortune: each its cap and three `MagicEffect`s a point, `EFFECT_NAMES` naming them), `effectTotal`, `HERO_BASE` (1,000 MHP, 10 MAtk, 0 MDef, MCrit ×1.5), `magician(c)` (the `Magician`: MHP, MAtk as (base + Might + Magic ATK +1) × Might's percent, MDef, MCrit chance and factor, Armor Penetration flat and percent, the Spirit and MXP factors, each Drop's chance with Fortune's added, the Gold Crosshair chance), `movePoint` (never below 0, past a skill's cap or past the points earned) and `skillLines`.
- `abilities.ts`: `ABILITIES` (Spell Surge, Renewing Rush, Arcane Barrage: cooldown in turns, 5 attacks, the tooltip), `MAX_CHARGES` (1), `abilityUnlocked` (by `upgrades.unlock`), `canCast`, `castAbility` (a charge spent, the cooldown started if none ran, the buff's attacks added, Rush's 50 MHP), `coolAbilities` (a turn off each unlocked ability short of a charge) and `autoCast`.
- `spirit-upgrades.ts`: `SPIRIT_UPGRADES` (Unlock Ability's three prices, Magic ATK +1's 25), `upgradePrice` and `buyUpgrade`.
- `combat.ts`: `CatacombGains` and `noGains`, `striking` (the Magician with Spell Surge's MAtk and MCrit damage while it lasts), `pierced` (MDef less Armor Penetration, percent then flat), `hitFor` (MAtk less MDef, then × the MCrit factor if it crits and × Barrage's share), `rollCrit` (the `crits` stream, no draw at 0%) and `defeat` (MXP and Spirit times strength, gains and the MXP and Loot Drops, the Speed Drop's attacks, the MHP Drop up to the maximum, the slot emptied).
- `taps.ts`: `rollTaps` (after each turn: a 5% crosshair on each undead without one, gold at the Gold Crosshair chance; then a 5% potion on a free side tile), `tapCrosshair` (one hit, MCrit rolled, ×3 gold, no turn) and `takePotion` (10 MHP up to the maximum).
- `state.ts`: `CatacombSave` (with the Speed Drop attacks, the abilities, buffs and Spirit upgrades) and `CatacombRun` (with its potions), `defaultCatacombs`, the saved streams (`drawing(c, which, draw)`: `spawns`, `drops`, `crits`, `crosshairs` and `potions`, each seeded once from its named stream, `catacombs`, `catacombDrops`, `catacombCrits`, `crosshairs` and `potions`, its state stored after each draw, as the missions' and gifts' are), `rollDrops`, `enterHall` (spawns, then Drops, the latter only at a chance above 0), `startRun` and `enterCatacomb` (the same number keeps the run, another starts Hall 1 there).
- `turn.ts`: `catacombTurn(c, gains)` takes one turn: the abilities cool a turn; a restart if the hero fell; else those on Auto are cast, then, by the next target, a descent (the stairs: the next hall rolled, the hero at its entrance with the MHP it had), a pickup (Lore into `gains.lore`, Ingots into the save), a dash (to the tile left of the next enemy) or a round; then `rollTaps`.
  A round: the hero strikes `strikesNow` times (one, plus one with Speed Drop attacks left, plus one under Renewing Rush), each `hitFor` with its own MCrit roll, stopping when the target falls; Arcane Barrage then hits every undead standing for 20% of a hit past its MDef; each buff and the Speed Drops lose an attack; the beaten pay (`defeat`); a target still standing strikes back, and 0 MHP marks the run `fell`.
  Neither side able to hurt the other (at the best MCrit, with no Barrage) restarts the run that same turn; nothing raises MDef yet, so only an undead without MAtk can.
  MHP is capped at the current maximum at the start of each turn, so moved points apply on the next turn and taking Vitality back never heals.
  A kill empties its slot, so the run's `target` index (every earlier target done) already points at the next target.
  `runTurns(c, n)` takes `n` turns across however many runs and sums their gains.
- `clock.ts`: `tick(clock, now, carry)` returns the turns due: a whole second gone is a real turn; a gap over `BANK_GAP_MS` (10 s, the app closed or the tab hidden) banks its whole seconds instead, which is how offline time reaches the bank; at ×N each second also owes N − 1 hasted turns (`HasteCarry`, not saved, carries the fraction), paid from the bank, and what the bank can't pay drops the haste to ×1 (`slowed`).
  At ×0 (paused) each whole second goes to the bank (`banked`) instead of being played.
  `setHaste` refuses haste with nothing banked, and always allows ×0; `offlineSeconds` is what the next tick will bank, for the Offline dialog.
- `forecast.ts`: `forecastFight(c, i)`, slot `i`'s fight played out from now on copies (MHP now, buffs and Speed Drop attacks running down a round at a time, Spell Surge's MAtk, Arcane Barrage's share), without MCrits or abilities cast: the strikes to banish it, the MHP the whole fight costs (Infinity when it can't end), whether the Magician lives, and a stalemate.
- `save.ts`: `decodeCatacombs`, reading every field through the shared readers (MXP and Spirit keep their fractions; a Drop out of its range, a mark not known, a potion off the side tiles or doubled, a level or cooldown past its maximum is dropped); a run that doesn't fit its hall (wrong slots, a target past the hall's last) is dropped, and points spent past those earned, or past a skill's cap, are handed back.
- `open.ts`: `catacombsOpen`.
- `index.ts` re-exports them.

## Rules

- Everything the engine decides is saved or played by, so it uses only `+ - * /` and `Math.round`: no `Math.pow` or `**`, no draws in a sort.
  `tests/engine-independence.test.ts` fingerprints its halls, stats, levels and a 3,000-turn run, and scans `src/catacombs/` for `**`.
- Spawns, Drops, MCrits, crosshairs and potions each draw only from their own saved stream, so drawing from one never shifts another and a run replays from its save; a hall and its Drops are rolled once, as the hero arrives, so a reload never rerolls them.
- The engine credits only what the Catacombs keep (MXP, Spirit, Ingots, best halls); Lore belongs to `save.cells` and is reported for the caller to credit with `earn`.

## Native Catacombs artwork

`tools/prepare-catacomb-sprites.mjs` exports the retained generated sheets in `assets/game-sprites/catacombs-sources/` to 33 native PNGs with the shared palette, binary alpha and clear padding.
`sprites/catacombs.ts` names the seven undead, eight Spirit subjects including fragments, separate board/UI ingots, five menu icons, four buff marks and the 72-pixel Mausoleum.
Spirit currently has seven live rarity balances; the fragment asset and `spiritIcon("fragments")` are available without adding a balance or conversion rule.
Currency helpers, upgrade prices, currency tables, the Codex, the in-run Catacomb button and the four menu tabs use the shared icon renderer in both atlas modes.
The board uses whole backing pixels per native texel; buff marks stay outside the body and MHP bar, within the space before the next slot.
New atlas entries append after existing artwork, preserving old source rectangles and atlas dimensions.

`node tests/catacomb-sprites.mjs` checks literal texels for all 33 subjects in both atlas modes, real phone/desktop forest and menu layouts, all Codex portraits and read-only board rendering.
`tests/catacomb-sprites.test.ts` checks native sizes, exact palette, binary alpha, transparent edges, unique art and currency helpers.
The appended `catacombSprites` render scene shows the Mausoleum, every undead and buff mark, all four Ingot rooms and consumed pickups without moving existing scene seeds.
