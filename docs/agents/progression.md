# Progression and economy

Enemy curves, the loadout, fractions, levels and Training (points and trainers), the Archives, free purchases, skill trees, and Equipment.

**Progression/economy.** Balancing, upgrade definitions, and currency rewards are in `src/config.ts`.
Every enemy's stats, in both modes, come from `src/enemy-curves.ts` (`enemyStats(mode, tier, step, strength, profile)`, a step being a Tower floor counted from 0 or a Delve depth): one **enemy curve** per mode and tier (`ENEMY_CURVES`, falling back to `DEFAULT_CURVE`) gives a normal, balanced enemy's HP and ATK as anchors (`[floor, value]`, growth curves: one rate a step between anchors, the last rate carrying on) and its DEF as a share of ATK (`defenseOfAttack`, a multiplier curve that holds its last value), and each mode's `strength` and `profile` tables multiply it (an elite reads the curve `ahead` ten floors in the Tower; a boss is a multiplier on its own floor's curve; a Greater Boss twice a boss, `GREATER_BOSS_OVER_BOSS`).
Rates come from `root` and `intPow` (`exact.ts`), so stats are the same in every engine; they are rounded to hundredths, and the tier's factor (`tierStats`) still applies on top.
Generation passes the tier in (the Tower's floor graph carries it as `tower`, the Delve's lab as `tier`), so a tier given its own curve generates from it; the Delve's region cache is keyed by tier.
Tower enemies' names come from the ten-floor zone rosters (`TOWER_ZONE_ENEMIES` in `src/scaling.ts`, `towerEnemyName`, one per profile; each tower starts the cycle one zone further along, `towerZoneIndex`), and every generated enemy carries its `profile`, the Delve's from `DELVE_ENEMY_NAMES`.
`tests/enemy-curves.test.ts` covers the anchors, that every stat only rises, and that every enemy's DEF stays at most half its ATK.
What a character starts a run with (ATK, DEF, max HP, shroud, keys and undo capacity) is worked out only by `loadout(save)` in `src/loadout.ts`: the baseline, then each upgrade's and provision's `grants` from its row in `config.ts` and each stat's training (`trained`: what its ranks add up to on the row's own super-linear rank curve, `curve` in `TRAINING`, never on the hero's level; the next rank's worth `trainingWorth`), then the mode's worn Equipment (`wornEffects`: flat, then percent of the total), then provisions, then the Archives' ATK, DEF, Max HP and Shroud research multiplying the whole stat (`statFactor`, which run training and the siphons apply to each rank too), every fraction kept (`loadout(save, mode)`: each mode's hero wears its own loadout; Equipment's starting keys sit apart in `startKeys`, which `startingHero` adds only as a run starts, so `changeLoadout` never hands them to a run inside).
**Fractions:** Gold, HP and the hero's and enemies' stats keep their fractions (a 2% bonus on 1 ATK strikes for 1.02); each sum or difference of them in `loadout`, `trained`, `combat.ts`, `step-effects.ts`, the potions and `Game` is snapped to a grid of millionths (`snap` in `exact.ts`), so the forecast (`predict`) and the fight played out (`bout`) agree to the last strike (`tests/fractions.test.ts`), the same in every engine.
The screen shows them through `whole.ts`: held amounts (Gold, the hero's stats) rounded down (`whole`; HP never 0 while standing, `wholeHp`), amounts gained or lost (strikes, heals, rewards) to the nearest whole (`wholeChange`), and enemy stats to two decimals when not whole (`enemyStat`).
Saves keep Gold and section HP with their fractions.
The Upgrades and Gear pages write those rows' descriptions from the same grants (`upgradeText`, `provisionText`), so a stat's number changes in its row and nowhere else.
Each run keeps the loadout it started with (`run.loadout`), and a gear change or training shifts a run inside and its player by exactly what the change did to `loadout(save)`, so ATK/DEF gathered from items are kept; the run's undo history and a fall's snapshot shift with it, so undo takes back steps, never what was bought.
Provisions last for good, like training: `gear.buyProvision` (`GearDesk`, `game/gear-desk.ts`, the game's `gear`) goes through the same `changeLoadout` (`game/hero-sync.ts`) (which also hands a run inside the keys a provision adds), at a price that rises with each one bought (`provisionPrice`, on `schedulePrice`, the schedule run training's Silver prices use, which the stat provisions compound by `PROVISION_COMPOUND`, 5%, a buy).
A run still in the forest hasn't started: after every purchase, unlock, training, gear change or research completed, `readyForestRuns` (`game/hero-sync.ts`) gives it (in both modes) the hero and loadout everything owned now makes.
Levels come from lifetime XP (`levelForXp`, `xpForLevel`), paid per kill by the enemy's strength and the equivalent floor (`xpForKill`, `XP_MULTIPLIER`), so both modes pay alike and part of each undo snapshot (`MoveSnapshot.xp`), so undo takes a kill's XP and level back;
each level (`xpForLevel` grows with the cube of the level; `levelForXp` searches its exact whole-number costs) earns `TRAINING_PER_LEVEL` training points, spent on the ranks in `TRAINING`, each stat rank worth more than the one before on its row's curve, levels only earning points (Potion % is no stat: it adds to what a percent potion restores, `potionPercent`, read through `game.stepRules`; `save.training`; `trainingPoints`, `trainingStep` and `training.train`, which reaches a run still in the forest like a gear change; every row trains no further than its `max`: ATK, DEF, Regen and Max HP stop at 6,000 ranks, Shroud at 5,000, Potion % at 300, Gold / Floor, Silver / Floor, Silver Bonus and Gold / Kill at 150, Revive at 99, Lifesteal at 80, Find Potion at 72, Crit % at 80 and Crit x at 150, run training's ranks counted in; the Training tab shows the cap beside each row's level, *Lv 12 / 6,000*), shown on the Research page's Training tab.
The Training tab is open from the start.
A rank is bought one of two ways (`training-jobs.ts`), on separate schedules per row: `training.train` (`TrainingDesk`, `game/training-desk.ts`, the game's `training`) spends its points and counts it at once, and `training.trainWithGold(id, quantity)` trains a batch of as many ranks as Buy Quantity buys (`TrainingDesk.batch`: `bulkBuy` over `trainingGold` for each rank, Max as many as the Gold pays for): the batch's base time is each rank's `trainingMs` summed, taken off by credit and bank first (`batchWork`), then divided by `batchSpeedup(count)` in `buy-quantity.ts` (×2 from 5 ranks, ×3 from 10, ×5 from 25, ×10 from 100, multiplying with the boost, Faster Trainers and the Coin Pack rate);
the row's timer shows *base ×2x → reduced* (`nextWork`), in light green once credit or bank is banked toward it, and the Training backdrop (`training-particles.ts`, a `TIERS` table by speedup) shifts hue and stream rate with each active row's speedup, x3 the original look;
a stop or reset returns only the time worked (`jobInvested`, never the speedup's worth) (`training.trainWithGold` refused, like `buyTrainer`, until the Inspiration skill **Trainers** is owned, `training.hired`, or in Dev mode; until then the tab shows no trainer, timer, boost or auto-continue) pays a trainer `trainingGold` (20 Gold a point times the rank's number, times 1 + ranks / `growth` on the row's `TRAINER_GOLD_CURVES` entry in `config.ts`: 10 for the rows starting at 20 Silver in a run, 20 at 10, 40 at 5, 60 for max HP at 3, so the dearer a row in Silver the steeper its trainers, plus a `cubic` scale on each row, +(ranks / cubic)³ to that multiplier: 800 for Max HP, 700 for ATK, DEF, Regen and Shroud, 60 for Potion %, 25 Find Potion, 30 Revive, 45 for the Gold and Silver rows, near nothing early and about 4–5× the linear price at the cap) and starts a `TrainingJob` (`save.trainingJobs`: row, start and completion time, the Gold paid, its whole base time `ms`, and the batch's `ranks`, `speed` and `used`: the time credit and bank it took at the start) of `trainingMs` (`trainingSeconds(ranks)`, `ranks` being the row's ranks trainers finished, `save.trainerRanks`, which `trainingGold` reads too, so ranks bought with points never make a trainer dearer or slower, and a reset starts it over; the Archives' long-project schedule: 15 s, 1 min, 5 min, 10 min, then a quarter hour more each rank, divided by 1 + `trainingSpeed`, the Faster Trainers research), less the row's time credit (`save.trainingCredit`, by row, used up first) and then the time bank (`save.trainingBank`, shared by every row), at most `trainingSlots` at once (1, and one per trainer bought with Gems, `save.trainers`, `training.buyTrainer` at `TRAINER_GEMS`) and one per row.
A row's auto-continue box (`save.trainingAuto`, `training.setAutoContinue`) has its trainer start the next rank the moment one is done, when the Gold is there: `training.settle` completes due ranks earliest first and starts each auto-continued row's next from its completion time, so ranks passed while the game was closed chain on.
The training boost (`save.trainingBoostUntil`, `training.claimBoost`: an hour a claim, up to four banked, `claimBoost`) makes jobs go twice as fast: a job keeps its real completion time, `workLeft` turns it into the training still to do (what its timer shows, counting down twice as fast while boosted) and `doneAt` back, and a claim moves every job's completion to match.
`training.settle` (once a second from `main.ts`'s tick, and before each train or reset) counts the ranks whose time is up through `changeLoadout`, so a run inside gains them at once, records what they were paid with (`save.trainingPaid`: points, Gold and trainers' time per row, which `trainingPoints` sums for the points spent), and queues each in `training.done`, which `update` hands to the notification boxes (`research-toast.ts`) as "Training Complete!".
The Training tab lists each row a trainer is training under the trainer count, in the order they started, and only those rows show the auto-continue box, with a looping arrow (`redoIcon` in `ui/dom.ts`); a row goes back to its group once its training ends.
`training.cancel` gives the Gold back and the time spent as that row's credit (as does `training.train` buying a rank of a row in training, which stops its trainer; the Training tab raises both amounts over the Gold held and the row's time to its next rank, `riseFrom` in `ui/dom.ts`; each row shows that time, `training.toNextRank`: what is left of the rank in training, or the next rank's time less the row's credit), `training.finish` completes a job at once for `finishGems` (1 Gem per 10 minutes of training left, rounded up), a Gem reset (only from the forest: `training.canReset`, the `DeskHost`'s `run` outside) returns the row's points and Gold, and all the training time spent on it (its trainers' ranks, the rank in training it stops, and its time credit) into the time bank, and Dev free purchases train at once for nothing.
A level-up also sets `game.levelUpPoints`, the points it earned, which `drawLevelUp` raises over the hero as "+3" and the training points' arrow once the burst is over.
The tab draws `TrainingParticles` (`training-particles.ts`, a canvas behind the rows): golden square specks drifting softly, all fading to dark blue while a rank is in training, with a left-to-right stream of the same kind of fluid the skill trees use along each such row turning the specks near it brighter red.
`tests/training-jobs.test.ts` covers the jobs; other tests train through `tests/train-now.ts`.
A new level sets `game.levelUpAt`, which the renderer draws as the burst, glow and "LEVEL UP!" in `level-up.ts`.
`tests/loadout.test.ts` covers it.
The **Archives** (`src/archives.ts`, unlocked by the `archives` skill) are research that lasts between runs, all data: `RESEARCH` defines each project (categories, requirements on skills, other research or the hero's level, and its levels, each a Gold cost, hours and one effect), and an effect names a target in `RESEARCH_TARGETS` that the game reads through `researched(archives, target, base)` (every completed level's effect: adds, then multipliers, or the latest `set`), so a new project is a row there and a new kind of effect one target plus the line that reads it (Focus Count: `focusPerRun`, read by `Game.focusPerRun`; Movement Speed: `moveSpeed`, read by `Game.maxSpeed`; Rush: `rushTiles`, read by `Game.rushTiles`; Faster Trainers: `trainingSpeed`, read by `trainingSpeed` in `loadout.ts` when a trainer starts a rank; Research Speed: `researchSpeed`, read by `duration` as a level starts, and on each level completed `settleArchives` shortens every job under way (`hastenAll`), keeping its share done; Research Cost Discount: `researchCost`, in tenths of a percent, read by `price`, the Gold a level costs when it starts (definitions keep the undiscounted Gold and hours; long projects follow `docs/RESEARCH_CURVES.md`, built with `curve`); Pocket Money: `startingSilver`, read by `Game.dealHand` as a run goes inside, into `run.silver`; Undo Count: `undoCapacity`, read by `loadout`, which stores no undo at all until Rehearsed steps (whose grant is the first) or Echoes of time is owned, each opening four of its eight levels (the definition's `levelsPer`, read by `maxLevel`, which `nextLevel` and `status` take the upgrades for, and its `anyUpgrade` requirement), so the HUD hides the undo button while it stores none, and `keepUndos` trims histories, since `slice(-0)` would keep them whole; Regen: `regenPercent`, the percent of `player.regen` a step regains, read by `Game.stepRules`; ATK, DEF, Max HP and Shroud: `attackPercent`, `defensePercent`, `maxHpPercent` and `shroudPercent`, read by `statFactor` in `loadout.ts`, so `ResearchDesk.settle` completes research through `changeLoadout` and a run inside gains a level at once; Potion HP: `potionHeal`, read by `Game.stepRules`, which every `resolveStep` caller and Delve Automove's `DelveMind.rules` pass on; Heart Door Resilience: `heartToll`, the percent of its toll a Heart Door drains, read by `Game.stepRules` as `StepRules.heartToll`; Find Yellow Key: `yellowKeyChance`, in tenths of a percent, read by `Game.findYellowKey` on each floor climbed for the first time in a run (`advanceTowerRoom`, each new equivalent floor in `payDelveFloors`), its roll a fixed `tileRandom` number per run seed and floor; Key Efficiency: `keyCost`, in tenths of a percent of a key, read by `Game.stepRules` as `StepRules.keyCost`, which `resolveStep` multiplies with a badge's scale for each key a door takes, and the door cards (`WANTS` in `cards.ts`, and Charge's doors) plan by through `HandAt.keyCost` (`Game.keyCost`); Interest %: `interestRate` (tenths of a percent) and Max Interest: `interestCap` (`set` by level), read by `RunPurse.interest` on the same new floors, after Wishing Well; Mug: `instakillGold`, read by `RunPurse.killGold` for a fight whose first strike won (`FightEnd.instakill`); Refocus, Ignore More and Target More: `refocusFloors`, `ignoreFloors` and `targetFloors`, and Ignore Count and Target Count: `ignorePerRun` and `targetPerRun`, read by `run-charges.ts`; Floor Skip Reward: `floorSkipGold`, read by `RunPurse.skippedFloorGold` when Skip climbs past a Tower floor, paying that percent of the floor's enemies' kill Gold and closed chests' average Gold, `averageGold` in `loot.ts`, and marking them looted).
Research runs on the wall clock: each archivist slot's job keeps `startedAt`/`completesAt` timestamps, `settleArchives(owner, now)` completes whatever is due (in order, starting the next level from the moment the last completed when the slot auto-continues and the Gold is there), stopping a job refunds it and keeps its share done in `progress`, Rush (`research.rush`, the busy archivist's Gem button) completes it at once for `research.rushGems`, one Gem per ten minutes left rounded up like finishing training (`finishGems`), and `duration` applies research speed without changing the definition.
Every function takes `now`; `Game` passes `game.clock()` (`Date.now` unless a test sets it) and the commands of its `research` desk (`ResearchDesk`, `game/research-desk.ts`: `start`, `cancel`, `rush`, `setAutoContinue`, `hire`, `settle`, Dev mode's `finishNow`) wrap them.
Each level completed also queues in `research.done`, which `update` in `main.ts` hands to `ResearchToasts` (`ui/research-toast.ts`, which also announces ranks trainers finish): one "Research Complete!" box at a time at the top of the screen, outside `#app` (so the UI golden never sees it) and in the browser's top layer, shown four seconds and then sliding up.
The Dev setting **free purchases** (`settings.freePurchases`, read as `game.free`) lets every purchase through for nothing, unlocking nothing itself: skills (`Game.buy`), Training (a rank bought free, with points or by a trainer, counts at once and records nothing paid, so a reset returns nothing for it), provisions, research and archivists (the `ArchivesOwner`'s settings: no Gold, and a level due at once, which `research.start` then settles) and Defend (the `Wallet`'s `free`).
`tests/free-purchases.test.ts` covers it and the notification queue.
The save keeps `save.archives` (`decodeArchives`).
`tests/archives.test.ts` covers it.
Skill trees are in `skill-trees.ts`; the Inspiration tree is one of `unlocks` (each skill bought once, no ranks shown; more comes from the panels it opens); the metal bars treasure chests hold (`rollTreasureLoot` in `loot.ts`, `save.materials`), which only the Defend page spends, are in `materials.ts`; a kill drops nothing but its Gold, Silver and, once Equipment is open, Equipment's upgrade materials and a boss's piece.
**Equipment** (`src/equipment/`, see `docs/EQUIPMENT.md`) opens by claiming Tower I's floor 60 Goal (the `equipment` `GoalUnlock`; `Game.claimGoal` rebuilds the forest at once), and the forest then holds the Blacksmith (`OutsideWorld`'s `blacksmith`, `BLACKSMITH` in `outside.ts`, drawn by `drawBlacksmith`; `Game.atBlacksmith`, which `BoardOverlay.tap` sends to the Equipment screen).
`catalog.ts` holds the static data (nine categories, their upgrade materials, the effect kinds and every item definition: a Standard piece and three Uniques per category), `balance.ts` every tunable number (rarity tiers from the Shop's `rarity.ts`, level caps, merge and salvage, the cost curve, boss and material drops, the Gem pull's price, rates and pity), `inventory.ts` the saved state (`save.equipment`: items by definition id, rarity and level with what leveling them cost, a loadout per mode, material balances, pity per category, the next id, the pull stream and the Auto-salvage toggle, `autoSalvage`, under which `keepDrop` dismantles a Common boss drop at once, and `EquipmentDesk.pull` each Common piece a Gem pull brings) and what changes it (`addItem`, `levelUp`, `merge`, `dismantle`, `equip`, `decodeEquipment`), `effects.ts` what a loadout does, worked out each time from what is worn (`wornEffects`, never written into the hero: intrinsic lines and slot effects alike), `acquire.ts` Gem pulls (on a saved stream like badges, seeded from `stream("equipment")`, which `withSavedStream` carries on), boss drops and material drops (`RunPurse.enemyLoot`, gated per kill by `lootedTiles`), `slot-effects.ts` the effect slots' data (`SLOT_EFFECTS` by family, `CATEGORY_FAMILIES`, `slotValue`) and `slots.ts` every change to a slot: each rarity opens one slot at its `slotLevel` (`openSlots`, `fillSlots` on add, level-up and decode), a free `firstRoll`, a paid `refine` that offers `EFFECT_CANDIDATES` beside the effect held and adds to the slot's Refinement (every `REFINE_GUARANTEE_EVERY`th guarantees a top-rarity candidate, every `REFINE_CHOICE_EVERY`th earns a Choice, `spendChoice`), `takeCandidate`, `keepCurrent` and `improve`;
an offer is saved with its slot, so reloading never rerolls, and merging keeps the target's slots.
Effects are read where they apply: `loadout`, `attackAgainst` and `defenseAgainst` in `combat.ts` (Boss ATK, and Piercing's share of enemy DEF ignored, `player.pierce` up to `PIERCE_CAP`), `StepRules.victoryHeal` (`healAfterVictory`), `Game.floorHeal`, `RunPurse` (Gold, Silver, materials, drops), `gainXp`, `dealHand` (starting Silver), `Game.moveRate` (movement speed, which the frame loop reads) and `Game.rushTiles`.
`EquipmentDesk` (`game/equipment-desk.ts`, the game's `equipment`) runs the commands, through `changeLoadout` when a hero's stats change.
`ui/equipment-panel.ts` draws the screen's views (how they work kept off them, behind the ? beside the Equipment heading, `EquipmentPanel.showHelp`), chosen by tabs along its bottom: List (the loadout's nine slots in a 3×3 grid shaped like the hero, `GRID`, its Tower/Delve switch at the top left, over the inventory's grid of square tiles, with filters, Select and salvage), Assemble (*3× → 1×*: the three pieces going in, numbered, the piece kept first, beside the result, chosen from the grid below) and Acquire (Gem pulls in a category, or of all types at `PULL_ALL_DISCOUNT` off, `pullGems`, each pull's category drawn evenly), each assemble or pull ending on a full-screen result (`showResult`);
an assemble first plays `ui/slag-merge.ts`'s melt (`playSlagMerge`: the pieces fall into pixels of glowing slag that flow together and rise into the new piece, on a canvas outside `#app`, drawn from `stream("effects")`, skipped by a press or Reduce motion), and `ui/equipment-icons.ts` draws the pixel icons (`categoryIcon` for filters and empty slots, `itemIcon` for a piece: each Unique and the Silver Band its own, other Standard pieces their category's; `iconPixels` hands the melt a piece's pixels).
`tests/equipment.test.ts` and `tests/equipment-slots.test.ts` cover it.
`docs/EQUIPMENT.md`, `docs/CRAFTING_AND_EQUIPMENT.md` (only its chest Gold and metal bars still apply) and `docs/PROGRESSION_AND_DIFFICULTY.md` are the design source of truth.
Tests (e.g. `tests/progression.test.ts`) assert numbers taken from them, so keep code and docs in agreement.
