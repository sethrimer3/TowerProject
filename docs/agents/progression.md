# Progression and economy

Enemy curves, the loadout, fractions, levels and Training (points and trainers), the Archives, free purchases, skill trees, and Equipment.

**Progression/economy.** Balancing, upgrade definitions, and currency rewards are in `src/config.ts`.
Every enemy's stats, in both modes, come from `src/enemies/curves.ts` (`enemyStats(mode, tier, step, strength, profile)`, a step being a Tower floor counted from 0 or a Delve depth): one **enemy curve** per mode and tier (`ENEMY_CURVES`, falling back to `DEFAULT_CURVE`) gives a normal, balanced enemy's HP and ATK as anchors (`[floor, value]`, growth curves: one rate a step between anchors, the last rate carrying on) and its DEF as a share of ATK (`defenseOfAttack`, a multiplier curve that holds its last value), and each mode's `strength` and `profile` tables multiply it (an elite reads the curve `ahead` ten floors in the Tower; a boss is a multiplier on its own floor's curve; a Greater Boss twice a boss, `GREATER_BOSS_OVER_BOSS`).
Rates come from `root` and `intPow` (`exact.ts`), so stats are the same in every engine; they are rounded to hundredths, and the tier's factor (`tierStats`) still applies on top.
Generation passes the tier in (the Tower's floor graph carries it as `tower`, the Delve's lab as `tier`), so a tier given its own curve generates from it; the Delve's region cache is keyed by tier.
Tower enemies' names come from the ten-floor zone rosters (`TOWER_ZONE_ENEMIES` in `src/enemies/scaling.ts`, `towerEnemyName`, one per profile; each tower starts the cycle one zone further along, `towerZoneIndex`), and every generated enemy carries its `profile`, the Delve's from `DELVE_ENEMY_NAMES`.
From the second tier, a Fast enemy's ATK is at least `fastCarry` (`src/enemies/fast.ts`), read from the tier below's floor-100 curve (Towers III to V set lower instead, `FAST_CARRY_AT`: 1,000, 2,000 and 4,000 rather than 3,500, 14,000 and 42,000, in the Delve's caves too).
Attack Range and Attack Speed are value rows opened by a Goal rather than a skill (the row's `goal`; `trainingOpen` in `loadout.ts` takes the save and checks both), read through `trainedAmount` with `game.trainingNow` at the end of each turn (volley.ts); their Silver and trainer Gold share one cubic curve each (`rangePrices`, `speedPrices` in `config.ts`).
ATK / m (Tower III's floor 50 Goal) is a third such row, read when the Archer shoots (its curve `{ per: 1002, square: -2 }` hundred-thousandths: 0.01 a tile, each rank 0.00004 less, 1.204 at its 200, unit "perMeter", shown as the multiplier a meter, ×1.010/m); its Silver and trainer Gold share `perTilePrices`, a `ratio` schedule with a `rise` (`risingPrice`: each rank 6.47% dearer than the last, that ratio rising 0.022% a rank, 50 for the first, 1 million for the 130th, 998 million for the 200th), which `TrainerCurve` takes as `{ start, ratio, rise }`.
Multishot Chance and Multishot Targets (Tower III's floor 70 Goal, `goal: "multishot"`) are read when the Archer shoots (Chance 0.5% a rank, 49.5% at 99; Targets 2 and 1 more a rank, 9 at 7, unit "count", shown bare).
A tile is one meter (CONTEXT.md's **Meter**): Attack Range's unit is "m".
A row's `time` (`TrainerTime`) sets its trainers' time when not the usual `trainingSeconds`: ATK / m and Multishot Chance `{ skip: 1 }` (1 min, 5 min, 10 min, then ten minutes more each), Multishot Targets `{ each: 3600 }` (n hours for the n-th).
Multishot Chance's Silver and trainer Gold share a `power` schedule of 1.5 (`halfPow` in `exact.ts`, one `Math.sqrt`: 60 × n × (1 + ((n − 1) / 12)^1.5), 1,163 for the 11th, 9,893 for the 32nd, 99,554 for the 85th), and Multishot Targets' a `prices` list (450, 2,000, 5,000, 12,500, 40,000, 120,000, 350,000), which `PriceSchedule` and `TrainerCurve` both take as `{ prices }`.
Rapid Fire Chance and Rapid Fire Duration (Tower III's floor 80 Goal, `goal: "rapidFire"`) are read when the Archer shoots (Chance 0.4% a rank, 34% at 85; Duration 100 hundredths of a turn and 5 more a rank, 5.95 turns at 99, unit "turns"; docs/agents/movement-and-combat.md).
These shot rows (`shotRow` in `loadout.ts`: the rows whose `goal` is Attack Range, Attack Speed, ATK / m, Multishot or Rapid Fire) train between runs whenever open, but inside a run Silver and Free Training reach them only while the hand holds the ARCHER card (`runTrainingOpen`, read by `runTrainingOffer`, Free Training's `candidates` and the run's Training bar).
Both share one `prices` list in Silver and a trainer's Gold (`rapidFirePrices`: 120 + 42m + 7.85m² + 0.085m³ for the n-th, m = n − 1, rounded up and worked in whole numbers: 1,002 for the 9th, 10,013 for the 30th, 100,199 for the 82nd).
`tests/enemy-curves.test.ts` covers the anchors, that every stat only rises, and that every enemy's DEF stays at most half its ATK.
What a character starts a run with (ATK, DEF, max HP, shroud, keys and undo capacity, the undos each run has to spend) is worked out only by `loadout(save)` in `src/loadout.ts`: the baseline, then each upgrade's `grants` from its row in `config.ts` and each stat's training (`trained`: what its ranks add up to on the row's own super-linear rank curve, `curve` in `TRAINING`, never on the hero's level; the next rank's worth `trainingWorth`), then the mode's worn Equipment (`wornEffects`: flat, then percent of the total), then the Archives' ATK, DEF, Max HP and Shroud research multiplying the whole stat (`statFactor`, which run training and the siphons apply to each rank too), every fraction kept (`loadout(save, mode)`: each mode's hero wears its own loadout; Equipment's starting keys sit apart in `startKeys`, which `startingHero` adds only as a run starts, so `changeLoadout` never hands them to a run inside).
**Fractions:** Gold, HP and the hero's and enemies' stats keep their fractions (a 2% bonus on 1 ATK strikes for 1.02); each sum or difference of them in `loadout`, `trained`, `combat.ts`, `step-effects.ts`, the potions and `Game` is snapped to a grid of millionths (`snap` in `exact.ts`), so the forecast (`predict`) and the fight played out (`bout`) agree to the last strike (`tests/fractions.test.ts`), the same in every engine.
The screen shows them through `whole.ts`: held amounts (Gold, the hero's stats) rounded down (`whole`; HP never 0 while standing, `wholeHp`), amounts gained or lost (strikes, heals, rewards) to the nearest whole (`wholeChange`), and enemy stats to two decimals when not whole, one from 100 and none from 1,000 (`enemyStat`).
Saves keep Gold and section HP with their fractions.
The Upgrades and Gear pages write those rows' descriptions from the same grants (`upgradeText`, `provisionText`), so a stat's number changes in its row and nowhere else.
Each run keeps the loadout it started with (`run.loadout`), and a gear change or training shifts a run inside and its player by exactly what the change did to `loadout(save)`, so ATK/DEF gathered from items are kept; the run's undo history and a fall's snapshot shift with it, so undo takes back steps, never what was bought.
**Hero stats** (`src/hero-stats.ts`) hold the one rule every mid-run change follows: a change moves the hero by what it changes the loadout (`loadoutShift` for a loadout before and after, `rankShift` in `loadout.ts`, since it is a loadout difference, for a stat row's ranks, a Silver rank's, a siphon's or the Training tab's next value, times the boons and the Max HP badge's multiplier (`heroFactor`, in `shiftHero`, on the player and `run.loadout`); the Max HP badge itself moves max HP by its multiplier's ratio (`boostMaxHp`); more max HP comes with the HP to fill it, however it came, and less leaves HP no higher than the max.
`tests/hero-stats.test.ts` covers it.
Provisions (`GOLD_SHOP` in `config.ts`, each with its own `description`) last for good but add nothing to the loadout: each one owned is a use every run carries.
`gear.buyProvision(id)` (`GearDesk`, `game/gear-desk.ts`, the game's `gear`) buys one at a time (no Buy Quantity) at `provisionPrice` (`schedulePrice`: 100 Gold, then a hundred times the last), and a run already inside has the new use at once, since what is left is always those owned less those spent.
The provisions pressed from the HUD (`USED_PROVISIONS`: `heal`, `edge`, `guard`, buttons `#heal-stat`, `#edge-stat`, `#guard-stat`) go through `Game.useProvision(id)`, which is no turn of its own and which undo never takes back: `provisionsLeft(id)` is `save.provisions[id]` less the run's `provisionsUsed` (in the forest, what the next run carries); the elixir fills HP to max HP on every copy of the run the timeline holds (`timeline.runs()`; `full` and unspent at or above max HP), recording the heal (`lastHeal`) so the HUD's bar fills; the Whetstone and Aegis charm (`PROVISION_BOOSTS`) add `PROVISION_BOOST_PERCENT` (20%) of ATK or DEF as it stands, a fixed `amount`, to every copy and record `run.boosts[stat] = { amount, turns: PROVISION_BOOST_TURNS }` (`active` and unspent while one goes on); `endTurn` counts the live run's boosts down (`tickBoosts`) and one run out takes its `amount` back off every copy.
`RunTimeline.undo` carries `provisionsUsed` and `boosts` onto the run it restores, as it does `undosUsed`, so a use stays spent and a boost keeps its turns left; the renderer draws `boost-glow.ts`'s pulsing glow round the hero for each boost (ATK red, DEF blue), and keeps drawing frames while one goes on.
The Gold Key provision uses itself: `Game.provisionKeys(floor)` gives a gold key for each owned on the floor a run goes inside on (`enterFromOutside`) and then on the first new floor (`advanceTowerRoom`, the Delve's `payDelveFloors`, by equivalent floor) `PROVISION_KEY_FLOORS` (100) or more past `run.keyFloor`, the last that gave them, so a floor Skip climbs past still counts; they come as a floor begins, past which undo never goes.
`tests/provisions.test.ts` covers them.
The Bomb provision: each one owned is a Bomb every run carries, `Game.bombsLeft` (`save.provisions.bomb` less the run's `bombsUsed`, which undo rewinds with the run, since a throw is a turn; in the forest, what the next run carries).
`Game.arm("bomb")` readies one from the HUD's `#bomb-stat` (shown once one is owned), and the board tap's `useArmed` goes to `bombTile`: a tap on a tile with no enemy (`foeOn`, a Flying one included) is `invalid` and keeps it armed; otherwise, after any fight still playing out (`fights.finish`), the throw is a turn of its own (an undo snapshot, as `trainInRun`), the enemy takes the hero's ATK with no DEF (`Board.wound`, as an arrow's), or, felled, is cleared and paid as an arrow's kill is (`Finds.kill`), and `game.bombed` has the renderer play `explosion.ts`'s explosion on the tile; `tests/bomb.test.ts` covers it.
A run still in the forest hasn't started: after every purchase, unlock, training, gear change or research completed, `readyForestRuns` (`game/hero-sync.ts`) gives it (in both modes) the hero and loadout everything owned now makes.
Levels come from lifetime XP (`levelForXp`, `xpForLevel`), paid per kill by the enemy's strength and the equivalent floor (`xpForKill`, `XP_MULTIPLIER`), so both modes pay alike and part of each undo snapshot (`MoveSnapshot.xp`), so undo takes a kill's XP and level back;
each level (`xpForLevel` grows with the cube of the level, each band of `XP_BAND` (25) levels costing more than the band below: 25% more past 25, 50% more again past 50, then 75%, 100%, and 25% each band after, compounded (×1.25, ×1.875, ×3.28, ×6.56, ×8.2, ×10.25, …); `levelForXp` searches its exact whole-number costs) earns `TRAINING_PER_LEVEL` training points, spent on the ranks in `TRAINING`, each stat rank worth more than the one before on its row's curve, levels only earning points (Potion % is no stat: it adds to what a percent potion restores, `trainedAmount(…, "potion")`, read through `game.stepRules`; `save.training`; `trainingPoints`, `trainingStep` and `training.train`, which reaches a run still in the forest like a gear change; every row trains no further than its cap, `trainingCap` in `config.ts`: its `max`, and `LIMIT_BREAK_RANKS` (500) more for each of its first `breaks` Limit Breaks owned (`LIMIT_BREAKS`, the Inspiration skills `limitBreak1` to `limitBreak10`, a chain under Torch at 50 Inspiration each); ATK, DEF, Regen and Max HP stop at 1,000 ranks, 6,000 with all ten, Shroud at 1,000, 5,000 with the first eight (a save keeps ranks up to `trainingCeiling`, the cap with every Limit Break, a row past its cap showing as maxed), Potion % at 300, Gold / Floor, Silver / Floor, Silver Bonus and Gold Bonus at 150, Revive and Thorn Damage at 99, Parry % at 100, Lifesteal at 80, Find Potion at 72, Overheal at 500, Crit % at 80, Crit x at 150, Attack Range at 80, Attack Speed at 100, ATK / m at 200, Multishot Chance at 99, Multishot Targets at 7, Rapid Fire Chance at 85, Rapid Fire Duration at 99 and the Free Upgrade rows at 100, run training's ranks counted in; the Training tab shows the cap beside each row's level, *Lv 12 / 1,000*), shown on the Research page's Training tab.
Each Training row is one `TRAINING` entry in `config.ts` (typed by `TrainingDef`): its name, group, unlock (`requires`, `goal`), point `cost`, `max` and `breaks`, its rank `curve`, the `research` that multiplies its value, its Silver schedule in a run (`silver`, read by `silverPrice`) and its trainers' Gold curve (`gold`, read by `trainingGold`).
A stat row names its `stat`; any other is a value row, whose amount is its `base` plus its curve, up to its `cap`, in its own units, `scale` of them to one `unit` shown (`amountAt`).
The game reads a value row through one function, `trainedAmount(save, id, ranks?)` in `config.ts` (the crits through `critRule`, Silver Bonus through `silverFound`): `off` (0 unless given: ×1 for Crit x and the two Bonus rows) until its `needs` upgrade is owned (its `requires` unless given; Find Potion's base needs Recovery), its ranks counting only once its `requires` is.
What a rank adds (`trainingStep`'s `worth`, `rowWorth`) is a stat row's `trainingWorth`, or the difference of a value row's amount, in the units it shows.
The tab's values and the run's cards read one function, `rowValue(save, id, extra, view)` in `loadout.ts` (`trainingStep`'s now and next, and `runTrainingValue`), on a `TrainingView`: the mode whose equipment counts, and the run inside, if any (`Game.trainingView`).
A stat row is `loadout(save, mode)` (equipment, provisions, research), or inside a run the run's hero's stat (what it found, its boons, the Max HP badge) plus what `extra` ranks change the loadout from the ranks it has (the run's own too, `rankShift`) times `boonFactor` and `badgeFactor`, as buying them would move it; any other row is its amount at its ranks (the run's Silver ranks too) times `rowResearchFactor`, its `research` (Regen's `regenPercent`, the crits, Gold and Silver / Floor, Gold and Silver Bonus), Silver Bonus also times the Silver boons and equipment's Silver found, Silver / Floor times that and its boon, and the Free Up rows plus the Free Up Chance boon.
No row counts a Gold multiplier (`goldMultipliers`: the tower's, the Shop's, the ad's, themes, equipment's Gold found, shrine Gold), which shows only in the HUD's total.
A card at its most turns gold (`.drill-card.maxed`).
The Training tab's row in training (`.training-row.active`) puts its auto-continue box, name and levels on their own line (`.training-head`), shows its batch's speedup large at the left of the values and no time note or reset; its Gem finish (`.training-finish`) takes the reset's column, after the points, and its countdown widens to keep its time on one line.
Free Training (`free-training.ts`): the Free Offense, Defense and Utility Up rows (value rows, 50 hundredths of a percent a rank, read by `trainedAmount` through `FREE_TRAINING_ROWS` in `loadout.ts`) roll on each floor passed, from `Game.freeTrain`, called by `advanceTowerRoom` (once per floor new to the run, Skip's included) and `payDelveFloors` (once per new equivalent floor).
`rollFreeTraining` rolls the groups in order, each on its own stream (`drawAt` from `freeTrainingSeed`: the salted run seed, +1 for Defense, +2 for Utility; draws counted in `run.freeRolls`, so undo and a reload carry on from them), drawing nothing while a group's chance is nothing, and on a hit drawing again to pick an open row of the group below its most (the Free rows themselves included).
`Game.freeTrain` adds each pick to `run.training` as a Silver rank (`applyRunTraining`, no Silver spent), so `silverPrice` counts it, and sets `game.freeTrained`, which `BoardOverlay.showFreeTraining` shows for 2 s.
Their Silver (`silver`, a `cubic` schedule) and trainer Gold (`gold`, `start` 75) both price by `curvePrice` in `config.ts`, so the two match rank for rank.
The Training tab is open from the start.
A rank is bought one of two ways (`training-jobs.ts`), on separate schedules per row: `training.train` (`TrainingDesk`, `game/training-desk.ts`, the game's `training`) spends its points and counts it at once, and `training.trainWithGold(id, quantity)` trains a batch of as many ranks as Buy Quantity buys (`TrainingDesk.batch`: `bulkBuy` over `trainingGold` for each rank, Max as many as the Gold pays for): the batch's base time is each rank's `trainingMs` summed, taken off by credit and bank first (`batchWork`), then divided by `batchSpeedup(count)` in `buy-quantity.ts` (×2 from 5 ranks, ×3 from 10, ×5 from 25, ×10 from 100, multiplying with the boost, Faster Trainers and the Coin Pack rate);
the row's timer shows *base ×2x → reduced* (`nextWork`), in light green once credit or bank is banked toward it, and the Training backdrop (`training-particles.ts`, a `TIERS` table by speedup) shifts hue and stream rate with each active row's speedup, x3 the original look;
a stop or reset returns only the time worked (`jobInvested`, never the speedup's worth) (`training.trainWithGold` refused, like `buyTrainer`, until the Inspiration skill **Trainers** is owned, `training.hired`, or in Dev mode; until then the tab shows no trainer, timer, boost or auto-continue) pays a trainer `trainingGold` (20 Gold a point times the rank's number, times 1 + ranks / `growth` on the row's `gold` curve in `config.ts`: 10 for the rows starting at 20 Silver in a run, 20 at 10, 40 at 5, 60 for max HP at 3, so the dearer a row in Silver the steeper its trainers, plus a `cubic` scale on each row, +(ranks / cubic)³ to that multiplier: 800 for Max HP, 700 for ATK, DEF, Regen and Shroud, 60 for Potion %, 25 Find Potion, 45 for the Gold and Silver rows, near nothing early and about 4–5× the linear price at the cap; Revive's trainers ask what its Silver does, 100 × n × (1 + ((n − 1) / 6.4)³) for the n-th rank) and starts a `TrainingJob` (`save.trainingJobs`: row, start and completion time, the Gold paid, its whole base time `ms`, and the batch's `ranks`, `speed` and `used`: the time credit and bank it took at the start) of `trainingMs` (`trainingSeconds(ranks)`, `ranks` being the row's ranks trainers finished, `save.trainerRanks`, which `trainingGold` reads too, so ranks bought with points never make a trainer dearer or slower, and a reset starts it over; 15 s, 1 min, 5 min, 10 min, then ten minutes more each rank unless the row's `time` says otherwise, `TRAINING_STEP_SECONDS`, set so a stat's 6,000 ranks take about 17 months with every speedup, divided by 1 + `trainingSpeed`, the Faster Trainers research), less the row's time credit (`save.trainingCredit`, by row, used up first) and then the time bank (`save.trainingBank`, shared by every row), at most `trainingSlots` at once (1, and one per trainer bought with Gems, `save.trainers`, `training.buyTrainer` at `TRAINER_GEMS`) and one per row.
A row's auto-continue box (`save.trainingAuto`, `training.setAutoContinue`) has its trainer start the next rank the moment one is done, when the Gold is there: `training.settle` completes due ranks earliest first and starts each auto-continued row's next from its completion time, so ranks passed while the game was closed chain on.
The training boost (`save.trainingBoostUntil`, `training.claimBoost`: an hour a claim, up to four banked, `claimBoost`) makes jobs go twice as fast: a job keeps its real completion time, `workLeft` turns it into the training still to do (what its timer shows, counting down twice as fast while boosted) and `doneAt` back, and a claim moves every job's completion to match.
`training.train` leaves a row's job alone: points buy up to the row's most counting only the ranks it has (`trainingFull`, `trainingBulk`'s room; `trainingMaxed`, which counts the ranks in training too, gates only the trainers), and when the ranks bought leave the job's batch no room, `trimJob` drops its last ranks: their `trainingGold` back, their share of its base `ms` (by `trainingSeconds`) off, credit or bank it used past the new `ms` back as the row's credit, the work already done kept and `completesAt` set by `doneAt` for what is left (the batch's `speed` unchanged); a job left with no rank is cancelled.
`training.settle` (once a second from `main.ts`'s tick, and before each train or reset) counts the ranks whose time is up through `changeLoadout`, so a run inside gains them at once, records what they were paid with (`save.trainingPaid`: points, Gold and trainers' time per row, which `trainingPoints` sums for the points spent), and queues each in `training.done`, which `update` hands to the notification boxes (`research-toast.ts`) as "Training Complete!".
The Training tab lists each row a trainer is training under the trainer count, in the order they started, and only those rows show the auto-continue box, with a looping arrow (`redoIcon` in `ui/dom.ts`); a row goes back to its group once its training ends.
`training.cancel` gives the Gold back and the time spent as that row's credit (the Training tab raises both amounts over the Gold held and the row's time to its next rank, `riseFrom` in `ui/dom.ts`; each row shows that time, `training.toNextRank`: what is left of the rank in training, or the next rank's time less the row's credit), `training.finish` completes a job at once for `training.finishGems` (`finishGems`: 1 Gem per 10 minutes of training left, rounded up, then divided by 1 + 1.5% for each open row at its most, `trainingRowsFull`, `RUSH_EFFICIENCY_TENTHS` in `training-jobs.ts`, and rounded up again; a reset or Limit Break can lower it), a Gem reset (only from the forest: `training.canReset`, the `DeskHost`'s `run` outside) takes back one way's ranks of the row, the other's staying (`training.reset(id, way)`, the counts from `training.resettable`): `"points"` the ranks bought with points (`training` less `trainerRanks`), returning the points to the pool, or `"gold"` the ranks trainers finished, stopping the rank in training and returning their Gold and all the training time spent on the row (its trainers' ranks, the rank in training, and its time credit) into the time bank, and Dev free purchases train at once for nothing.
A level-up also sets `game.levelUpPoints`, the points it earned, which `drawLevelUp` raises over the hero as "+3" and the training points' arrow once the burst is over.
The tab draws `TrainingParticles` (`training-particles.ts`, a canvas behind the rows, its velocity read as the skill trees' is, `sampleField` in `tree-particles.ts`): golden square specks drifting softly, all fading to dark blue while a rank is in training, with a left-to-right stream of the same kind of fluid the skill trees use along each such row turning the specks near it brighter red.
`tests/training-jobs.test.ts` covers the jobs; other tests train through `tests/train-now.ts`.
A new level sets `game.levelUpAt`, which the renderer draws as the burst, glow and "LEVEL UP!" in `level-up.ts`.
`tests/loadout.test.ts` covers it.
The **Archives** (`src/archives.ts`, unlocked by the `archives` skill) are research that lasts between runs, all data: `RESEARCH` defines each project (categories, requirements on skills, other research or the hero's level, and its levels, each a Gold cost, hours and one effect), and an effect names a target in `RESEARCH_TARGETS` that the game reads through `researched(archives, target)` (the target's declared `base` changed by every completed level's effect: adds, then multipliers, or the latest `set`), so a new project is a row there and a new kind of effect one target plus the line that reads it (Focus Count: `focusPerRun`, read by `Game.focusPerRun`; Movement Speed: `moveSpeed`, read by `Game.maxSpeed`; Dash: `dashTiles`, read by `Game.dashTiles`; Faster Trainers: `trainingSpeed`, read by `trainingSpeed` in `loadout.ts` when a trainer starts a rank; Research Speed: `researchSpeed`, read by `duration` as a level starts, and on each level completed `settleArchives` shortens every job under way (`hastenAll`), keeping its share done; Research Cost Discount: `researchCost`, in tenths of a percent, read by `price`, the Gold a level costs when it starts (definitions keep the undiscounted Gold and hours; Parry % (opened by Tower V's floor 30 Goal, with the Parry skill): `parryPercent`, in tenths of a percent, added to the trained Parry % by `loadout` (capped at `PARRY_CAP`), so it reaches a run inside through `changeLoadout` like ATK; Floor Skip Reward: `floorSkipGold`, read by `RunPurse.skippedFloorGold` when Skip climbs past a Tower floor, paying that percent of the floor's enemies' kill Gold and closed chests' average Gold, `averageGold` in `loot.ts`, and marking them looted).
Research runs on the wall clock: each archivist slot's job keeps `startedAt`/`completesAt` timestamps, `settleArchives(owner, now)` completes whatever is due (in order, starting the next level from the moment the last completed when the slot auto-continues and the Gold is there), stopping a job refunds it and keeps its share done in `progress`, Rush (`research.rush`, the busy archivist's Gem button) completes it at once for `research.rushGems`, one Gem per ten minutes left rounded up like finishing training (`finishGems`), divided by 1 + 1.5% for each project completed (`researchCompleted`: `status` "completed", the gold box) and rounded up again, and `duration` applies research speed without changing the definition.
**Speed Ups** (`src/speed-ups.ts`) spend Cells (`save.cells`, kept with fractions, which a Greater Boss's kill pays through `RunPurse.enemyLoot`: 1 in Tower I, else 1 to the tier evenly from the purse's `rng`, times the fight's badge `scale`) to make one archivist research faster: `ArchivistSlot.speedUp` holds the `factor` (`SPEED_UP_FACTORS`), `hours` (`SPEED_UP_HOURS`), `endsAt`, whether it is `queued` and `appliedUntil`, how far its boost has been carried into the job (`carrySpeedUp`: both job times move back by (factor − 1) × the time boosted, so `jobProgress`, a switch's kept share, Rush's Gems and the countdown all read research done and research left, unchanged); while the archivist is idle the carry moves `endsAt` on instead, so a Speed Up is paused (the idle row shows it, `.archivist-speed.paused`) and only time spent researching uses it up.
A queued Speed Up may hold `next`, another factor and hours chosen meanwhile (`queueNextSpeedUp`, through `research.queueNextSpeedUp`, from the dialog's Next Speed Up while Queue is ticked; nothing paid then): `endSpeedUp` buys `next` in its place, or the same again; unqueuing (`research.setSpeedUpQueued`) drops it, and `decodeSpeedUp` keeps it only on a queued one.
`settleArchives` is an event loop (`nextEvent`): the earliest job completion (`completionAt`, its boost counted) or busy archivist's Speed Up end due by `now`, every slot's boost carried to it first; an end buys a queued Speed Up again from that moment when the archivist is busy and the Cells are there (`endSpeedUp`), and the boost carries on into an auto-continued level.
`jobAt(slot, now)` is a job's reading between settles, for the page.
Prices are `CELLS_PER_HOUR` × hours (`speedUpCells`); `research.speedUp` (settling first, refused while one is in progress or the archivist is idle), `research.setSpeedUpQueued` and `research.speedUpCells` (none with free purchases) are the desk's commands, and `cancel` and `dash` settle first too.
The Archives tab shows the Cells beside History (`.research-corner`), and each busy archivist's time left, its Speed Up in green and the Speed Up button opening `ArchivesPanel.showSpeedUp`.
The time left reads to the second (`formatCountdown` in `ui/archives-page.ts`: every unit from the largest down to seconds), and `ResearchPage.frame` moves it on each frame the moment a countdown shows another second (`nextCountdownIn`: the real time until then, its research time shared by a running Speed Up's factor), besides the frame loop's once-a-second tick, which still redraws the tab on a completion or a Speed Up begun or ended; `tests/speed-ups.test.ts` covers both.
The Training tab's countdowns read to the second too (`formatCountdown`), and `ResearchPage.frame` moves them on the same way (`nextTrainingCountdownIn`: the training time to the next shown second, `toNextSecond`, shared by the Shop's `trainingRate` and the boost's ×2 while it lasts; `tests/training-jobs.test.ts`); either tab's countdowns move at most every `COUNTDOWN_MIN_MS` (100 ms), so above ten seconds a second a move shows more than one gone.
The Cells, the Speed Up buttons and the help's Speed Up line show only once the player profile has earned any Cells (`save.profile.earned.cells`, `ArchivesPanel.cellsShown`); a Speed Up in progress shows regardless.
Research tallies the player profile (`tally` in `profile.ts`): `begin` adds the Gold paid to `researchGold`, `cancelResearch` takes the refund off, and `settleArchives` counts each level completed in `researchLevels`; `ArchivesOwner.profile` is optional, so a bare owner in a test tallies nothing.
The `TrainingDesk` likewise counts each rank gained (points, trainers, free) in `trainingLevels` (never lowered by a reset) and the trainers' Gold in `trainingGold`, less a stopped rank's and a Gold reset's refund.
`tests/speed-ups.test.ts` covers them.
Every function takes `now`; `Game` passes `game.clock()` (`Date.now` unless a test sets it) and the commands of its `research` desk (`ResearchDesk`, `game/research-desk.ts`: `start`, `cancel`, `rush`, `setAutoContinue`, `hire`, `settle`, Dev mode's `finishNow`) wrap them.
Each level completed also queues in `research.done`, which `update` in `main.ts` hands to `ResearchToasts` (`ui/research-toast.ts`, which also announces ranks trainers finish): one "Research Complete!" box at a time at the top of the screen, outside `#app` (so the UI golden never sees it) and in the browser's top layer, shown four seconds and then sliding up.
The Dev setting **free purchases** (`settings.freePurchases`, read as `game.free`) lets every purchase through for nothing, unlocking nothing itself: skills (`Game.buy`), Training (a rank bought free, with points or by a trainer, counts at once and records nothing paid, so a reset returns nothing for it), provisions, research and archivists (the `ArchivesOwner`'s settings: no Gold, and a level due at once, which `research.start` then settles) and Defend (the `Wallet`'s `free`).
`tests/free-purchases.test.ts` covers it and the notification queue.
The save keeps `save.archives` (`decodeArchives`).
`tests/archives.test.ts` covers it.
Skill trees are in `skill-trees.ts`; the Inspiration tree is one of `unlocks` (each skill bought once, no ranks shown; more comes from the panels it opens); a treasure chest's Gold is `rollGold` in `loot.ts`; the metal bars the Defend page spends (`save.materials`) are in `materials.ts`, though nothing finds them any more; a kill drops nothing but its Gold, Silver and a Greater Boss's Cells.
Once Equipment is open, a treasure chest may be a **Supply Chest** or an **Equipment Chest**, fixed as its floor is generated: `Game.dealHand` fixes the run's `chestOdds` as it goes inside (the hand's `HandBonus.chest`, CHEST's `CardBonus.chest`, each raised by the worn `chestFind`, Supply and Equipment Chest chance; none before Equipment opens or without the card), the boards take them (`RoomWorld`'s `chestOdds`, the Delve `World`'s run), and their `tile` marks each treasure tile through `withChest` in `board.ts`: `chestKindAt` compares the tile's fixed number (`tileRandom` under the floor's seed, salted) with the Equipment Chest's chance, then both, and the floor left by an opened one keeps the mark (`Tile.chest`), so nothing about a chest's kind is saved or drawn from a stream.
`Finds.treasure` passes the tile's kind to `RunPurse.treasure`: a Supply Chest adds `rollSupplies` (`SUPPLY_CHEST_MATERIALS`: 5 in tower I and 2 more each tower after, by `run.tier`, raised by Material Find), an Equipment Chest `rollChestPiece` (a Standard piece at `EQUIPMENT_CHEST_RARITY`, kept or salvaged by `keepDrop`), both drawn from Equipment's stream, and `Finds.treasure` raises them with the chest's Gold.
`chest-marks.ts` draws each one's glow and stars over the board (`drawChestMarks`, among the renderer's overlays, above the darkness), `lighting-pass.ts` gives it a glow of its colour, and `ui/tile-info.ts` names it.
`tests/card-bonus.test.ts` covers them.
**Equipment** (`src/equipment/`, see `docs/EQUIPMENT.md`) opens by claiming Tower II's floor 60 Goal (the `equipment` `GoalUnlock`; `Game.claimGoal` rebuilds the forest at once), and the forest then holds the Blacksmith (`OutsideWorld`'s `blacksmith`, `BLACKSMITH` in `outside.ts`, drawn by `drawBlacksmith`; `Game.atBlacksmith`, which `BoardOverlay.tap` sends to the Equipment screen).
`catalog.ts` holds the static data (nine categories, their upgrade materials, the effect kinds and every item definition: a Standard piece and three Uniques per category), `balance.ts` every tunable number (rarity tiers from the Shop's `rarity.ts`, level caps, merge and salvage, the cost curve, the Supply and Equipment Chests' contents, the Gem pull's price, rates and pity), `inventory.ts` the saved state (`save.equipment`: items by definition id, rarity and level with what leveling them cost, a loadout per mode, material balances, pity per category, the next id, the pull stream and the Auto-salvage toggle, `autoSalvage`, under which `keepDrop` dismantles a Common chest piece at once, and `EquipmentDesk.pull` each Common piece a Gem pull brings) and what changes it (`addItem`, `levelUp`, `merge`, `dismantle`, `equip`, `decodeEquipment`), `effects.ts` what a loadout does, worked out each time from what is worn (`wornEffects`, never written into the hero: intrinsic lines and slot effects alike), `acquire.ts` Gem pulls (on a saved stream like badges, seeded from `stream("equipment")`, which `withSavedStream` carries on), and the Supply and Equipment Chests' finds (`rollSupplies`, `rollChestPiece`, gated per chest by `lootedTiles`), `slot-effects.ts` the effect slots' data (`SLOT_EFFECTS` by family, `CATEGORY_FAMILIES`, `slotValue`) and `slots.ts` every change to a slot: each rarity opens one slot at its `slotLevel` (`openSlots`, `fillSlots` on add, level-up and decode), a free `firstRoll`, a paid `refine` that offers `EFFECT_CANDIDATES` beside the effect held and adds to the slot's Refinement (every `REFINE_GUARANTEE_EVERY`th guarantees a top-rarity candidate, every `REFINE_CHOICE_EVERY`th earns a Choice, `spendChoice`), `takeCandidate`, `keepCurrent` and `improve`;
an offer is saved with its slot, so reloading never rerolls, and merging keeps the target's slots.
Effects are read where they apply: `loadout`, `attackAgainst` and `defenseAgainst` in `combat.ts` (Boss ATK, and Piercing's share of enemy DEF ignored, `player.pierce` up to `PIERCE_CAP`), `StepRules.victoryHeal` (`healAfterVictory`), `Game.floorHeal`, `RunPurse` (Gold, Silver, materials, drops), `gainXp`, `dealHand` (starting Silver), `Game.moveRate` (movement speed, which the frame loop reads) and `Game.dashTiles`.
`EquipmentDesk` (`game/equipment-desk.ts`, the game's `equipment`) runs the commands, through `changeLoadout` when a hero's stats change.
`ui/equipment-panel.ts` draws the screen's views (how they work kept off them, behind the ? beside the Equipment heading, `EquipmentPanel.showHelp`), chosen by tabs along its bottom: List (the loadout's nine slots in a 3×3 grid shaped like the hero, `GRID`, its Tower/Delve switch at the top left, over the inventory's grid of square tiles, with filters, Select and salvage), Assemble (*3× → 1×*: the three pieces going in, numbered, the piece kept first, beside the result, chosen from the grid below) and Acquire (Gem pulls in a category, or of all types at `PULL_ALL_DISCOUNT` off, `pullGems`, each pull's category drawn evenly), each assemble or pull ending on a full-screen result (`showResult`);
an assemble first plays `ui/slag-merge.ts`'s melt (`playSlagMerge`: the pieces fall into pixels of glowing slag that flow together and rise into the new piece, on a canvas outside `#app`, drawn from `stream("effects")`, skipped by a press or Reduce motion), and `ui/equipment-icons.ts` draws the pixel icons (`categoryIcon` for filters and empty slots, `itemIcon` for a piece: each Unique and the Silver Band its own, other Standard pieces their category's; `iconPixels` hands the melt and the board's gain popups a piece's pixels, `materialPixels` the popups a material's).
Common pieces never merge (their tier's `next` is null): only Uncommon and above assemble.
Auto-assemble, beside *Choose the piece to keep*, lists every merge the inventory makes at once (`autoMerges` in `inventory.ts`: none sharing a piece, worn pieces left out, a locked piece only ever kept; each keeps the best piece left and uses up the lowest-level copies), each checked by default; its Assemble makes those checked, one `EquipmentDesk.merge` each, without the melt, then shows them all on `showResult`.
Its ✕ or a press on the backdrop cancels.
