# Movement and combat

The step pipeline, `resolveStep`, fights (encounters, falls, Revive, crits, Lifesteal, Thorns, parries), kill pay, run training, Buy Quantity, and gains.

**Movement pipeline.** `input.ts` (tap routes, pointer-captured swipes, keyboard, optional d-pad) → `pathfinding.ts` (wrap-aware, approaches necessary locked doors, prefers an available detour over a door with no key held, and of two equally long routes takes the one stepping on fewer tiles that hold something, then the one whose doors eat cheaper keys; `takesSomething` in `board.ts`: a key, potion, shard or treasure chest, not an area reward chest, or a lit torch, `litTorches`) → `Game` step logic in `state.ts` (undo history, feedback, loot, death/revive, floor transitions).
What a step *does* to the player (fight damage via `combat.ts`, where the enemy's ATK rises after every round by `raisedAttack`, 1% and at least 1, and the hero's shroud, `player.shroud` from the loadout, takes each fight's first damage after DEF, whole again every fight; a hero without one carries no field, door key costs, pickup amounts, and the HP each step regains after all that, `regenerate`, from the hero's `player.regen`, the loadout's Regen training, passed as `StepRules.regen`: none in the forest, and `Game.move`'s `regen` false for the tiles a Rush crosses after its first, so a rushed turn regains once; the revive and retrained-fight paths add it to the HP they work out) is decided only by the pure `resolveStep()` in `step-effects.ts`, under the `StepRules` research sets (`game.stepRules`: Potion HP's `potionHeal` percent, read at the moment of the step, so undo never keeps a stale value, and Recovery's `percentPotion`).
How many of a run's potions are percent potions is fixed when it goes inside (`run.percentPotions`, the chance in hundredths of a percent from `percentPotionChance`: Recovery's 2%, raised by Find Potion training up to 20%): generation leaves every potion that may be one blue (the Tower's, and the Delve's corridor guards'), and both boards pass generated tiles through `withPotions` (`board.ts`), which makes one red when its tile's fixed `tileRandom` number falls under the run's chance, so a higher chance only ever adds percent potions.
`Game.move`, route previews, the inspect panel, and Delve Automove's planning all call it, so change pickup or door rules there and nowhere else.
`Game.move` rejects a blocked or declined step before any bookkeeping, then `enter` commits it (undo history, stats, the door or fight) and `land` applies the tile (torches, chests, pickups, room clears, floor transitions).
In a game that can settle them (`game.playsFights`, which only `main.ts` sets, since only the frame loop settles encounters; tests and tools therefore settle every fight at once and show none), each fight becomes `game.fight` (a `ShownFight`: the strikes the board shows, from where, from when, and the enemy's starting HP), which the board popups read for the damage numbers and the enemy's HP bar (`enemyBar` in `board-popups.ts`: shown from the hero's first strike, draining to each strike's HP, fading once the enemy falls, and kept while the hero lies fallen; never rising, so a summary round's thorns can't leave it short of the felling strike), until undo, a new run or a mode switch clears it.
The popups keep each earlier fight's bar too, until its strikes have landed and its bar has drained and faded, so a fight begun before the last one's bar is gone leaves that bar to finish on schedule.
While fights animate (`game.animatesFights`: always, until the Courage tree's **Instant Combat** shows the Animate fights setting, `settings.fightAnimation`, and it is turned off), a step into an enemy instead becomes an `Encounter` (`game.encounter`, the same object as `game.fight`): the fight's strikes, scheduled by the pure `bout()` in `combat.ts` (0.25 s a strike in the first round, 90% of that each later round, down to 0.05 s), play out while nothing counts and every step, route and Automove turn waits.
Otherwise the fight counts at once and shows in summary rounds (`summarize` in `combat.ts`: the hero's strikes as one, the enemy's as another; no number rises off an enemy that falls); only a fight with a revival waits as an encounter, each round after a revival starting once its skull and fire (`REVIVE_PAUSE_MS`) are over.
The frame loop settles it (`finishEncounter`, which runs the same `enter`/`land`) once its last strike is done, and refreshes the HUD's HP (`game.shownHp`) as strikes land, with the bar's red already at the HP the fight leaves (`game.fightEndHp`) and the HP still to go shown in purple just past it, shrinking strike by strike, any left when the fight settles shrinking away over a second (`renderVitals` in `ui/hud.ts`, which also fills the bar up over a second, through a blue strip that shrinks as the red fills it, after each potion picked up: `game.lastHeal`, which the board popups also raise as a blue `+N`, blue being kept for HP the hero gains, `HEAL_COLOR` in `damage-labels.ts`); undo, switching mode, leaving the board or ending the run settle it first.
A fight the hero loses leaves it fallen (`game.fallen`: a run inside at 0 HP) rather than ending the run: `fallIn` pauses the hand and keeps the run as it was before the fight in `slice.fall` (saved, with the enemy's name, so a reload brings the defeat dialog back), and no step, route or turn runs until the player answers.
`fallIn` also adds the moment to `game.struckDown`, which the renderer draws as a skull and crossbones in a pulsing red glow over the hero's head (`drawStruckDown` in `struck-down.ts`, for `DEFEAT_MS`, 2.5 s), and the defeat dialog waits for it (`Game.defeatShownUntil`, read by `RunEndDialog.check`, which looks again once it is over); undo clears it.
`undo` then spends one undo to restore it, and `acceptDefeat` ends the run through `finalizeRun`, the one path that ends a run (End Run's `finish` too): it pays out (the run's Gold, `slice.runGold`, is already banked in `save.gold` as it is found; the closing message and the run's end dialog say how much) and starts the next run in the forest, with no summary in between.
The **Revive** skill (Inspiration, below Evade) is no take-back: each enemy strike that would fell the hero rolls a chance (`reviveChance` in `loadout.ts`: 0.5%, and 0.5% more per Revive training rank, up to 50% at 99) to raise it at full HP instead, the fight going on from the next round with the enemy as it was.
`Game.move` plays a lethal fight out with `bout(…, revives)` (`combat.ts`) when the hero owns it, so the step's result and the fight played on the board agree; the roll is a fixed `tileRandom` number per run seed, Tower floor, tile and strike (`Game.revival`), so taking the fight back and fighting it again ends the same way, and more training only adds revivals.
Previews, the planners and `isLethal` still count such a fight as lost.
**Critical hits** (the Inspiration skill **Critical**, 2, where Larger Hand stood: Larger Hand is now beside the Archives, which require Critical) are the Offense Training rows Crit % (`critChance`, 1% a rank to 80%) and Crit x (`critFactor`, ×1.2 and 0.1 a rank to 150 ranks), with the Archives' `critChance` and `critFactor` research (`critChancePercent` and `critFactorPercent`, +2% of the trained value a level for 100 levels): `critRule(save)` in `loadout.ts` gives the `CritRule` (`chance` in percent, `factor`), read when a fight is taken, so research and run Silver ranks count at once (`Game.crit`, also `StepRules.crit`).
A strike's ATK is multiplied by `critMultiplier(factor, applications)` before DEF (`combat.ts`; chance of 100% or more lands `floor(chance/100)` applications for certain, the rest rolls for one more, applications adding: ATK × (1 + n × (factor − 1))); `bout`/`resume` take a `Crits` (the factor and each hero strike's applications by index, the `Strike` carrying `crit`), whose rolls `Game.critRolls` fixes per run seed, floor, tile and strike like `Game.revival` (the `CRIT_SALT` stream apart, no other stream drawn), so undo, refights and tournament replays agree and more Crit % only adds crits.
`resolveStep` and every planner count only the certain applications (`atWorst`; `StepRules.crit`), the worst case, so Automove and the hand never take a fight a missed crit would lose; `Game.fightStep` plays the real rolls, then `restate` makes the step end as the played fight does (also after `retrainFight`).
`crit-forecast.ts` (`forecast`) works out the odds of each fight length exactly (a normal approximation past 400 strikes) for the inspect panel's expected damage, 10–90% range, chance to survive and Risky verdict, and Damage Visual's labels show the same expected damage (`DamagePredictions.cost` takes the `CritRule`; the forecast never caps damage at the hero's HP: a fight that fells it counts its whole damage, so HP regained never moves a label); a critical number rises glowing and brighter (`board-popups.ts`).
Both use the forecast only once the Critical Prediction skill is owned (`save.upgrades.critPrediction`, read in `tile-info.ts` and `Renderer.drawDamageLabels`); without it they show the plain, crit-free prediction, though fights still roll crits.
Since a label is worked out on the frame it first shows, `DamagePredictions` passes `predict` and `forecast` `PLAYED_ROUNDS` (50): a longer fight plays its first 50 rounds and estimates the rest in closed form (`estimatedDamage` in `combat.ts`: the enemy's ATK rising 1 a round below 200, then ATK − 50 growing 1% a round; a Lifesteal fight by `lifestealEstimate`), within a few percent; the inspect panel, steps and planners always play every round (`tests/long-fight-estimate.test.ts`).
With no Crit % rank every path is exactly as before.
`tests/crits.test.ts` covers it.
**Lifesteal** (the Inspiration skill of that name, 10, under Into the depths, with Buy Quantity and Key Siphon below it; a Defense training row of 80 ranks, a stat row like Regen: `Loadout.lifesteal`, `Player.lifesteal` in percent, absent without it, carried by `startingHero` and shifted by `changeLoadout`; rank curve `per` 0.1005 and `square` −0.0005, so 0.1% for the first and 0.001% less each rank; its Silver schedule, `{ base, growth, power }`, is the trainer Gold's shape, 60 × n × (1 + ((n − 1) / 20.5)²)) restores that share of the damage each hero strike deals (the strike's whole damage, crits and overkill counted: a last strike heals by all it deals, however little HP the enemy had left) as HP before the enemy's reply, up to max HP.
`predict` plays the rounds when the hero has it (`lifestealEnding`; `CombatPrediction.hpAfter` is the exact ending, `damage` the net HP lost) and `resolveStep` ends the step on `hpAfter`, so it and `bout` end alike (`tests/lifesteal.test.ts` sweeps both); a hero strike that heals carries `Strike.heroHp`, which `heroHpDuring` and the summary rounds read, and `retrainFight` shifts with max HP bought.
The crit forecast takes a fight of `t` strikes to heal by `t` average hits (the worst hit plus the crit chance left over of one crit's step), at least the enemy's HP its thorns left and at most `t` critical hits, in equal parts over the strikes (the max HP cap left out).
A hero strike that heals also carries `Strike.healed`, which the popups raise as a blue `+N` over the hero (`BoardPopups.heal`, nothing when it rounds to 0); in summary rounds `roundStrikes` folds each round's heals into the damage the enemy's summary strike shows (the net loss), or, when the hero came out ahead, leaves one blue gain where the hero ends.
A fight Lifesteal heals the hero by on balance (`netDamage` in `combat.ts`, below 0; the crit forecast's `expected`, `p10` and `p90` likewise, capped at the HP missing) reads *N Healing* in the inspect panel and wears a blue `+N` as its Damage Visual label.
The label counts the heals past max HP (`DamagePredictions.cost` predicts with max HP uncapped), so it shows what a fight would heal at full HP too; the step itself still heals no further than max HP.
With no Lifesteal every path is exactly as before.

**Thorns** (the Inspiration skill of that name, 10, between Buy Quantity and Key Siphon; a Defense stat row of 99 ranks, `curve: { per: 1 }`, `Loadout.thorns`, `Player.thorns` in percent, absent without it) cost the enemy `thornDamage` (`combat.ts`: that percent of its HP as the fight starts, halved for a boss or Greater Boss, no DEF) after each of its strikes the hero lives through (a revived strike too, none after one that fells it).
`play`'s `enemyBlow` takes it off and records it on the enemy's `Strike` (`thorns`, and the enemy's HP after it, `thornHp`); a strike's thorns that fell the enemy end the fight, which the hero wins.
Thorns are certain, so `predict` counts them: with thorns it plays the rounds (`thornRounds`, or `lifestealEnding` with Lifesteal), never estimating (thorns end a fight within 200 rounds), and marks a fight its thorns end `thornKill` (the hero takes `turns` strikes, `replies`); `instakill` (a first strike that wins, no thorn kill) replaces `turns === 1` in kill pay, the inspect panel and Damage Visual's gray.
`resume` reads `thornHp`, `summarize` sums a round's thorns, and the crit forecast's fight lengths (`turnOdds`) end on either side's turn.

**Parries** (the Inspiration skill **Evade**, 10, between Regen Research and Revive; Defense stat rows Parry Chance, `parryChance`, 1% a rank, and Parry %, `parry`, 0.5% a rank, 100 ranks each, on `Player` in percent) cut a parried strike's ATK by Parry % (`parriedAttack`) before DEF and the shroud; the enemy's ATK rises from its own.
`parryCut` holds Parry % to `PARRY_CAP` (98), and `loadout` adds the Archives' Parry % research (`parryPercent`, in tenths of a percent) to the trained Parry % under the same cap; research completes through `changeLoadout`, so a run inside gains a level at once and undo keeps it.
`bout`/`resume` take a `Parries` (each enemy strike's roll by index), which `Game.parryRolls` fixes per run seed, floor, tile and strike (`PARRY_SALT`) like `Game.critRolls`, so undo and refights parry the same strikes and more Parry Chance only adds parries; none is rolled at 0%, without a cut, or at 100%, where `certainParry` parries every strike and `predict` (`damageTaken`'s and `lifestealEnding`'s `cut`, `parriedEstimate` past the played rounds) counts it.
Below 100% `resolveStep` and every planner count no parry (the worst case); `Game.fightStep` plays the rolls and `restate` ends the step as the fight played (also after `retrainFight`).
A parried `Strike` carries `parried`, and in an animated fight the hero gives way a little from it (`lunges`, `PARRY_GIVE`); summary rounds drop it, showing only the damage.
Tower II's floor 50 Goal, **Evade Prediction** (`countsParries` in `goals.ts`: claimed and Evade owned), passes `forecast` (`crit-forecast.ts`, which then takes no `CritRule` too) `parries`: each fight length's damage gets a mean and spread from the parry odds (`damageByStrikes`), and `parried` sums their normal curves for the chance to survive and the 10–90% range, `best` counting every strike parried; the inspect panel and Damage Visual's labels (`DamagePredictions.cost`, keyed by the hero's thorns and parries and the enemy's strength) use it.
`tests/thorns-parry.test.ts` covers both.
A **Fast** enemy (`enemy.fast`, `src/fast-enemies.ts`) strikes first: `bout` starts with `enemyNext`, and `predict` counts one more enemy strike (`CombatPrediction.fast`, read by `replies`), as do `thornRounds`, `lifestealEnding` (whose opening strike heals nothing) and the crit forecast's `turnOdds` (`opening`); `instakill` is never true against one, so Mug pays nothing more and Damage Visual never grays it (`DamagePredictions` keys by `fast`), and `RunPurse.killGold` pays `FAST_GOLD` more.
`tests/fast-enemies.test.ts` covers it.
**Ranged enemies and the Archer** (`src/ranged.ts`): from the third tier, `rangedCells` marks `enemy.ranged` on its own tile roll (`RANGED_SALT`, `rangedShare`: Fast's share less 1,000 thousandths), after `fastCells`, in both worlds; `RunPurse.killGold` pays `RANGED_GOLD` more.
A turn ends in `Game.take` (after `land`), or, for a rush, once `rushOn` stops (`holdTurn`): `endTurn` (nothing in the forest, nothing below the third tier unless the hero is an Archer, nothing on a turn that climbed to another Tower floor) counts `run.volleys.turn`, then `foesShoot` and, with `isArcher` (`goals.ts`), `loose`.
Every cooldown is whole charge units (`charged`, `ready`, `spent`, `held`), absent meaning ready: a Ranged enemy gains `FOE_GAIN` (500) a turn and a shot costs `foeShotCost(floor)` (5,000 less a unit a floor, at least 500), kept by tile in `run.volleys.foes` (dropped once its tile holds no Ranged enemy, or full again); the Archer gains its Attack Speed (`attackSpeed`, twentieths of a shot every 10 turns) and an arrow costs `ARROW_COST` (200), kept in `run.volleys.hero`. A shooter ready with nothing to shoot waits full, banking nothing.
Reach is Euclidean in hundredths of a tile (`within`, `enemiesWithin`: closest first, then row and column, wrapping in the Delve): the Archer's `attackRange` (300, +5 a rank), a Ranged enemy's `foeRange` (five sixths of it).
A shot (`shotDamage`) is the enemy's ATK, cut by Parry % when the per-turn roll parries it (`SHOT_SALT`), less DEF, less the whole shroud; it costs the area's mastery when it costs HP, and one that leaves no HP fells the hero through `fallIn` with the snapshot from before the turn (`turnBefore`), so one undo takes the step back. No Revive; planners and `resolveStep` don't see shots.
An arrow (`arrowDamage`) is one hero strike (boss ATK, times its `arrowBoost`, crit rolls by `ARROW_SALT`, Piercing, enemy DEF) at the closest enemy it can hurt (judged with the boost, before the crit roll, so a far enemy plain arrows can't hurt can become the target), healing by Lifesteal; it wounds the enemy (`Board.wound`, a `wound` on the generated tile in `run.changes`, which both worlds' `tile` takes off the enemy's HP after the boons' `foeTile`; `save.ts` accepts wounded enemies in changes) or clears its tile and pays the kill (`payKill`, shared with `winFight`; an instakill when the tile had no wound).
`arrowBoost` multiplies two factors: ATK / Tile (`attackPerTile`, hundred-thousandths of ×1 a tile, times `Math.sqrt(d2)`, the Euclidean distance `enemiesWithin` found, round the Delve's wrap) and Charged Shot (the `chargedShotPercent` research times `run.volleys.waited`, the turns the ready arrow held unfired, counted in `Game.loose` only once that research has a level and cleared when it fires); a crit multiplies on top.
A multishot (`Game.multishot`: a `tileRandom` of the hero's tile under Multishot Chance, by `MULTISHOT_SALT` and the turn) spends one arrow's charge on up to `multishotTargets` of the closest enemies the boost lets it hurt (fewer when fewer are in range); each arrow goes through `shoot` with its own boost for its distance, its own crit roll (by its target's tile), and the first's `waited` Charged Shot.
A charged arrow's `Shot` carries `charged`: `drawVolley` flies it in a blue glow (`CHARGED_GLOW`), and `BoardPopups` draws its number with a gold outline (`CHARGED_RING`) under the black one.
`Game.volley` (`Shot`s, flying for up to `FLIGHT_MS`) is what the board draws (`volley-art.ts`) and the popups land: a shot's damage over the hero, an arrow's over the enemy with its HP bar (`arrowBars`); undo, a new run or a mode switch clears it.
`tests/ranged.test.ts` covers it.
Each revival's strike first raises the same skull (`game.struckDown`, the hero's HP shown at 0 meanwhile, `heroHpDuring`), and `bout` holds the fight's next strike back `REVIVE_PAUSE_MS` (the skull's `DEFEAT_MS` and the fire's `REVIVE_MS`), as `summarize` does its next round (`Game.showRevivals` sets both lists).
Once the skull is over, `game.revivedAt` begins, which the renderer draws as a smaller golden version of the level-up burst under a white and golden beam of light that a few small white angel wings flutter down and fade from over a second (`drawRevive` in `level-up.ts`); `tests/revive.test.ts` covers it.
Each kill pays Gold (`ENEMY_GOLD`, into the save, gated by `lootedTiles`) and Silver (`silverForKill` by strength and equivalent floor, into `run.silver`, so undo takes it back).
A kill's Silver rises smoothly, fractions kept (`silverBase`: 2 on floor 1, 0.2 a floor to floor 201, `SILVER_SLOWDOWN_FLOOR`, then 0.1).
Silver buys **run training** (`run-training.ts`): Training ranks for the run alone, kept in `run.training` beside the hero's own (`ranksInRun`), each row's price from its `RUN_TRAINING_PRICES` schedule (`silverPrice`, by ranks bought this run) and its level capped at its `max`.
`Game.trainInRun` remembers an undo snapshot first (a purchase is a turn), then adds what a stat row's next rank adds on its curve (times its research, `statFactor`) to the player and `run.loadout` (max HP with the HP to fill it), or raises `run.percentPotions` (and the floor's `RoomWorld`); bought during an encounter (not one in summary rounds), `retrainFight` plays the fight on from its first strike not yet swinging with the hero's new stats (`resume` in `combat.ts`; max HP raising the HP shown so far) and updates the step's outcome the encounter carries, so the fight played and the one that counts agree; Potion % and Revive count through `game.trainingNow`, which `stepRules` and `revival` read.
Both are open only once the Inspiration skill **On the Job** is owned (`game.trainsOnTheJob`, or Dev mode).
**Buy Quantity** (`buy-quantity.ts`, the Inspiration skill of that name below Lifesteal, above Key Siphon) sets how many ranks one Training press buys: `Game.buyQuantities` is x1 once the skill is owned and one more per level of its research (`buyQuantity` target: x5, x10, x25, x100, Max), each screen keeping its own choice (the Training tab's `buyQuantity`, the run training bar's `runBuyQuantity`, the Gear page's `provisionBuyQuantity`; no Settings page rows), read through `Game.buyQuantityFor(scope)` and set with `setBuyQuantity(q, scope)` (a provisions press goes through `GearDesk.buyProvision(id, quantity)` and `provisionBulk`, each rank dearer);
`bulkBuy` works out a press's ranks and summed price (a fixed quantity all or none, Max as many as what is held pays for), for training points (`trainingBulk` in `loadout.ts`, `training.train(id, quantity)`) and run Silver (`runTrainingBulk`, `Game.trainInRun(id, quantity)`, one undo for them all);
`ui/buy-quantity-select.ts` draws the dropdown (only once there is more than x1 to choose) and the ranks a press buys: over a Max press's price on the Training tab (`maxCount`), and past x1 in the corner of each run training card's price (`runCount`), and `tests/buy-quantity.test.ts` covers it.
`ui/run-training-bar.ts` (`RunTrainingBar`) draws the bar of Training group buttons under the hand inside a run (and the Buy Quantity dropdown at its right), and the open group's cards in the hand's place; the first run after On the Job it teaches the bar (`save.tutorials.onTheJob`: a note over the hand and the tutorials' pointing hand, `POINTER_SVG` in `ui/dom.ts`, until a group is opened and closed again).
`tests/run-training.test.ts` covers it.

Rewards picked up (pickups, treasure, enemy drops, an opened treasure chest) and what a door takes (each key, drawn with a minus sign, or a Heart Door's heart with the HP it drained: a Heart Door always opens and leaves the hero 1 HP, `HEART_DOOR_HP` in `step-effects.ts`) are queued as `Gain`s (`game.gains`: a tile's contents, a material, the heart or a Gold or Silver coin to draw (Silver's baked once from the Gold coin, drained of colour, as the purse shows it), or text), which the renderer takes each frame; other feedback (floors, undo) is still the rising text over the board (`game.effect`).
`tests/fight-playback.test.ts` covers the schedule, an encounter against the same fight settled at once, undo and death mid-fight, gains, and the popups.
