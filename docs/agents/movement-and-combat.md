# Movement and combat

The step pipeline, `resolveStep`, fights (encounters, falls, Revive, crits, Lifesteal, Thorns, parries), kill pay, run training, Buy Quantity, and gains.

**Movement pipeline.** `input.ts` (tap routes; pointer-captured swipes and the keyboard, which step the hero only in Dev mode) → `pathfinding.ts` (wrap-aware, approaches necessary locked doors, prefers an available detour over a door with no key held, and of two equally long routes takes the one stepping on fewer tiles that hold something, then the one whose doors eat cheaper keys; `takesSomething` in `board.ts`: a key, potion, shard or treasure chest, not an area reward chest, or a lit torch, `litTorches`) → `Game` step logic in `state.ts` (undo history, feedback, loot, death/revive, floor transitions).
In a Tower forest, route planning treats each connected side-edge tile as an exit and reaches it only as the chosen destination, so an interior grass route cannot accidentally switch towers.
`Game.travelForest` is shared by side-arrow presses and steps onto an unlocked side edge, chooses a clear entry connected to the main path, cancels the source route and locks movement through `forestArrival`.
The transient arrival starts on the new screen's first frame: 100ms without the hero, then a 300ms walk from beyond the opposite edge to a clear inset tile (150ms with Fast, no translation with Instant or Reduce motion).
The saved hero already stands on that safe tile, so reloads never retain off-screen positions or a pending animation.
What a step *does* to the player (fight damage via `combat.ts`, where the enemy's ATK rises after every round by `raisedAttack`, 1% and at least 1, and the hero's shroud, `player.shroud` from the loadout, takes each fight's first damage after DEF, whole again every fight; a hero without one carries no field, door key costs, pickup amounts, and the HP each step regains after all that, `regenerate`, from the hero's `player.regen`, the loadout's Regen training, passed as `StepRules.regen`: none in the forest, and `Game.move`'s `regen` false for the tiles a Dash crosses after its first, so a dashed turn regains once; the revive and retrained-fight paths add it to the HP they work out) is decided only by the pure `resolveStep()` in `step-effects.ts`, under the `StepRules` research sets (`game.stepRules`: Potion HP's `potionHeal` percent, read at the moment of the step, so undo never keeps a stale value, and Recovery's `percentPotion`).
**Overheal** (the Breakthrough skill, between Physician and Life, and its Utility training row, value ×1 in thousandths, `curve: { per: 31 }`, 500 ranks) lets HP rise above max HP: `Game.overheal` reads the row (`trainedAmount(trainingNow, "overheal")`), `stepRules.overheal` carries it into `resolveStep` (absent at ×1), and `hpCeiling(maxHp, rules)` (`step-effects.ts`) is the most HP the hero may hold (`Game.hpCeiling`).
Potions (regular and percent), the smelts (Smelt GK, BK and RK) and the badges' heals (`card-activation.ts`, through `HandRules.hpCeiling`) heal up to it; every other heal stops at max HP through `healUpTo` (`combat.ts`), which never lowers HP already above its ceiling: Lifesteal in `predict`, `bout` and `lifestealEstimate`, Regen and the victory heal (already gated on `hp < maxHp`), equipment's floor heal, and a scale giving damage back (`afterExtra`).
Max HP moving mid-run keeps HP above it: `shiftHero` takes the Overheal (`changeLoadout`, run training and the siphons pass it) and caps HP at the new ceiling, and `boostMaxHp` and the Shrines' `rescaleHero` keep its share.
The save keeps HP up to the ceiling at Overheal's last rank (`validPlayer`), and Automove counts a potion worth taking while HP is under the ceiling (`SearchRules.ceiling`).
`tests/overheal.test.ts` covers the skill, the row, the heals and the save.
How many of a run's potions are percent potions is fixed when it goes inside (`run.percentPotions`, the chance in hundredths of a percent, Find Potion's `trainedAmount`: Recovery's 2%, raised by Find Potion training up to 20%): generation leaves every potion that may be one blue (the Tower's, and the Delve's corridor guards'), and both boards pass generated tiles through `withPotions` (`board.ts`), which makes one red when its tile's fixed `tileRandom` number falls under the run's chance, so a higher chance only ever adds percent potions.
`Game.move`, route previews, the inspect panel, and Delve Automove's planning all call it, so change pickup or door rules there and nowhere else.
`Game.move` rejects a blocked or declined step before any bookkeeping, then `enter` commits it (undo history, stats, the door or fight) and `land` applies the tile (torches, chests, pickups, room clears, floor transitions).
What a step finds is paid, raised and counted by `Finds` (`game/finds.ts`, the game's `finds`): a kill (`kill`), an item or chest taken (`pickup`), a door opened (`door`), Pickup Gold (`pickupGold`) and the coins a floor pays (`coins`), each through the purse (`RunPurse`, which gates Gold so undo can't pay it twice), as gains, toward the missions, the Event and the run's notes, and on the status line; `Game` decides when (`enter`, `land`, `winFight` once the hero is known to stand, the volley's kills).
A step into an enemy goes through **fight playback** (`FightPlayback`, `game/fight-playback.ts`, the game's `fights`) first: it rolls Revive and the crits, shows the fight, plays an Encounter out, and hands the step back to `Game.take` once the fight is settled, so the undo history and the payouts stay `Game`'s.
A torch put out (`breakTorchAt`, `breakTorch` in `torches.ts`) leaves its tile in `run.changes` (a `floor` edit, unless one is there already), and each board builds its torches lit only where the changes hold nothing (`torchesLeft`), so undo snapshots and the save carry them like any other tile edit.
In a game that can settle them (`fights.playsFights`, which only `main.ts` sets, since only the frame loop settles encounters; tests and tools therefore settle every fight at once and show none), each fight becomes `fights.shown` (a `ShownFight`: the strikes the board shows, from where, from when, and the enemy's starting HP), which the board popups read for the damage numbers and the enemy's HP bar (`enemyBar` in `render/board-popups.ts`: shown from the hero's first strike, draining to each strike's HP, fading once the enemy falls, and kept while the hero lies fallen; never rising, so a summary round's thorns can't leave it short of the felling strike), until undo, a new run or a mode switch clears it.
The popups keep each earlier fight's bar too, until its strikes have landed and its bar has drained and faded, so a fight begun before the last one's bar is gone leaves that bar to finish on schedule.
While fights animate (`game.animatesFights`: always, until the Breakthrough tree's **Instant Combat** shows the Animate fights setting, `settings.fightAnimation`, and it is turned off), a step into an enemy instead becomes an `Encounter` (`fights.encounter`, the same object as `fights.shown`): the fight's strikes, scheduled by the pure `bout()` in `combat.ts` (0.25 s a strike in the first round, 90% of that each later round, down to 0.05 s), play out while nothing counts and every step, route and Automove turn waits.
Otherwise the fight counts at once and shows in summary rounds (`summarize` in `combat.ts`: the hero's strikes as one, the enemy's as another; no number rises off an enemy that falls); only a fight with a revival waits as an encounter, each round after a revival starting once its skull and fire (`REVIVE_PAUSE_MS`) are over.
The frame loop settles it (`fights.finish`, which runs the same `enter`/`land`) once its last strike is done, and refreshes the HUD's HP (`fights.shownHp`) as strikes land, with the bar's red already at the HP the fight leaves (`fights.endHp`) and the HP still to go shown in purple just past it, shrinking strike by strike, any left when the fight settles shrinking away over a second (`renderVitals` in `ui/hud.ts`, which also fills the bar up over a second, through a blue strip that shrinks as the red fills it, after each potion picked up: `game.lastHeal`, which the board popups also raise as a blue `+N`, blue being kept for HP the hero gains, `HEAL_COLOR` in `render/effects/damage-labels.ts`); undo, switching mode, leaving the board or ending the run settle it first.
A fight the hero loses leaves it fallen (`game.fallen`: a run inside at 0 HP) rather than ending the run: `fallIn` pauses the hand and keeps the run as it was before the fight in `slice.fall` (saved, with the enemy's name, so a reload brings the defeat dialog back), and no step, route or turn runs until the player answers.
`fallIn` also adds the moment to `fights.struckDown` (`fights.strikeDown`), which the renderer draws as a skull and crossbones in a pulsing red glow over the hero's head (`drawStruckDown` in `render/effects/struck-down.ts`, for `DEFEAT_MS`, 2.5 s), and the defeat dialog waits for it (`fights.defeatShownUntil`, read by `RunEndDialog.check`, which looks again once it is over); undo clears it.
`undo` then spends one undo to restore it, and `acceptDefeat` ends the run through `finalizeRun`, the one path that ends a run (End Run's `finish` too): it pays out (the run's Gold, `slice.runGold`, is already banked in `save.gold` as it is found; the closing message and the run's end dialog say how much) and starts the next run in the forest, with no summary in between.
The **Revive** skill (Inspiration, below Parry) is no take-back: each enemy strike that would fell the hero rolls a chance (Revive's `trainedAmount` in `progression/training.ts`: 0.5%, and 0.5% more per Revive training rank, up to 50% at 99) to raise it at full HP instead, the fight going on from the next round with the enemy as it was.
`Game.move` plays a lethal fight out with `bout(…, revives)` (`combat.ts`) when the hero owns it, so the step's result and the fight played on the board agree; the roll is a fixed `tileRandom` number per run seed, Tower floor, tile and strike (`FightPlayback.revival`), so taking the fight back and fighting it again ends the same way, and more training only adds revivals.
Previews, the planners and `isLethal` still count such a fight as lost.
**Angel Wings** (Inspiration, under Revive; Revive and both Angel skills hidden until the Breakthrough skill **Life**) adds the ANGEL WINGS card (`PASSIVE_CARDS`, `WANTS` never): while either hand holds it and the run hasn't spent it (`run.wingsSpent`), `Game.wingsReady`, and `Game.wingsFor(enemy)` hands `bout` a `Wings` (`combat.ts`).
`enemyBlow` raises the hero at full HP from the first lethal strike Revive's roll didn't save, once a bout (`BoutState.winged`, which `resume` rebuilds from the strikes kept), marking the strike `revived` and `winged`; with Avenging Angel covering the enemy (`Wings.smites`), the strike is `smote` too, leaving the enemy at 0 (`thornHp: 0`), so the fight ends there.
`FightPlayback.fight` sets `step.winged`, and `Game.enter` spends the charge (`spendWings`) before `winFight`: `run.wingsSpent` goes in the run, so undoing the fight gives it back.
A shot that would fell the hero (`endTurn`) spends it the same way (`fights.raise`).
**Avenging Angel** (Inspiration, under Angel Wings) opens its research (`avengingAngel`, Defense, `BUY_QUANTITY_LEVELS` at ten times the Gold), read as `spendWings` runs: every enemy of `SMITE_RANKS` up to its level (`angel.ts`) that `Board.floorEnemies` lists (a Tower floor's every enemy; in the Delve, those of the hero's area on its equivalent floor, as `floorDoors` scopes Master Key) but the one being fought is cleared and paid through `Finds.kill`, and `Game.smitten` keeps each, enemy and all, for its slash and poof (`drawSmite`, `render/effects/smite.ts`).
`tests/angel-wings.test.ts` covers both.
**Critical hits** (the Inspiration skill **Critical**, 2, where Larger Hand stood: Breakthrough, then the Archives, follow it) are the Offense Training rows Crit % (`critChance`, 1% a rank to 80%) and Crit x (`critFactor`, ×1.2 and 0.1 a rank to 150 ranks), with the Archives' `critChance` and `critFactor` research (`critChancePercent` and `critFactorPercent`, +2% of the trained value a level for 100 levels): `critRule(save)` in `progression/loadout.ts` gives the `CritRule` (`chance` in percent, `factor`), read when a fight is taken, so research and run Silver ranks count at once (`Game.crit`, also `StepRules.crit`).
A strike's ATK is multiplied by `critMultiplier(factor, applications)` before DEF (`combat.ts`; chance of 100% or more lands `floor(chance/100)` applications for certain, the rest rolls for one more, applications adding: ATK × (1 + n × (factor − 1))); `bout`/`resume` take a `Crits` (the factor and each hero strike's applications by index, the `Strike` carrying `crit`), whose rolls `FightPlayback.critRolls` fixes per run seed, floor, tile and strike like `revival` (the `CRIT_SALT` stream apart, no other stream drawn), so undo, refights and tournament replays agree and more Crit % only adds crits.
`resolveStep` and every planner count only the certain applications (`atWorst`; `StepRules.crit`), the worst case, so Automove and the hand never take a fight a missed crit would lose; `fights.fight` plays the real rolls, then `restate` makes the step end as the played fight does (also after `fights.retrain`).
`crit-forecast.ts` (`forecast`) works out the odds of each fight length exactly (a normal approximation past 400 strikes) for the inspect panel's expected damage, 10–90% range, chance to survive and Risky verdict, and Damage Visual's labels show the same expected damage (`DamagePredictions.cost` takes the `CritRule`; the forecast never caps damage at the hero's HP: a fight that fells it counts its whole damage, so HP regained never moves a label); a critical number rises glowing and brighter (`render/board-popups.ts`).
Both use the forecast only once the Critical Prediction skill is owned (`save.upgrades.critPrediction`, read in `tile-info.ts` and `Renderer.drawDamageLabels`); without it they show the plain, crit-free prediction, though fights still roll crits.
Since a label is worked out on the frame it first shows, `DamagePredictions` passes `predict` and `forecast` `PLAYED_ROUNDS` (50): a longer fight plays its first 50 rounds and estimates the rest in closed form (`estimatedDamage` in `combat.ts`: the enemy's ATK rising 1 a round below 200, then ATK − 50 growing 1% a round; a Lifesteal fight by `lifestealEstimate`), within a few percent; the inspect panel, steps and planners always play every round (`tests/long-fight-estimate.test.ts`).
With no Crit % rank every path is exactly as before.
`tests/crits.test.ts` covers it.
**Lifesteal** (the Inspiration skill of that name, 10, under Breakthrough, with Buy Quantity and GK Siphon below it; a Defense training row of 80 ranks, a stat row like Regen: `Loadout.lifesteal`, `Player.lifesteal` in percent, absent without it, carried by `startingHero` and shifted by `changeLoadout`; rank curve `per` 0.1005 and `square` −0.0005, so 0.1% for the first and 0.001% less each rank; its Silver schedule, `{ base, growth, power }`, is the trainer Gold's shape, 60 × n × (1 + ((n − 1) / 20.5)²)) restores that share of the damage each hero strike deals (the strike's whole damage, crits and overkill counted: a last strike heals by all it deals, however little HP the enemy had left) as HP before the enemy's reply, up to max HP.
`predict` plays the rounds when the hero has it (`lifestealEnding`; `CombatPrediction.hpAfter` is the exact ending, `damage` the net HP lost) and `resolveStep` ends the step on `hpAfter`, so it and `bout` end alike (`tests/lifesteal.test.ts` sweeps both); a hero strike that heals carries `Strike.heroHp`, which `heroHpDuring` and the summary rounds read, and `fights.retrain` shifts with max HP bought.
The crit forecast takes a fight of `t` strikes to heal by `t` average hits (the worst hit plus the crit chance left over of one crit's step), at least the enemy's HP its thorns left and at most `t` critical hits, in equal parts over the strikes (the max HP cap left out).
A hero strike that heals also carries `Strike.healed`, which the popups raise as a blue `+N` over the hero (`BoardPopups.heal`, nothing when it rounds to 0); in summary rounds `roundStrikes` folds each round's heals into the damage the enemy's summary strike shows (the net loss), or, when the hero came out ahead, leaves one blue gain where the hero ends.
A fight Lifesteal heals the hero by on balance (`netDamage` in `combat.ts`, below 0; the crit forecast's `expected`, `p10` and `p90` likewise, capped at the HP missing) reads *N Healing* in the inspect panel and wears a blue `+N` as its Damage Visual label.
The label counts the heals past max HP (`DamagePredictions.cost` predicts with max HP uncapped), so it shows what a fight would heal at full HP too; the step itself still heals no further than max HP.
With no Lifesteal every path is exactly as before.

**Thorns** (the Inspiration skill of that name, 10, under Buy Quantity, above the three Mastery paths; a Defense stat row of 99 ranks, `curve: { per: 1 }`, `Loadout.thorns`, `Player.thorns` in percent, absent without it) cost the enemy `thornDamage` (`combat.ts`: that percent of its HP as the fight starts, or of its `fullHp` once arrows have wounded it, which `wounded` in `enemies/ranged.ts` sets as the board reads the tile and `DamagePredictions` keys by; halved for a boss or Greater Boss, no DEF) after each of its strikes the hero lives through (a revived strike too, none after one that fells it).
`play`'s `enemyBlow` takes it off and records it on the enemy's `Strike` (`thorns`, and the enemy's HP after it, `thornHp`); a strike's thorns that fell the enemy end the fight, which the hero wins.
A Ranged or Flying enemy's shot the hero lives through costs the shooter its thorns too (`thornsOf` in `volley.ts`: `thornDamage` of its full HP, `hp` plus the tile's `wound`), wounding it or clearing it as a `VolleyKill` marked `thorns` (no Instakill, no crits; the status line says "Thorns"); the enemy's `Shot` records `thorns` and its `before`/`hp`/`full`, which the board popups raise over the shooter (`from`) with its HP bar, and a felled shooter stands at `enemyOf(shot)` in `ShotsInFlight` until its shot lands.
The planners and forecasts don't count shots, so neither do they count their thorns.
Thorns are certain, so `predict` counts them: with thorns it plays the rounds (`thornRounds`, or `lifestealEnding` with Lifesteal), never estimating (thorns end a fight within 200 rounds), and marks a fight its thorns end `thornKill` (the hero takes `turns` strikes, `replies`); `instakill` (a first strike that wins, no thorn kill) replaces `turns === 1` in kill pay, the inspect panel and Damage Visual's gray.
`resume` reads `thornHp`, `summarize` sums a round's thorns, and the crit forecast's fight lengths (`turnOdds`) end on either side's turn.

**Parries** (the Inspiration skill **Parry**, 10, between Regen Research and Revive; the Defense stat row Parry %, `parry`, 0.5% a rank, 100 ranks, on `Player` in percent) are certain: a hero with any Parry % parries every enemy strike, cutting what it does after DEF by Parry % (`struckDamage`) before the shroud and Lifesteal's heals; the enemy's ATK rises as usual.
There is no parry chance and no parry roll.
`parryCut` holds Parry % to `PARRY_CAP` (98), and `loadout` adds the Archives' Parry % research (`parryPercent`, in tenths of a percent) to the trained Parry % under the same cap; research completes through `changeLoadout`, so a run inside gains a level at once and undo keeps it.
`bout`/`resume` parry every strike once `parryCut` is above 0, and `predict` (`damageTaken`'s and `lifestealEnding`'s `cut`, `parriedEstimate` past the played rounds) counts it, so `resolveStep`, every planner, the inspect panel and Damage Visual's labels (`DamagePredictions.cost`, keyed by the hero's thorns and Parry %) all count parries, and a fight plays out as predicted.
A parried `Strike` carries `parried`, and in an animated fight the hero gives way a little from it (`lunges`, `PARRY_GIVE`); summary rounds drop it, showing only the damage.
`tests/thorns-parry.test.ts` covers both.
How a fight begins (`squaredUp`): ATK UP's Power Strike doubles the hero's ATK, and a boss's Takedown and Tangle (TORCH's Greater kin for a Greater Boss) cut the enemy before the first strike, in every prediction and in `bout`, whose `opening` holds the first strike back while the board shows them.
**Dash Combo** (the Breakthrough skill, off Dash) is `run.combo` (`{ next, idle }`: the Combo the next fight would have and the turns since the last fight; absent with none), so undo and the save carry it.
`Game.comboTurn`, called from `endTurn`, counts a turn with no fight (`COMBO_TURNS`, 2, end it) and starts a combo (`{ next: 1, idle: 1 }`) on a dash that lands beside an enemy (`besideEnemy`); `enter` raises `next` once a fight is won.
`Game.fighter` is the hero with `comboAttack` (`next` × `COMBO_PERCENT`), a copy that only `Game.move`'s `resolveStep` and `FightPlayback`'s `bout` and `retrain` take, and `squaredUp` applies as its own ATK factor: every preview and planner reads the plain hero, so they count the worst case, as with crits.
`Game.comboShown` is the meter (`renderCombo`, `#combo-stat`): `next` while a fight plays out, otherwise `next` − 1, so a dash with no fight after it never shows.
`tests/dash-combo.test.ts` covers it.
A **Fast** enemy (`enemy.fast`, `src/enemies/fast.ts`) strikes first: `bout` starts with `enemyNext`, and `predict` counts one more enemy strike (`CombatPrediction.fast`, read by `replies`), as do `thornRounds`, `lifestealEnding` (whose opening strike heals nothing) and the crit forecast's `turnOdds` (`opening`); `instakill` is never true against one, so Mug pays nothing more and Damage Visual never grays it (`DamagePredictions` keys by `fast`), and `RunPurse.killGold` pays `FAST_GOLD` more.
`tests/fast-enemies.test.ts` covers it.
**Ranged enemies and the Archer** (`src/enemies/ranged.ts`): from the third tier, `rangedCells` marks `enemy.ranged` on its own tile roll (`RANGED_SALT`, `rangedShare`: Fast's share less 1,000 thousandths), after `fastCells`, in both worlds; `RunPurse.killGold` pays `RANGED_GOLD` more.
A turn ends in `Game.take` (after `land`), or, for a dash, once `dashOn` stops (its steps' `CardStep.hold`): `endTurn` (nothing in the forest, nothing below the third tier unless the hero is an Archer, nothing on a turn that climbed to another Tower floor) counts `run.volleys.turn` and plays the turn's volley through `volleyTurn` (`src/volley.ts`), passing the tile the hero began the turn on (`turnBefore`'s player), then applies its outcome: the hero's HP, the volley state, the area's mastery and the Event's hurt count when a shot got through, each kill's pay (`finds.kill`, in the order the arrows felled them) and message, the shots for the board, and a fall.
`volleyTurn` takes the board, its wrap, each tile's equivalent floor, the hero and the tile it began on (`from`), the run's seed and volley state, whether the hero is an Archer (`Game.isArcher`: the hand holds the ARCHER card, `shootsWith` in `cards/cards.ts`), the ranks and research its arrows read, and the hand's `handShot` bonus, and changes nothing else it is given.
It plays the turn a **tick** at a time (`TURN_TICKS`, 100 a turn, 1 to 100, the last the turn's end), the hero walking its step from `from` to where it stands at an even pace (`Walk`); a turn that came farther than a step (a dash, a floor's stairs) or no `from` counts as standing all turn where it ended.
Step effects (the fight, pickups, doors, Regen) come before tick 1, and the Aggroed enemies' steps after tick 100.
It finds every enemy the step may bring in reach once (`enemiesWithin` from the start tile, the reach plus 1.5 tiles while walking), and for each the ticks the hero is within the Archer's reach of it and within its own (`reachTicks`), then runs the ticks: the Archer's clock (`archerTick`, through `arrows`) first, its arrow aimed at what stands that tick, then each shooter whose cooldown is ready and whose reach holds the hero, closest first by the distance that tick (then row and column).
Every shot lands the tick it fires: an arrow wounds or clears its enemy on the board at once, so the next arrow aims at what is left and an enemy it fells shoots no more that turn, and the shot that fells the hero ends the turn there (the arrows before it have landed, and their kills pay; the fall's undo takes the whole turn back).
In a tick both fire in, the Archer's arrows land first.
**`reachTicks`** is closed form: the hero's offset from a target at tick k is X(k) = 100·(dx, dy) + k·(ux, uy) hundredths of a tile, |X(k)|² = A·k² + 2B·k + C, and it is within reach between the quadratic's roots; each end is then settled on whole ticks by the exact integer test, so `Math.sqrt`'s rounding never matters and every engine agrees.
A Ranged enemy's reach (`foeRange`, five sixths of the hero's) is compared in whole numbers as `FOE_REACH`: 36 times the squared distance against 25 times the range squared.
`tests/volley.test.ts` checks `reachTicks` against tick-by-tick distances for every offset, step and several ranges.
Paying the kills after the volley changes nothing: kill pay draws from the game stream, the volley's rolls are fixed per tile, and XP and found equipment don't change the hero's stats mid-run.
Every cooldown is whole charge units (`charged`, `ready`, `spent`, `held`), absent meaning ready: a Ranged enemy gains `foeGain` a turn (`FOE_GAIN`, 500, twice that for a Fast one, so it shoots twice as often on any floor), a hundredth of it each tick (`foeTickGain`, a whole number), and a shot costs `foeShotCost(floor)` (5,000 less a unit a floor, at least 500), kept by tile in `run.volleys.foes` (dropped once its tile holds no Ranged enemy, or full again); the Archer gains its Attack Speed (`attackSpeed`, twentieths of a shot every 10 turns) every tick and an arrow costs `ARROW_COST` (20,000), kept in `run.volleys.hero`.
A shooter the hero comes within reach of in the turn charges a tick at a time and fires the tick it is ready and the hero in reach, its cooldown starting over from that tick (the charge over the cost carrying); the rest charge a whole turn at once, which comes to the same.
A shooter ready with nothing to shoot waits full, banking nothing, and fires the first tick something is in reach (an arrow or shot waiting ready as a turn begins fires at tick 1 if it can).
`archerTick` runs one tick of the Archer's clock (`archerState`/`archerClock` convert the saved clock), and `archerTurn` a whole turn of it, for tests.
**Rapid Fire** (Tower III's floor 80 Goal): a shot fired outside it starts it on a `tileRandom` of the hero's tile under `rapidFireChance` (`rapidFire` in `volley.ts`, `RAPID_FIRE_SALT`), for `rapidFireDuration` ticks from that tick (`run.volleys.rapid`, so undo takes it back), while which the charge grows `RAPID_FIRE_SPEED` (4) times as fast; the charge left when it ends carries at the usual pace.
A turn may then fire more than once: each shot of the turn has its own index (`shot`), mixed into its multishot, crit and Rapid Fire rolls (`shotRoll`; the first keeps the turn's own roll).
Reach is Euclidean in hundredths of a tile (`within`, `enemiesWithin`: closest first, then row and column, wrapping in the Delve): the Archer's `attackRange` (300, +5 a rank), a Ranged enemy's `foeRange` (five sixths of it).
A shot (`shotDamage`) is the enemy's ATK less DEF, cut by Parry % (every shot is parried), less the whole shroud; it costs the area's mastery when it costs HP, and one that leaves no HP fells the hero through `fallIn` with the snapshot from before the turn (`turnBefore`), so one undo takes the step back. No Revive; planners and `resolveStep` don't see shots.
An arrow (`arrowDamage`) is one hero strike (boss ATK, times its `arrowBoost`, crit rolls by `ARROW_SALT`, Piercing, enemy DEF) at the closest enemy it can hurt (judged with the boost, before the crit roll, so a far enemy plain arrows can't hurt can become the target), healing by Lifesteal; it wounds the enemy (`Board.wound`, a `wound` on the generated tile in `run.changes`, which both worlds' `tile` takes off the enemy's HP after the boons' `foeTile`; `save/save.ts` accepts wounded enemies in changes) or clears its tile and pays the kill (`finds.kill`, shared with `winFight`; an instakill when the tile had no wound).
`arrowBoost` multiplies two factors: ATK / m (`attackPerTile`, hundred-thousandths of ×1 a tile, times `Math.sqrt(d2)`, the Euclidean distance the tick the arrow fires, mid-step perhaps, round the Delve's wrap) and Charged Shot (the `chargedShotPercent` research times `run.volleys.waited`, the turns the arrow ended waiting ready, counted in `arrows` only once that research has a level and cleared when it fires); a crit multiplies on top; the hand's `handShot` (FLYING ENEMY's Shot ATK, and ARCHER's `boss` percent added to it only for an arrow at a boss or Greater Boss, `isBoss` in `combat.ts`; fights never read it) goes to `arrowDamage` as `cards`, which `attackAgainst` adds to the enemy cards' percents, one factor for the hand.
A multishot (`multishot`: a `tileRandom` of the hero's tile under Multishot Chance, by `MULTISHOT_SALT`, the turn and the shot of the turn) spends one arrow's charge on up to `multishotTargets` of the closest enemies the boost lets it hurt (fewer when fewer are in range); each arrow goes through `arrow` with its own boost for its distance, its own crit roll (by its target's tile), and the first's `waited` Charged Shot.
A charged arrow's `Shot` carries its `charge` (the percent Charged Shot added, absent at 0): `drawVolley` flies it in a blue glow (`CHARGED_GLOW`), or a blue PNG arrow with the Classic sprite kit active, and `BoardPopups` draws its number with a gold outline under the black one, `CHARGED_RING` from `FULL_CHARGE` (50%) and the fainter, narrower `FAINT_RING` below it.
`Game.volley` (`Volley`: the turn's `Shot`s, each with the `tick` it fired, shown from `at` for the turn's `ms`, `min(TURN_MS, 800 / steps a second)`) is what the board draws (`render/volley-art.ts`) and the popups land: each shot leaves `tick / TURN_TICKS` of the way through the turn (`leavesAt`) and flies `SHOT_FLIGHT` (half) of a turn (`landsAt`), so a turn's last shots land as the next begins; a shot's damage rises over the hero, an arrow's over the enemy with its HP bar (`arrowBars`), as it lands; undo, a new run or a mode switch clears it.
`Game.turnShown` (`ShownTurn` in `shown.ts`: from, to, `at` and `ms`, the volley's own) is the turn's single step, which the renderer walks the hero along at an even pace, so it stands where each shot leaves from; null when the turn came no single step.
An arrow's `Shot` carries the hero's place the tick it fired (`from`, between tiles mid-step) and the enemy it felled (`fell`); an enemy's shot is drawn homing on the hero as drawn, and its `to` is the tile the step ends on.
`tests/ranged.test.ts` covers it through `Game`, and `tests/volley.test.ts` plays single turns through `volleyTurn` on a stub board (order through the turn and in a tick, a fall, reach mid-step, cooldowns restarting from the tick, charges carried and dropped, Rapid Fire retargeting, wounds, the multishot rate) and checks the display timing (`shotFlight`, `ShotsInFlight`).
**Flying and Aggroed enemies** (`src/enemies/flying.ts`; `docs/ENEMY_SCHEDULE.md`): from the fifth tier, `flyingCells` lifts an enemy on its own tile roll (`FLYING_SALT`, the Ranged share, `flyingShare`; never a boss, Greater Boss or Shielded enemy), before `fastCells` and `rangedCells`, in both worlds: its tile becomes open floor carrying it (`Tile.flyer`, the enemy `flying`), so every step, card, route and planner treats it as floor and no fight ever starts with it.
The boards' `wound` takes a flyer's wound on the same tile field, and `wounded`, `foeTile` (boons) and `tournamentTile` scale it as they do an enemy; `save/save.ts` accepts open floor carrying a valid flyer in the changes.
`foeOn` (enemies/ranged.ts) is the enemy a tile holds, standing or hovering, and `shoots` whether it shoots: `volleyTurn` plays a flyer's shots as a Ranged enemy's, and `enemiesWithin` reaches flyers (one over the hero's own tile first), so the Archer's arrows shoot them; `RunPurse.killGold` pays `RANGED_GOLD` for one.
An arrow that wounds a flyer makes it Aggroed (`woundMore` sets `aggro`): after each turn's volley (unless it felled the hero), `Game.endTurn` plays `aggroTurn`, which finds every Aggroed enemy in `run.changes`, makes one map of the walk to the hero over open floor (`walkToHero`) for them all, and steps each, closest first, onto a neighbour nearer the hero once its `stride` charge (gaining `FOE_GAIN` a turn) holds `foeShotCost` of its floor: a flyer onto open floor no flyer holds and no lit torch stands on, or over the hero; one on the ground the same but never onto the hero (nothing yet makes a ground enemy Aggroed, and no fight begins from its side).
A moved enemy keeps `home`, the tile it was made on, which `RunPurse.killKey` keys its loot by, and its Ranged cooldown moves with it in `run.volleys.foes`; it all lives in the run, so undo takes a step back.
`tests/flying.test.ts` covers it.
**Shielded enemies** (`src/enemies/shielded.ts`, from the fourth tier, one at most a floor; `docs/ENEMY_SCHEDULE.md`): the board sets `enemy.guard` (`SHIELD_CUT`, 60) on every enemy tile within a standing Shielded enemy's bubble as it reads it (`guardedTile`; never saved), and `guarded` in `combat.ts` cuts what gets past DEF by it in `heroHit` and `arrowDamage`, so `predict`, `bout`, the crit forecast, the planners, Damage Visual (`DamagePredictions` keys by `guard`) and the inspect panel (which notes it) all count it; a hit that can't get past DEF stays impervious, and one that does still lands. Thorns are not cut; Lifesteal heals from the cut hit. `attackForFewerHits` guesses through the cut. `RunPurse.killGold` pays `SHIELDED_GOLD` more.
`tests/shielded.test.ts` covers it.
Each revival's strike first raises the same skull (`fights.struckDown`, the hero's HP shown at 0 meanwhile, `heroHpDuring`), and `bout` holds the fight's next strike back `REVIVE_PAUSE_MS` (the skull's `DEFEAT_MS` and the fire's `REVIVE_MS`), as `summarize` does its next round (`showRevivals` sets both lists).
Once the skull is over, `fights.revivedAt` begins, which the renderer draws as a smaller golden version of the level-up burst under a white and golden beam of light that a few small white angel wings flutter down and fade from over a second (`drawRevive` in `render/effects/level-up.ts`); `tests/revive.test.ts` covers it.
A revival Angel Wings gave is also in `fights.wingedAt`, drawn instead by `drawWingedRevive`: a broader blaze reading "ANGEL WINGS", a great pair of wings spreading behind the hero, and three times the small wings.
The inspect panel's Gold for an enemy is `Game.enemyGold` (`RunPurse.killGoldPreview`: `enemyLoot`'s Gold without paying it, the card's Effective or Dampen left out).
Each kill pays Gold (`ENEMY_GOLD`, into the save, gated by `lootedTiles`) and Silver (`silverForKill` by strength and equivalent floor, into `run.silver`, so undo takes it back).
The hand's enemy cards raise both sides of it: `attackAgainst` adds `Player.foeAttack`'s percents for the cards covering the enemy (`cards/foe-cards.ts`) into one factor, which multiplies the equipment's boss ATK, and `RunPurse.killGold` multiplies by `foeGold` (docs/agents/hand-and-automation.md); `DamagePredictions` keys by them, the boss ATK, Piercing and `flying`.
A kill's Gold, Cells and drops, and its count toward missions and the Event, are gated by `RunPurse.killKey`: the tile's loot key, a spawned enemy's with `:s<spawn>` after it, since it may stand where an enemy already fell and paid (the Greater Boss appears on the floor nearest the stairs, most often the stairs' guard's tile).
Every enemy made during a run, rather than with its floor, goes through `Game.spawnEnemy`, which numbers it (`Enemy.spawn`) from the run's count (`run.spawns`) and writes it into `run.changes`; undo rewinds the count with the run, so a spawn undone and made again keeps its number and its loot stays paid.
`tests/greater-boss.test.ts` covers two spawns on one paid tile, undo and reload.
`decodeLootedTiles` keeps a loot key with a word after it (`:pickup`, `:s0`, a card's), so a reload doesn't let undo pay it twice.
A kill's Silver rises smoothly, fractions kept (`silverBase`: 2 on floor 1, 0.2 a floor to floor 201, `SILVER_SLOWDOWN_FLOOR`, then 0.1).
Silver buys **run training** (`progression/run-training.ts`): Training ranks for the run alone, kept in `run.training` beside the hero's own (`ranksInRun`), each row's price from its `silver` schedule (`silverPrice`, by ranks bought this run) and its level capped at its `max`.
`Game.trainInRun` remembers an undo snapshot first (a purchase is a turn), then moves the player and `run.loadout` by what the row's next rank changes the loadout (`rankShift` in `progression/loadout.ts` and `shiftHero` in `progression/hero-stats.ts`: equipment's percent and research counted, then the boons; max HP with the HP to fill it), or raises `run.percentPotions` (and the floor's `RoomWorld`); bought during an encounter (not one in summary rounds), `fights.retrain` plays the fight on from its first strike not yet swinging with the hero's new stats (`resume` in `combat.ts`; max HP raising the HP shown so far) and updates the step's outcome the encounter carries, so the fight played and the one that counts agree; Potion % and Revive count through `game.trainingNow`, which `stepRules` and `reviveChance` (what `fights` rolls Revive by) read.
Both are open only once the Inspiration skill **On the Job** is owned (`game.trainsOnTheJob`, or Dev mode).
**Buy Quantity** (`progression/buy-quantity.ts`, the Inspiration skill of that name below Lifesteal, above GK Siphon) sets how many ranks one Training press buys: `TrainingDesk.buyQuantities` is x1 once the skill is owned and one more per level of its research (`buyQuantity` target: x5, x10, x25, x100, Max), each screen keeping its own choice (the Training tab's `buyQuantity` and the run training bar's `runBuyQuantity`; no Settings page rows; provisions are bought one at a time), read through `TrainingDesk.buyQuantityFor(scope)` and set with `setBuyQuantity(q, scope)`;
`bulkBuy` works out a press's ranks and summed price (a fixed quantity all or none, Max as many as what is held pays for), for training points (`trainingBulk` in `progression/loadout.ts`, `training.train(id, quantity)`) and run Silver (`runTrainingBulk`, `Game.trainInRun(id, quantity)`, one undo for them all);
`ui/buy-quantity-select.ts` draws the dropdown (only once there is more than x1 to choose; the game's own button and list, not a native `<select>`, whose picker covers the screen on phones: `buyQuantityHtml` draws it and `bindBuyQuantity` wires it, its list opening upward in the screen's lower half and closing on a press elsewhere) and the ranks a press buys: over a Max press's price on the Training tab (`maxCount`), and past x1 in the corner of each run training card's price (`runCount`), and `tests/buy-quantity.test.ts` covers it.
`ui/run-training-bar.ts` (`RunTrainingBar`) draws the bar of tabs under the hand inside a run (Hand, `[data-run-tab="hand"]`, wearing the Deck tab's `DECK_ICON` (named Auto, with an arrow, once Auto-Climb is owned or the run holds a Manual hand), then Manual, `[data-run-tab="manual"]`, while the run holds a Manual hand (see hand-and-automation.md), then the Training group buttons, `[data-run-group]`; each only its icon while not selected, the selected one adding its name, `.group-name`, so the bar fits a phone; and the Buy Quantity dropdown at its right; `open` is the group selected, `null` for Hand, which is selected outside a run and again whenever the mode or run seed changes, and pressing the selected tab does nothing), and the open group's cards in the hand's place (each card's value by `trainingText` with `bare`, Attack Range, Attack Speed, ATK / m and Multishot Targets without their units, which the details give; a card's face pressed shows its details in `#drill-tip`, a mouse over it too, and a second press on that card hides them, `tipPressed`); the first run after On the Job it teaches the bar (`save.tutorials.onTheJob`: a note over the hand and the tutorials' pointing hand, `POINTER_SVG` in `ui/dom.ts`, until a group is opened and Hand pressed again).
