# Tiers, Goals, floors and areas

Tiers (towers and caves), Goals and Warp, Tower floor transitions (`TowerClimb`), undo's `restore`, and area rewards (`AreaLedger`).

**Tiers** (`tiers.ts`) are the numbered towers and the Delve's caves: claiming floor 100's Goals reward in a tower (a `tower` reward, `TOWER_UNLOCK_FLOOR`, in towers 1 to 8; ready once floor 101 is reached, its boss beaten) opens the next (`slice.tiersOpen`, up to `TIERS` = 9), and the cave of the same number with it (`save.delve.tiersOpen` follows the Tower's, set by `openTower` in `goals.ts` and by `decodeTiers`; no boss kill opens anything itself).
A run carries its `tier` (absent for the first, so tier-1 runs and every golden are unchanged), and its boards (`RoomWorld`, the Delve `World`) pass their generated cells through `tierCells`, which multiplies each enemy's HP, ATK and DEF by `tierStats` (the product of `TIER_STEP_FACTORS` up to the tier: ×5 in Tower II, ×4 more in III, ×3 each after: 1, 5, 20, 60 …).
Layouts differ between tiers only in their doors and key colours: a Tower floor's keys of each colour appear from that colour's door's first floor (`towerKeyColorsOn`, by the door stage's schedule, which `generateTowerFloor` and `generateStrategicGraph` take the tier for), and each delve follows the tower of its number by equivalent floor (the Delve's `region` takes the tier for it); options holding a closed colour are dropped, by `onlyOpenKeys`, before each pick.
In the first tower (`bypassesRareKeys`) a blue or red door never stands alone on the way to the stairs: the door stage puts them only on branches there, and `planForks` lets a main-route fork hold one only beside a lane that needs neither colour, so the embedder's fallback to the single gate never makes one either.
`tests/key-schedule.test.ts` covers it.
Kill and treasure Gold (`tierGold`, its fraction kept) and the Delve's end-of-run Gold take the tier's bonus (`TIER_BONUS_TENTHS`: ×1, ×2, ×3.1, ×4.3 … ×11.8), and kill XP the enemies' stat factor (`tierXp`: `tierStats`, ×5, ×20 …); Silver, potions and keys don't; an ATK or DEF shard raises its stat by `tierShard` (1, ×2.75 compounding a tier), which `tierTile` writes into the shard's `amount` and `resolveStep` reads (`shardGain`, `SHARD` = 1 in the first tier).
Each tier keeps its own records: the slice's `best` and `reached` belong to the selected `slice.tier`, and `Game.selectTier` (from the forest only, the arrows beside the board) moves them into `slice.tierRecords` and brings the chosen tier's in (`switchTier`), so each tier pays its own milestones.
The forest's weather comes from its run seed (`outsideWeather`), so `selectTier` keeps each tier's forest seed per mode (`Game.forestSeeds`, never saved) and brings it back when the tier is selected again: switching between towers or caves shows the weather each had, until a run begins (`enterFromOutside`) or ends (`finalizeRun`), which forget them so the next forests are rolled afresh.
The forest's entrance shows the tier's numeral (`drawEntrance`) and the board's subtitle its bonus once a second is open.
`tests/tiers.test.ts` covers it.
**Goals** (`goals.ts`) are each Tower tier's checkpoints (`CHECKPOINTS`: `CHECKPOINT_FLOORS`, every ten floors to 100 and then 150 and 200, a reward and a premium reward each; Tower I's `TOWER_ONE` and Tower II's `TOWER_TWO` set the ones that aren't the stub (Tower I's floor 40 pays 20 Gems, floor 90 2,000 Gold and 10,000 premium, floor 200 40 Gems), and Tower I's unlocks are `GoalUnlock`s, read by `goalUnlocked`: floor 10's Damage Prediction, the inspect panel's damage and Survivable/LETHAL/Instakill line (`prediction` in `ui/tile-info.ts`; without it the panel shows only the enemy's stats), floor 20's Combat Forecast and floor 30's Attack Lore, its hits-to-defeat and ATK-for-one-hit-fewer lines (`forecast`, the latter from `attackForFewerHits` in `combat.ts`), floor 50's Damage Visual, each enemy in view wearing its fight's cost in its lower-left corner, coloured once Tower II's floor 10 unlock (`TOWER_TWO`), Relative Damage Color, is claimed by its share of the hero's HP (`relativeColor`: bright green below 1%, yellow at 10%, orange at 25%, red from 50%, blended between; an Instakill stays gray, a fight costing no HP as shown a white 0, a lethal fight red, and a net heal a blue `+N`) (`damage-labels.ts`: `DamagePredictions` caches `predict` by enemy stats until the hero's ATK, DEF or shroud changes, `damageLabel` shortens it with `compactAmount` in `whole.ts` and colours it against the live HP, drawn by `Renderer.drawOverlays` over the darkness, never on the enemy being fought, and off with the `damageVisual` setting; `tests/damage-labels.test.ts`), and floor 60's Equipment (`claimGoal` sets `save.equipment.unlocked`; the Goals page's claim dialog offers the Blacksmith), and floor 70's Tournament (below), and floor 150's Research Speed (the Archives project's `goalUnlock` requirement, `ResearchRequirement` in `archives.ts`: `ArchivesOwner` carries the save's `goals`); Tower II's floor 40 unlocks Warp (starting a run past a completed checkpoint, in any open tower); floor 100 opens the next tower; the rest stubs; `goalUnlocked` finds an unlock's checkpoint in any tower, `unlockAt`) and the Premium Passes (`PASSES`, three towers each, entitlements sold as Shop offers).
`goalState` reads a reward's state from the tier's highest floor completed (`floorsCompleted`: the slice's `reached`, or the tier's record, 0 before the first floor's stairs are climbed; a checkpoint's floor counts once the floor above is reached) and `save.goals` (claimed floors by tower, kept apart from the tier records so `switchTier` never moves them); `claimGoal` pays it once.
`Game.warp` (forest only, once `warpUnlocked` and the area ending at the checkpoint mastered, `canWarp`) selects the tier if needed and starts a run inside at once with `newRun`'s `height` on the floor after the checkpoint, at full HP; every other run starts on floor 1.
`tests/goals.test.ts` covers it.

Tower floor transitions go through `TowerClimb` (`tower/climb.ts`, over the run): the floor the player stands on keeps its changes in `run.changes` and every other visited floor keeps its own in `run.floors` by height, moved across on each climb or descent so no floor's changes are ever shared; it hands out any floor's board (`board(h)`, the same view play and the deadlock search read), `up`/`down` return the new floor's board and where the player stands, and a section's first floor is sealed below.
`Game` applies what a new floor or section means (keys, progress, an area's rewards; the hero keeps every stat into a new section).
`tests/tower-climb.test.ts` covers it.
`restore` (undo) rebuilds the board from a copy of the snapshot run that keeps damage taken in the same area on record; `loadMode` shares `adoptRun`/`buildWorld` with it and migrates runs saved under an older layout.
Tower area rewards are decided only by `AreaLedger` (`tower/area-ledger.ts`): an area is a section's ten floors, ending at a Goals checkpoint, and `enter`, called as `advanceTowerRoom` climbs onto a section's first floor, judges the area below, reading every floor of it from the climb.
It is **mastered** when the hero took no fight damage in it (`run.damaged`, set by a fight that cost HP, never by a Heart Door's toll, and reset as each area starts; only an area ending at a checkpoint, `checkpoint` in `goals.ts`) and **cleared** when no enemy is left on any of its floors (`CLEARED_INSPIRATION`, 10, into `save.tower.inspiration` and `runCurrency`, at any height).
Each is paid the moment it is earned, once per tower and area, and recorded in `save.goals` (`mastered` and `cleared`, by tower, by the area's last floor; `areaMastered`, `areaCleared`), outside the run, so undo, reloads and later runs never pay it twice.
A chest for each then replaces what stands on the first tile inside (`ENTRY`; the second on the tile behind or beside it): a `reward` tile in the floor's `run.changes`, gold for mastered and silver for cleared (`AREA_CHEST`, `chestReward`), which pays nothing when opened but queues a burst in `game.areaBursts` (one opened while another plays starts as it ends), which the renderer draws as a golden burst (`drawAreaBurst` in `level-up.ts`: *AREA MASTERED* over *Checkpoint Warp Unlocked*, or *ENEMIES CLEARED* over +10 and the Inspiration icon).
`tests/area-ledger.test.ts` covers it.
`tests/step-trace.test.ts` replays seeded Tower/Delve runs against `tests/fixtures/step-trace.golden.json`, and `tests/undo-clear-trace.test.ts` does the same for undo, reloads (some from an older layout), room clears and their chests against `tests/fixtures/undo-clear-trace.golden.json`; if a gameplay change is intended, regenerate with `UPDATE_GOLDEN=1`.
