# The hand, badges, charges and automation

The planners, Tower automation, the hand of cards, card badges, Focus/Ignore/Target charges, Dash, play/pause, and deadlock.

The planners (`routeTo`, Tower automation's search and Delve Automove) work from a `Position` (`board.ts`: a board and the run standing on it), never the whole `Game`.
`chooseStep(game)` in `automation.ts` is the one entry that reads the rest (the mode and Delve Automove's memory, plan and upgrades), and `tests/planners.test.ts` drives the planners on hand-built boards.
Tower automation (`automation.ts`) runs a bounded breadth-first search (`Search`) with separate destination scoring.
It stops searching at interactions and replans every step rather than assuming future keys or health, never takes lethal fights, and scores progress against the run record so it can backtrack without oscillating.
Inside a run (Tower or Delve, not the forest) the player's **hand** moves the hero: `cards.ts` holds the cards (`CARDS`, each with its name, its text and, for some, a `bonus`, the new profile's `BASE_HAND` (STAIRS, DOOR, KEY, ENEMY) filling the hand's `BASE_HAND_SLOTS`, more with Larger Hand and Gems (`handSlots`), saved as `save.hand`, and `moveCard`, the Deck's reorder) and `planHand`, a pure breadth-first search from the hero over crossable tiles, the whole ground the hero can walk with no row limit (only `MOST_WALKED`, 20,000 tiles, more than a Tower floor or a Delve area holds, so a search can't run on up the endless Delve) (floor, one-way gates and every item; walls, doors, monsters and stairs only as targets) (of equally short paths, the one stepping on the fewest `takesSomething` tiles, so the hand walks round items and lit torches unless they lie on the only shortest way; Charge's search doesn't) that returns the first card in priority order that can act: one that acts in place (`IN_PLACE`: KEY SIPHON, asked through the `HandAt`'s `canAct`, which `HandRules` answers; such a plan has no path, and `handTurn` runs it as a turn with no step: `CardActivation.siphon` (`game/card-activation.ts`, the game's `activation`) trades the run's top Max HP training levels, `siphonCost` of them (one more each use: `run.siphoned` counts the uses), out of `siphonLevel`, the hero's ranks plus those bought with Silver less those siphoned, for a yellow key, after an undo snapshot; without enough left the HUD greys the card, `unable`), or one with a reachable target on the current floor only (never through stairs) and the shortest path to its closest one (the Delve's STAIRS card, whose text, `cardText`, says so only once Into the depths is owned, climbs to the highest crossable tile it can reach instead, however far from the view, only above the highest row the run has reached, `run.top`, so it never leads back to a row stood on before).
**Card bonuses** (`CardBonus`, read with `cardBonus`) are what a card gives for as long as the run's hand holds it, whichever card leads the hero there: YELLOW, BLUE and RED KEY make each key of their colour picked up worth 5% more and pay 1, 2 or 3 more Pickup Gold, and CHEST gives each treasure chest a 1.5% chance of being a Supply Chest and 0.5% of being an Equipment Chest, fixed as the run goes inside and its floors are generated (see progression.md); KEY, heading for a key of any colour, gives none.
`handBonus(hand)` adds them up (`Game.handBonus`, none in the forest): `Game.stepRules` passes the key percents as `StepRules.keyFind`, which `resolveStep` multiplies a key by (after a badge's scale), `Finds.pickup` adds the key Gold to the key's Pickup Gold, and `Game.dealHand` fixes the chest odds as the run's `chestOdds`, which its boards mark each chest's kind by.
`cardBonusLines` words them for the card's details (`ui/card-reveal.ts`, the chest's chances light blue and purple) and `cardBonusText` for the hand's and Deck's tooltips; `tests/card-bonus.test.ts` covers them.
A run copies `save.hand` into `run.hand` when it goes inside (`game.hand` reads the run's, or in the forest the saved one), so reordering never reaches a run already inside; `deck.arrange` (`DeckEditor`, `game/deck-editor.ts`, the game's `deck`) reorders `save.hand` only in the forest, once Combat Stance is owned, and `deck.add` and `deck.remove` move the deck's cards (`deckCards(upgrades)`: the base hand's, and each card an upgrade row names with `card`, `upgradeCard`) in and out of it once Buildout is owned; STAIRS never leaves.
**Card Groups** (Tower II's floor 90 Goal, `goalUnlocked(save, "cardGroups")`) link cards side by side in the hand: `save.cardLinks` holds each link as the pair of cards it joins (`CardLink`, left then right), `deck.link(i)` (`DeckEditor`, in the forest, `canLink`) toggles the link after slot `i` (`toggleLink`), and every change to the hand (`arrange`, `place`, `remove`, through `DeckEditor.setHand`, and the decoder) keeps only the links still joining neighbours (`keptLinks`), so a card moved out of place breaks its links.
A run copies them into `run.links` with its hand (`dealHand`), and `HandRules.links` (`linkedToNext`: for each card, whether it is linked to the next) is the `HandAt.links` `planHand` reads: it plans each card as before (`planCard`) but takes the shortest plan of a run of linked cards (a card acting in place, with no path, before all; ties to the earlier card), going on past the group only when none of its cards can act; a focused card (`only`) plans alone.
The Deck page (`linksHtml` in `ui/deck-page.ts`, `.with-links`) draws a diamond toggle after each hand card but the last, in a row gap under each row of the grid, and each link as two half brackets, one under each card, so a group wrapping to the next row reads across it.
`tests/card-groups.test.ts` covers it.
**The hand rules** (`hand-rules.ts`, `HandRules`, the game's `handRules`) are what the hand's cards may do now, kept apart from `Game`'s turn: each card's `CardRules` from its badges, Deprioritize's marks and the Stairward and Skip Open Nodes cooldowns by floor, Skip's stream of rolls, the tiles Ignore marked, Card Groups' links, and whether a card that acts in place can act (`canAct`, `cardUses`, `siphonLevel`, `siphonCost`).
It is the `HandAt` the hand plans from (`planHand`, `planTarget`, `explainStuck` and `walkAll` take it), reads the save, the run, its board, the mode, the hand and Key Efficiency's cost from a narrow host (`HandHost`) each time, and keeps everything it changes in the run, so undo and reloads keep it.
`Game`'s turn (`handTurn`, the dash, Target's walk, Focus and the payouts) asks it and applies the effects; the HUD and the renderer read its marks and greying from it.
`tests/hand-rules.test.ts` drives it on hand-built boards through a plain-object host, with no `Game`.
**Card badges** (`badges.ts`, opened by the Courage skill **Badges**) are tokens attached to cards: `BADGES` holds each one's rarity, kind (`reward`, `gate`, `aim`, `scale` or `chance`), symbol and values by level, levels come from copies (`COPIES_FOR_LEVEL`: the first copy opens level 1 and counts toward level 2; 80 reach level 7), and `drawBadges` draws by `RARITY_WEIGHTS` among the rarities with a badge left below level 7 (maxed ones leave the pool, and a badge with an `unlock` joins it only once that upgrade is owned: the key gates wait for Key < Badges and Key ≥ Badges), continuing the stream saved in `save.badges.rng` (seeded once from `stream("badges")`), so a purchase can't be redrawn by reloading.
`save.badges` also holds the copies and a gate's chosen threshold (`pick`) per badge, and `cards`, the badges each card holds, top slot first (a badge on one card at most).
A card has one slot, and one more for each level of the Archives' **Badge Slots** research (`researched(…, "badgeSlots")`, `BadgeDesk.slots`; opened by Tower VI's floor 50 Goal): `attachBadge` fills the next free slot or, every slot full, replaces the bottom one, which swaps into the dropped badge's old slot (or comes off).
Badges that can't work together never share a card (`badgeConflict`, the pairs in `CONFLICTS`: a key colour's `<` and `≥` gates, Effective with Dampen; gates of different things, HP < with YK ≥, may share one): `attachBadge` refuses one, `attachConflict` (`BadgeDesk.conflict`) names the badge in the way so the Deck page can say why in a dialog, and both decoders keep only the first of such a pair.
The Deck page's `BadgeDesk` (`game/badge-desk.ts`, the game's `badges`) draws for Gems (`DRAW_GEMS`), attaches, detaches and picks, in the forest only; a run copies its hand's into `run.badges` as it goes inside (`runBadges`, a list per card, read with `badgesOn` and `badgeOn`).
A gate badge is checked again on every turn of a committed path (`HandRules.gateShut`, unless the card is focused): if it no longer holds (HP rose past HP <, say, from Regen), the path is dropped and the hand chooses a card again.
The hand card's tooltip names each of its badges on a line of its own, top slot first, with the value in effect (`badgeTitle` in `ui/hud.ts`: "HP < 100%"), not its level; the card wears their icons down its top right (`.hand-badges`), and on the Deck page their marks down its top left (`.badge-marks`).
Inside a run `HandRules.cardRules` turns each card's badges into one `CardRules` (each badge's rules merged, `closed` if any closes it) `planHand` takes through `HandAt.cardRules`: a gate's `closed` skips the card (unless it is the focused card, planned with `only`), Stairward's `stairward` picks the target nearest the stairs by walk (the highest in the Delve), Skip Open Nodes' `skipOpen` passes over a door or monster whose open neighbours the hero can all reach already (reporting each into `run.skipped`, which the renderer marks with an X for that floor), and Charge's `charge` searches through up to that many fightable monsters and doors the hero holds keys for (`chargeSearch`).
Stairward and Skip Open Nodes cool down by floor (`run.badgeFloors`, the floor each last worked on, by `HandRules.floor`: the Tower floor, or the Delve's equivalent floor where the hero stands).
**Deprioritize**'s `deprioritize` passes over the target the card would pick, marking it ? (`run.marks`, by `HandRules.floor`, cleared as its tile is stepped on), and the cards after it plan round it; `HandAt.marked` (the ? tiles, `HandRules.marked`) keeps every card's search off them, and once its level's marks are made the card rests `closed` for the floor (`HandRules.cardResting`, which the HUD greys as `unable`).
When no card can act, `handTurn` turns the ? nearest by walk into ! (`raiseBang`) and the card's `bang` heads for it by any path; the renderer draws both marks (`drawBadgeMarks`).
**Effective** and **Dampen** (`scale`) multiply the card's target's effect when it activates: `handTurn` resolves the card's last step with `StepRules.scale` (`scaleOf`: 1.05 … 1.35, or 0.95 … 0.65), so `resolveStep` scales a potion's heal, a key (keys held are fractional, `snap`ped, shown by `keyCount` in `whole.ts` to two decimals when not whole), a shard, each key a door takes (`doorCost(tile, player, scale)` needs that much of each colour held; DOOR's `CardRules.scale` makes the card want only doors it can pay; the door cards want a door run only while the hero can pay for every door of it, `paysRun`), a Heart Door's toll (only ever smaller) and a fight's damage, which the fight plays out as predicted and adds (`StepEffect.extraDamage`, which can fell the hero; given back as a heal below 0) when it settles, with its Silver, Gold and XP (`StepEffect.scale`, through `winFight` and `RunPurse`); KEY SIPHON's key too.
**Skip** (`chance`) rolls on the card's last step, drawing the next number of its own stream, started from the run seed (`HandRules.skipHits`, spent by `skipRolled`; `run.skipRolls` counts those drawn, counted once the step is in the undo history, so undo takes a roll back and a run of the same seed played the same way skips the same targets): the target vanishes without effect (`vanish`, a turn of its own, with `poof.ts`'s puff at `game.vanished`), never a boss or Greater Boss; on the Tower's stairs `advanceTowerRoom` climbs two floors (the step's `CardStep.floorSkip`; the floor climbed past pays no Spare Change or Wishing Well, and an area climbed past is still judged) with *Floor Skipped*.
It has no limit a floor.
**More Enemies** (`floor`, the one badge that changes the floors themselves, `more-enemies.ts`) sits only on a monster card (`BadgeDef.cards`, `fitsCard`: `attachBadge` refuses another card, a swap that would carry it onto one takes it off, and both decoders drop it there); `extraEnemies(run.badges)` sums each strength's percent over the cards covering it (MONSTER every strength, each from its own count; the Greater Boss never), and the boards add the extras as a last stage before `tierCells`: `RoomWorld` (its `extras`, through `withExtraEnemies`) and the Delve `World` (its second argument, through `delveExtras`, cached per area, each of the area's ten equivalent floors by tile depth counting and placing its own, bottom first, on one `extraGround` for the area: the tiles as a grid of indices with their neighbours and the shortest walk worked out once; the area's torches come from the cache `World.torches` reads too, so they are placed once).
`addEnemies` adds each strength's generated count times its percent, the fraction rolled as a chance of one more, each a copy of one of the floor's own of that strength, placed by `nextSpot`: corners of reward rooms behind a gate (a door or enemy), corners of other rooms behind one, corners and then edges of the core, any tile that cuts nothing off (Tarjan's articulation points, extras already placed counting as walls), and only then one that does, off the shortest walk from the way in to the target (the stairs, or the area's boss) first; never the way in or beside it, beside the stairs, near the milestone gate or on a torch.
All its rolls come from a stream seeded by the run seed and floor (`extrasSeed`), so a floor comes out the same whenever it is rebuilt; runs without it generate exactly as before, so no layout version moves.
`Game.newRun` rebuilds the board after `dealHand` so a run going straight inside gets its extras.
`tests/more-enemies.test.ts` covers it.
**Dash** (`aim`, rare, opened by the Courage skill Dash Badge) adds its value (1 to 7) to the tiles its card's first step toward a new target may dash across (`dashFor`), on top of Dash and End Rush; it needs no run state, since a dash is one turn and one undo.
Turning on Dev mode (`setDevMode`) owns Combat Stance, Buildout and Badges and every badge at level 7.
`handTurn` activates a card when its path's last step lands (after its fight settles, wrapping the encounter's `settle`) or an in-place card acts (`activation.actInPlace`): `activation.activate` (`CardActivation`, `game/card-activation.ts`) pays the rewards, HP and Silver and XP through the run (so undo takes them back) and Gold through `RunPurse.badgeGold`, gated per target in `lootedTiles`.
`ui/badge-token.ts` draws the tokens, the marks cards wear for them and each badge's value at every level (`badgeLevelsHtml`, from each `BADGES` entry's `unit` and `lead`, and a gate's `sign`, `<` or `≥` (`gateSign`), under its description in the Deck page's detail and the draw animations) and `ui/badge-reveal.ts` the draw animations.
**Max HP** raises the hero's max HP (and `run.loadout`'s) and its HP by the badge's value for the rest of the run, raised as a provision is (`flatShift`: Max HP research, then the boons).
**Goldback** (last card of the hand; it raises *×2* beside the Gold coin) adds its value to `run.goldCombo`, which the next kill's or chest's Gold multiplies by and spends (`RunPurse.withCombo`, once the Gold is actually paid; the combo is the run's, so undo takes an activation back).
The Badges draw screens offer `DrawAgain` ("x1 more" / "x10 more" with the Gem price, its price red (`short`) while unaffordable, and a press then asks for Gems) on their last screen.
`tests/badges.test.ts` covers it.
The run also gets `FOCUS_PER_RUN` Focus uses (`run.focusUsed` counts those spent, so a Focus Count level completed mid-run adds one at once) once the Focus skill is owned: `Game.focus(card)` spends one to put that card first (`run.focused`; refused, with a reason the HUD flashes, when none are left, the card leads already, or `planHand(…, only)` finds it no path), and `handTurn` plans the focused card alone until its path's last step or until it has none.
Both live in the run, so undo and reloads keep them.
Focus, **Ignore** and **Target** are the run's **charges** (`run-charges.ts`): each opens with its Courage skill, starts a run with its research's count (`chargesPerRun`: Focus Count, Ignore Count, Target Count), keeps only the uses spent in the run (`focusUsed`, `ignoreUsed`, `targetUsed`), and, once its regain skill is owned (Refocus, Ignore More, Target More), regains one every `regainEvery` new floors climbed (`REGAIN_FLOORS`, 100, less a floor a level of its research), `climbFloor` counting each floor new to the run into `run.regain` while the charge is below full (`Game.regainCharges`, from `advanceTowerRoom` and `payDelveFloors`).
`Game.arm` readies Ignore or Target from its HUD button (`game.armed`, never saved), and the next board tap goes to `Game.useArmed` instead of the overlay: Ignore marks the tile in `run.ignored` for the floor (`HandRules.ignore`), which `HandAt.ignored` keeps every card's path off, `?` and `!` paths and Charge's included; Target stores the tile in `run.targeted`, and `handTurn` first takes `targetTurn`, a step along `planTarget` (`cards.ts`: the plain search round ignored tiles, the last step onto whatever stands there) until the hero arrives or the tile goes out of reach ("Target lost"); it follows the path it committed to (`Game.targetPath`, never saved, dropped with the hand's plan and on Ignore) and plans again only when `stillOpen` finds it can't be followed: the path no longer starts at the hero, a tile on the way is no longer crossable or is ignored (a Greater Boss can appear on it), or the target tile became a wall.
The renderer marks both (`drawChargeMarks`); `tests/run-charges.test.ts` covers them.
`Game.autoTurn` runs it inside a run (`handTurn`: follow the committed `cardPlan` a step at a time, forced, so a lethal fight is taken; plan again once it ends or a step is refused; the first step of a new plan onto empty floor **dashes**, `dashOn`, carrying on along the path in the same turn across up to `game.dashFor(card)` more empty tiles (nothing on them, or a chest already opened, and no torch or Gem; `dashable`), and, with a tile of it left, onto the card's target when that is an item (`RUSH_ONTO` in `state.ts`: key, potion, ATK, DEF, treasure, reward) and Skip doesn't roll for it (`rollsSkip`), its step scaled as a card's last step is (`CardStep.scale`, the last argument of `move`, which says how the hand's turn takes a step) and the dash's undo snapshot no longer `quiet`, the card then activating, the later steps untracked so the dash is one undo, and sets `game.dash`, the tiles dashed off (each with the step taken off it) and, unless it ended on its target item, the empty tile it landed on (`end`, with the step onto it, echoed last), which the renderer snaps the hero past and `dash-echoes.ts` draws as fading echoes of the hero over 0.5 s (`DashEchoes` keeps every dash still echoing, so a dash soon after another leaves the first one's echoes to play out, and drops them on another board); `dashFor` adds up `dashTiles` (the Dash research's `dashTiles` target once the Courage skill Dash is owned, plus gear), on STAIRS the End Rush research's `endRushTiles` once the End Rush skill is owned (toward the stairs, or the Delve's climb), and the card's Dash badge, all read when used; a dashed tile skips Regen (`move`'s `regen`) but for the first `game.dashRegenSteps` (the Dash Regen research's `dashRegenSteps` target once the Courage skill Dash Regen is owned, read when used), the step onto the target counted with them; `tests/dash.test.ts` covers it), and Automove only in the forest.
`game.auto` is the hand's play/pause inside a run: on when a run starts or enters, and off after undo (which drops the path; a plain pause keeps it, and the card that led glowing, so playing on follows it) (and from the start in Dev mode, or when a page sets `globalThis.__handStartsPaused`, as the browser UI suite does).
When no card can act, `handStuck` is set and the hand pauses, the HUD shows End Run in red at the foot of the height column as well as in the run's menu, and the run ends only when the player ends it; each player action inside a run (run training, Ignore, and skills to come, through `afterPlayerAction`) checks the hand again and sets it playing once a card can act.
`explainStuck` (`cards.ts`, through `Game.stuckReport`) says why, for the run's end dialog: walking the same ground as the hand's search, it reports each **blocker**, a door, monster, stairs up or ignored tile at the edge of that ground with more ground beyond it (the stairs always count), closest first, with its reason (`BlockReason`: Ignore marked it, a badge holds back or passes over the card that would take it, the keys held don't open the door or run out partway through its door run, the hero can't hurt the monster, or no card in the hand takes it).
`walkAll` walks every tile but walls from a tile, stopping once it has reached the tiles asked for (Stairward's walk to the stairs, and the stuck hand's walk to the nearest ?), since in the Delve it would otherwise go on through each milestone gate.
`tests/cards.test.ts` covers both.
The player can't step or walk inside a run unless Dev mode is on (`game.manualMoves`).
`tests/cards.test.ts` drives `planHand` on hand-built boards, `tests/key-siphon.test.ts` and `tests/inspiration-cards.test.ts` the cards that act in place and the cards below Key Siphon, and `tests/game-commands.test.ts` the hand's turns.
`isDeadlocked` (`analysis.ts`), which searches the Tower floor the hero stands on, read from the climb, for anything the player could still do (reachable stairs always count, since the floor above is always new), no longer ends runs; `tests/tower-automation.test.ts` still records it.
`tests/tower-automation.test.ts` records the step Automove picks and the deadlock call on every step of seeded runs against `tests/fixtures/tower-automation.golden.json` (regenerate with `UPDATE_GOLDEN=1` only for an intended automation change), and `tests/tower-overhaul.test.ts` pins the deadlock rules on hand-built floors.

The paths go round lit torches through `litTorches` (`board.ts`), which the Delve `World` answers from a cached set, cleared when a torch goes out or its areas change, as its torch list is.
`npm run hand:bench -- [seeds] [turns]` (`tools/hand-bench.ts`) times a fresh `planHand`, each hand turn and a Target walk over seeded Delve runs: compare its numbers before and after a change to the hand's pathing, on the same machine.

**FLYING ENEMY** (Tower V's floor 10 Goal, `GOAL_CARDS`) is planned apart (`flyerPlan` in `cards.ts`, from `HandAt.shotReach`: the Archer's Attack Range and the wrap, which `HandRules` reads from `Game.shotReach`, absent unless the hand holds ARCHER inside a run): with a Flying enemy its arrows can hurt within reach, a plan with no path, which `handTurn` plays as `holdTurn` (a quiet undo snapshot and the turn's volley, no step, no Regen, no activation); otherwise the plain search's way to the closest such flyer, cut at the first tile with one in reach, its last step activating nothing and never rolling Skip.
While held its `CardBonus` (`shot`, 5% each) adds Shot ATK and Attack Speed, which `handBonus` sums and `volleyTurn` multiplies into each arrow's boost and the Archer's speed (`VolleyTurn.handShot`, from `Game.handBonus.shot`).
**ARCHER** (Tower III's floor 10 Goal, `archerCard` in `GOAL_CARDS`) never moves the hero (`WANTS.archer` is `never`): while the hand holds it (`shootsWith`, read by `Game.isArcher`; in the forest, the next run's hand) the hero is an Archer, so `endTurn` looses its arrows, `shotReach` is given, the HUD names the class (`heroClass`), and the shot Training rows open to Silver and Free Training in the run (`runTrainingOpen` in `loadout.ts`).
Its `CardBonus.boss` (5%) is summed into `handBonus(...).shot.boss`, which `loose` adds to the Shot ATK of an arrow at a boss or Greater Boss only.
A save that claimed the floor before the card existed (the old Archer class) finds the card in its deck and not its hand, since `deckCards` reads the claim.

The `add-badge` skill lists the files of the badge mechanism: update it when they move.
