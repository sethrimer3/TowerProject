# The hand, badges, charges and automation

The planners, Tower automation, the hand of cards, pressing a card, Auto-Climb, card badges, Ignore/Target charges, Dash, play/pause, and deadlock.

The planners (`routeTo`, Tower automation's search and Delve Automove) work from a `Position` (`board.ts`: a board and the run standing on it), never the whole `Game`.
`chooseStep(game)` in `automation.ts` is the one entry that reads the rest (the mode and Delve Automove's memory, plan and upgrades), and `tests/planners.test.ts` drives the planners on hand-built boards.
Tower automation (`automation.ts`) runs a bounded breadth-first search (`Search`) with separate destination scoring.
It stops searching at interactions and replans every step rather than assuming future keys or health, never takes lethal fights, and scores progress against the run record so it can backtrack without oscillating.
Inside a run (Tower or Delve, not the forest) the player's **hand** moves the hero: `cards/cards.ts` holds the cards (`CARDS`, each with its name, its text and, for some, a `bonus`, the new profile's `BASE_HAND`, STAIRS alone, in a hand of `BASE_HAND_SLOTS` (four), more with Larger Hand and Gems (`handSlots`), saved as `save.hand`; the upgrades' cards, `upgradeCards` (a row's `card`, or Door & Key's two `cards`), and `dealCard`, which deals a `toHand` upgrade's card (`dealsToHand`: the first skills Door & Key and Enemy) into the hand after the last card before it in `FIRST_CARDS` (STAIRS, DOOR, KEY, ENEMY) while there is room, called by `SkillDesk.buy`; and `moveCard`, the Deck's reorder) and `planHand`, a pure breadth-first search from the hero over crossable tiles, the whole ground the hero can walk with no row limit (only `MOST_WALKED`, 20,000 tiles, more than a Tower floor or a Delve area holds, so a search can't run on up the endless Delve) (floor, one-way gates and every item; walls, doors, monsters and stairs only as targets) (of equally short paths, the one stepping on the fewest `takesSomething` tiles, so the hand walks round items and lit torches unless they lie on the only shortest way; Charge's search doesn't) that returns the first card in priority order that can act: one that acts in place (`IN_PLACE`: GK SIPHON, asked through the `HandAt`'s `canAct`, which `HandRules` answers; such a plan has no path, and `handTurn` runs it as a turn with no step: `CardActivation.siphon` (`game/card-activation.ts`, the game's `activation`) trades the run's top Max HP training levels, `siphonCost` of them (one more each use: `run.siphoned` counts the uses), out of `siphonLevel`, the hero's ranks plus those bought with Silver less those siphoned, for a gold key; without enough left the HUD greys the card, `unable`), or one with a reachable target on the current floor only (never through stairs) and the shortest path to its closest one (the Delve's STAIRS card, whose text, `cardText`, says so only once Breakthrough is owned, climbs to the highest crossable tile it can reach instead, however far from the view, only above the highest row the run has reached, `run.top`, so it never leads back to a row stood on before).
**Card bonuses** (`CardBonus`, read with `cardBonus`) are what a card gives for as long as the run's hand holds it, whichever card leads the hero there: GOLD, BLUE and RED KEY make each key of their colour picked up worth 5% more and pay 1, 2 or 3 more Pickup Gold, and CHEST gives each treasure chest a 1.5% chance of being a Supply Chest and 0.5% of being an Equipment Chest, fixed as the run goes inside and its floors are generated (see progression.md); KEY, heading for a key of any colour, gives none.
Each enemy card but ENEMY (`foe`: WEAK, BASE, STRONG, ELITE, BOSS, NON-BOSS, FAST and FLYING ENEMY, each covering the enemies `FOE_CARDS` in `cards/foe-cards.ts` names, BOSS ENEMY the Greater Boss too) gives `FOE_ATTACK` (5%) more ATK against them and `FOE_GOLD` (5%) more kill Gold from them: `dealHand` puts the hand's ATK percents on the hero as `Player.foeAttack` (by card), which `attackAgainst` (`combat.ts`) adds into one card factor (with an arrow's Shot ATK, `cards`) multiplying the equipment's boss ATK, so fights, arrows, predictions and the planners all count it; `RunPurse.killGold` multiplies a kill's Gold by `foeGold` (5% a covering card in `run.hand`, the cards' percents added into one factor, ×1.1 for two, after Gold Bonus and Mug, multiplying the rest).
GOLD, HEART and WOODEN DOOR (`door`, `DOOR_CARD_DISCOUNT`, 5%) make their doors cost that much less (`DoorDiscounts` in `doors.ts`, by `DoorKind`, read with `doorDiscount`): a single gold door's key (`isYellowDoor`, a Gold Heart Door included), the HP a door draining HP takes (`drainsHp`), and a Wooden Door's key and the HP breaking it takes; DOOR gives none.
`handBonus(hand)` adds them up (`Game.handBonus`, none in the forest; `foe` by card): `Game.stepRules` passes the key percents as `StepRules.keyFind`, which `resolveStep` multiplies a key by (after a badge's scale), and the door percents as `StepRules.doorOff` (`Game.doorOff`), which `resolveStep` multiplies a door's key (with Key Efficiency's `keyCost` and the badge's scale) and its HP toll (with the badge's scale and, for a Heart Door, Heart Door Resilience's `heartToll`) by, each its own factor; `HandRules.doorOff` gives the planners the same (`HandAt.doorOff`: `keyScale` and `tollScale` in `cards/cards.ts`, which `paysRun`, Charge's `chargeable` and `explainStuck` read), so a door card heads for a door the cheaper keys pay for.
`Finds.pickup` adds the key Gold to the key's Pickup Gold, and `Game.dealHand` fixes the chest odds as the run's `chestOdds`, which its boards mark each chest's kind by.
`cardBonusLines` words them for the card's details (`ui/card-reveal.ts`, the chest's chances light blue and purple) and `cardBonusText` for the hand's and Deck's tooltips; `tests/card-bonus.test.ts` covers them.
A run copies `save.hand` into `run.hand` when it goes inside (`game.hand` reads the run's, or in the forest the saved one), so reordering never reaches a run already inside; `deck.arrange` (`DeckEditor`, `game/deck-editor.ts`, the game's `deck`) reorders `save.hand` only in the forest, once Combat Stance is owned, and `deck.add` and `deck.remove` move the deck's cards (`deckCards(upgrades)`: the base hand's, and each card an upgrade row names with `card`, `upgradeCard`) in and out of it once Buildout is owned; STAIRS never leaves.
**The Manual hand** (the Breakthrough skill Manual Hand, after Card Mastery) is a second saved hand, `save.manualHand`, `manualSlots(save)` slots (one, and `save.manualSlots` bought with Gems on `HAND_SLOT_GEMS`, `nextManualSlotGems`; `MAX_MANUAL_SLOTS`), never STAIRS and never a card `save.hand` holds (the decoder drops both, and all of it without the skill).
`DeckEditor` takes a `HandKind` ("auto" | "manual") for `add`, `place`, `arrange`, `slots`, `full`, `slotGems` and `buySlot`; `remove` takes a card from whichever hand holds it (`handOf`), and `switch` moves one to the end of the other hand while it has room (`canSwitch`; never STAIRS).
`dealHand` copies it into `run.manual` (dropped when empty), and `Game.held` is `[...hand, ...manualHand]` (`heldCards(run)` in `runs.ts` for code with only the run): the hand's slots first, so a Manual card's slot comes after them.
`planHand` for the hand's own play (`handTurn`, `awaitCard`, `afterPlayerAction`, `explainStuck`) still takes `game.hand`, so Manual cards never play by themselves; a press (`Game.focus(slot)`, the slot in `held`) and the pressed card's plan (`nextHandPlan`) plan over `held`, and the plan's card slot is read from `held` (`handTurn`, `dropGatedPlan`, `CardActivation.activate`), so a Manual card is pressed and acts exactly as a hand card does.
**GEM** (the Breakthrough skill Gem Card, `gemCard`, under Offline Gold) heads for a Gem lying out (`WANTS.gem`: `HandAt.gems`, the tiles `HandRules.gems` reads from `GemFinder.gems`, since a Gem is a mark over a plain floor tile, not a tile kind).
`CardActivation.activate` gives it no activation XP: `GemFinder.pay` gives it XP for each Gem collected while `Game.held` holds it (`GEM_VALUE`, raised by Gem XP), through `Game.gainCardXp`.
A Skip badge never vanishes a Gem (`handTurn`), and the last step's Effective or Dampen scale reaches `GemFinder.step(scale)`, which pays the Gem times it (fractions kept in `gemDrop.fraction`).
Its powers (`CARD_POWERS.gem`, the `GemStat`s in `cards/powers.ts`) work where their stats are read: inside a run from the run's levels (`Game.powerBonus`: Gem Chance, Golden Gem, Stellar Gem Chance, Gems in Chests, Gem XP), and for the Gem button from the save's while either hand holds the card (`cards/gem-card.ts`: `gemCardBonus`, `adTerms`).
`tests/gem-card.test.ts` covers it.
Everything held counts the Manual hand's cards too: `handBonus`, `isArcher`, the run's badges, trees, chest odds and foe ATK (`dealHand`), `RunPurse`'s foe Gold, `runTrainingOpen`'s Archer rows and `passiveCardXp`; Goldback pays only on the hand's last card, Card Groups link the hand's cards only.
`tests/manual-hand.test.ts` covers it.
**Card Groups** (Tower II's floor 90 Goal, `goalUnlocked(save, "cardGroups")`) link cards side by side in the hand: `save.cardLinks` holds each link as the pair of cards it joins (`CardLink`, left then right), `deck.link(i)` (`DeckEditor`, in the forest, `canLink`) toggles the link after slot `i` (`toggleLink`), and every change to the hand (`arrange`, `place`, `remove`, through `DeckEditor.setHand`, and the decoder) keeps only the links still joining neighbours (`keptLinks`), so a card moved out of place breaks its links.
A run copies them into `run.links` with its hand (`dealHand`), and `HandRules.links` (`linkedToNext`: for each card, whether it is linked to the next) is the `HandAt.links` `planHand` reads: it plans each card as before (`planCard`) but takes the shortest plan of a run of linked cards (a card acting in place, with no path, before all; ties to the earlier card), going on past the group only when none of its cards can act; a pressed card (`only`) plans alone.
With Manual Hand, the Deck page's hand heading carries an Auto | Manual slider (`kindsHtml`, `.hand-kinds`, the page's `kind`, back to Auto as the page is left), and the slots, the slot for sale, drags and the deck's adds work on the hand it shows; each hand card but STAIRS wears a switch (`.deck-switch`) beside its X, disabled while the other hand is full.
Inside a run with a Manual hand, `RunTrainingBar` shows a Manual tab (`[data-run-tab="manual"]`) beside the hand's, which `main.ts` passes to `renderHud` (`manual`) so `renderHand` draws `game.manualHand` at slots from `hand.length`; the hand's tab is named Auto, `DECK_ICON` wearing `.auto-arrow`, once Auto-Climb is owned or the run holds a Manual hand.
The bar shows inside a run with On the Job, or with a Manual hand (its group buttons only with On the Job).
The Deck page (`linksHtml` in `ui/deck-page.ts`, `.with-links`) draws a diamond toggle after each hand card but the last, in a row gap under each row of the grid, and each link as two half brackets, one under each card, so a group wrapping to the next row reads across it.
`tests/card-groups.test.ts` covers it.
**The hand rules** (`cards/hand-rules.ts`, `HandRules`, the game's `handRules`) are what the hand's cards may do now, kept apart from `Game`'s turn: each card's `CardRules` from its badges, Deprioritize's marks and the Stairward and Skip Open Nodes cooldowns by floor, Skip's stream of rolls, the tiles Ignore marked, Card Groups' links, and whether a card that acts in place can act (`canAct`, `cardUses`, `siphonLevel`, `siphonCost`, `tradeCost`, `smeltCost`, `smeltPercent`, `smeltHeal`).
The cards that act in place (`IN_PLACE` in `cards/cards.ts`) are the siphons (`SIPHONS`), the traders (`TRADERS`: GK -> BK and BK -> RK, three keys of one colour for one of the next) and the smelts (`SMELTS`: Smelt GK, BK and RK, a key for a percent of max HP up to the Overheal ceiling, only while the whole heal fits); `CardActivation.actInPlace` (`game/card-activation.ts`) plays each from its table.
The card ids keep their first names (`keyToHp` is Smelt GK, `blueTrader` GK -> BK), so saved decks and skills stay valid.
It is the `HandAt` the hand plans from (`planHand`, `planTarget`, `explainStuck` and `walkAll` take it), reads the save, the run, its board, the mode, the hand and Key Efficiency's cost from a narrow host (`HandHost`) each time, and keeps everything it changes in the run, so reloads keep it.
`Game`'s turn (`handTurn`, the dash, Target's walk, a pressed card and the payouts) asks it and applies the effects; the HUD and the renderer read its marks and greying from it.
`tests/hand-rules.test.ts` drives it on hand-built boards through a plain-object host, with no `Game`.
**Card badges** (`cards/badges.ts`, opened by the Breakthrough skill **Badges**) are tokens attached to cards: `BADGES` holds each one's rarity, kind (`reward`, `gate`, `aim`, `scale` or `chance`), symbol and values by level, levels come from copies (`COPIES_FOR_LEVEL`: the first copy opens level 1 and counts toward level 2; 80 reach level 7), and `drawBadges` draws by `RARITY_WEIGHTS` among the rarities with a badge left below level 7 (maxed ones leave the pool, and a badge with an `unlock` joins it only once that upgrade is owned: the key gates wait for Key < Badges and Key ≥ Badges), continuing the stream saved in `save.badges.rng` (seeded once from `stream("badges")`), so a purchase can't be redrawn by reloading.
`save.badges` also holds the copies and a gate's chosen threshold (`pick`) per badge, and `cards`, the badges each card holds, top slot first (a badge on one card at most).
A card has one slot, and one more for each level of the Archives' **Badge Slots** research (`researched(…, "badgeSlots")`, `BadgeDesk.slots`; opened by Tower VI's floor 50 Goal): `attachBadge` fills the next free slot or, every slot full, replaces the bottom one, which swaps into the dropped badge's old slot (or comes off).
Badges that can't work together never share a card (`badgeConflict`, the pairs in `CONFLICTS`: a key colour's `<` and `≥` gates, Effective with Dampen; gates of different things, HP < with GK ≥, may share one): `attachBadge` refuses one, `attachConflict` (`BadgeDesk.conflict`) names the badge in the way so the Deck page can say why in a dialog, and both decoders keep only the first of such a pair.
The Deck page's `BadgeDesk` (`game/badge-desk.ts`, the game's `badges`) draws for Gems (`DRAW_GEMS`), attaches, detaches and picks, in the forest only; a run copies its hand's into `run.badges` as it goes inside (`runBadges`, a list per card, read with `badgesOn` and `badgeOn`).
A gate badge is checked again on every turn of a committed path (`HandRules.gateShut`, unless the card is the pressed one): if it no longer holds (HP rose past HP <, say, from Regen), the path is dropped and the hand chooses a card again.
The hand card's tooltip names each of its badges on a line of its own, top slot first, with the value in effect (`badgeTitle` in `ui/hud.ts`: "HP < 100%"), not its level; the card wears their icons down its top right (`.hand-badges`), and on the Deck page their marks down its top right under the X (`.badge-marks`), its level standing at the top left.
Inside a run `HandRules.cardRules` turns each card's badges into one `CardRules` (each badge's rules merged, `closed` if any closes it) `planHand` takes through `HandAt.cardRules`: a gate's `closed` skips the card (unless it is the pressed card, planned with `only`), Stairward's `stairward` picks the target nearest the stairs by walk (the highest in the Delve), Skip Open Nodes' `skipOpen` passes over a door or monster whose open neighbours the hero can all reach already (reporting each into `run.skipped`, which the renderer marks with an X for that floor), and Charge's `charge` searches through up to that many fightable monsters and doors the hero holds keys for (`chargeSearch`).
Stairward and Skip Open Nodes cool down by floor (`run.badgeFloors`, the floor each last worked on, by `HandRules.floor`: the Tower floor, or the Delve's equivalent floor where the hero stands).
**Deprioritize**'s `deprioritize` passes over the target the card would pick, marking it ? (`run.marks`, by `HandRules.floor`, cleared as its tile is stepped on), and the cards after it plan round it; `HandAt.marked` (the ? tiles, `HandRules.marked`) keeps every card's search off them, and once its level's marks are made the card rests `closed` for the floor (`HandRules.cardResting`, which the HUD greys as `unable`).
When no card can act, `handTurn` turns the ? nearest by walk into ! (`raiseBang`) and the card's `bang` heads for it by any path; the renderer draws both marks (`drawBadgeMarks`).
**Effective** and **Dampen** (`scale`) multiply the card's target's effect when it activates: `handTurn` resolves the card's last step with `StepRules.scale` (`scaleOf`: 1.05 … 1.35, or 0.95 … 0.65), so `resolveStep` scales a potion's heal, a key (keys held are fractional, `snap`ped, shown by `keyCount` in `whole.ts` to two decimals when not whole), a shard, each key a door takes (`doorCost(tile, player, scale)` needs that much of each colour held; DOOR's `CardRules.scale` makes the card want only doors it can pay; the door cards want a door run only while the hero can pay for every door of it, `paysRun`), a Heart Door's toll (only ever smaller) and a fight's damage, which the fight plays out as predicted and adds (`StepEffect.extraDamage`, which can fell the hero; given back as a heal below 0) when it settles, with its Silver, Gold and XP (`StepEffect.scale`, through `winFight` and `RunPurse`); GK SIPHON's key too.
**Skip** (`chance`) rolls on the card's last step, drawing the next number of its own stream, started from the run seed (`HandRules.skipHits`, spent by `skipRolled`; `run.skipRolls` counts those drawn, counted once the step is taken, so a run of the same seed played the same way skips the same targets): the target vanishes without effect (`vanish`, a turn of its own, with `render/effects/poof.ts`'s puff at `game.vanished`), never a boss or Greater Boss; on the Tower's stairs `advanceTowerRoom` climbs two floors (the step's `CardStep.floorSkip`; the floor climbed past pays no Spare Change or Wishing Well, and an area climbed past is still judged) with *Floor Skipped*.
It has no limit a floor.
**More Enemies** (`floor`, the one badge that changes the floors themselves, `enemies/more-enemies.ts`) sits only on a monster card (`BadgeDef.cards`, `fitsCard`: `attachBadge` refuses another card, a swap that would carry it onto one takes it off, and both decoders drop it there); `extraEnemies(run.badges)` sums each strength's percent over the cards covering it (MONSTER every strength, each from its own count; the Greater Boss never), and the boards add the extras as a last stage before `tierCells`: `RoomWorld` (its `extras`, through `withExtraEnemies`) and the Delve `World` (its second argument, through `delveExtras`, cached per area, each of the area's ten equivalent floors by tile depth counting and placing its own, bottom first, on one `extraGround` for the area: the tiles as a grid of indices with their neighbours and the shortest walk worked out once; the area's torches come from the cache `World.torches` reads too, so they are placed once).
`addEnemies` adds each strength's generated count times its percent, the fraction rolled as a chance of one more, each a copy of one of the floor's own of that strength, placed by `nextSpot`: corners of reward rooms behind a gate (a door or enemy), corners of other rooms behind one, corners and then edges of the core, any tile that cuts nothing off (Tarjan's articulation points, extras already placed counting as walls), and only then one that does, off the shortest walk from the way in to the target (the stairs, or the area's boss) first; never the way in or beside it, beside the stairs, near the milestone gate or on a torch.
All its rolls come from a stream seeded by the run seed and floor (`extrasSeed`), so a floor comes out the same whenever it is rebuilt; runs without it generate exactly as before, so no layout version moves.
`Game.newRun` rebuilds the board after `dealHand` so a run going straight inside gets its extras.
`tests/more-enemies.test.ts` covers it.
**Dash** (`aim`, rare, opened by the Breakthrough skill Dash Badge) adds its value (1 to 7) to the tiles its card's first step toward a new target may dash across (`dashFor`), on top of Dash and End Rush; it needs no run state, since a dash is one turn.
`handTurn` activates a card when its path's last step lands (after its fight settles, wrapping the encounter's `settle`) or an in-place card acts (`activation.actInPlace`): `activation.activate` (`CardActivation`, `game/card-activation.ts`) pays the rewards, HP and Silver and XP through the run and Gold through `RunPurse.badgeGold`, gated per target in `lootedTiles`.
**Card levels** (`cards/levels.ts`): each card's XP, `save.cardXp` (by card, none left out), raises its level by `CARD_LEVEL_XP` (60 levels, `MAX_CARD_LEVEL`; 3,541,500 XP in all, `MAX_CARD_XP`, past which a card gains none), read with `cardLevel(xp, cap)` (the level, no higher than the card's max level `cap`; the XP `into` the next, which keeps gathering past the cap; what it costs, `needed`; and `capped`).
A card's rarity is its level's (`rarityAt` in `cards/rarity.ts`: `CardRarity`, base at 0, then common through mythic, `LEVELS_PER_RARITY` (10) each; `firstLevelOf`), and its max level is its rarity's last (`cardMaxLevel(save.cardAscension, card)`: 10 × (1 + its ascensions)); `levelIn(save, card)` reads a card's level under it, `rarityIn` its rarity, and `totalCardLevels` every card's, for the Deck tab's dot.
**Ascension**: `save.cardAscension` (`CardAscensions`, by card, 1 to `MAX_ASCENSION` (5), none left out; `decodeCardAscension`) counts each card's; `readyToAscend` is a card at its max level, short of 60, already holding the next level's XP; `nextAscension` names the rarity it climbs to, the Breakthrough skill that lets it (`ASCENSION_SKILLS`: `ascendUncommon` … `ascendMythic`, so a new game's cards stop at level 10) and its price, `ASCEND_RUBIES` (10, 100, 1,000, 10,000, 100,000) and `ASCEND_GEMS` (100 times the ascension's number: 100 to 500); `ascendCard` raises the count and returns the levels reached at once (lowest first), paying nothing.
`CardDesk` (`game/card-desk.ts`, the game's `cards`) is the Deck page's API: `next`, `ready`, `unlocked` (that skill owned), `refusal` (`notReady`, `locked` without the skill, `short` of Rubies, or `shortGems`; nothing to pay with free purchases) and `ascend`, which pays `save.rubies` and `save.gems` and hands the levels to `Game.cardLevelled`.
**Rubies** (`save.rubies`, `earn(…, "rubies")`): `RunPurse.enemyLoot` banks a boss's or Greater Boss's (`bossRubies`: the tier's number, `run.tier`, in either mode, and BOSS ENEMY's Boss Rubies more, its fraction a chance by `tileRandom` on the run seed with `BOSS_RUBY_SALT`, inside the kill's loot gate) and `Finds.kill` raises them (`rubiesText`, a `{ ruby: true }` gain that `board-popups.ts` draws with the `ruby` kit sprite or `paintRuby`); `Game.findFloorRubies` pays STAIRS' Ruby Chance on each floor new to the run (`advanceTowerRoom`, `payDelveFloors`, `tileRandom` with `FLOOR_RUBY_SALT`) through `RunPurse.bankRubies`; the ad prize `{ kind: "rubies", amount: 10 }` (`ads/series.ts`, `AdDesk.claim`) gives 10.
**Card powers** (`cards/powers.ts`): `CARD_POWERS` gives each card with powers a base power and one a rarity, common to mythic (ARCHER's mythic two), each a `CardPower` (`rarity`, `name`, the `PowerStat`s each level raises); a card left out (the siphons, Angel Wings, Flying Enemy) has none yet and still levels and ascends.
`powerLevel(power, level)` is a base power's every level and a rarity's power's levels within its band, up to ten; `powerBonus(levels)` sums each stat over the cards at `levels` (`CardLevels`, card → level), `powerAmounts` a power's at some levels, and a percent stat is a card factor (`powerFactor`, the cards' percents added), the `FLAT_STATS` added as they are.
A run copies its held cards' levels as it goes inside (`levelsOf(save, held)` in `dealHand`, `run.handLevels`), and `Game.cardLevelled` (from `gainCardXp` and the desk's `ascend`) raises a held card's there inside a run and sets the hero's powers by enemy again (`applyHandPowers`: `Player.foeAttack` and the rest, `cardParry`, `stealOverheal`, `takedown`, `tangle`), so a power counts from the moment it rises; what shapes the floors stays as the run went in (`run.doorOdds`, `run.treasureFloor`, `run.percentPotions`, set in `dealHand`), and Max HP's factor is applied once there.
`Game.powerBonus` and `RunPurse`'s read `run.handLevels` (none in the forest); `HandRules.inPlaceTree` reads the save's levels in the forest (`levelsOf(save, CARD_IDS)`); `gemCardBonus` reads GEM's while either hand holds it.
The new stats: `floorRuby` (above), `bossRubies` (above), `levelSkipBoost` (`boostedSkip` in `enemies/level-skips.ts`: both Level Skip chances raised by its percent in `Game.rollLevelSkips`, up to `LEVEL_SKIP_BOOST_CAP`, 80%, a chance past it kept), `torchGold`/`torchSilver` (`Game.putOutTorch`: Gold added to the torch's Pickup Gold before the bonuses, Silver through `RunPurse.silverAt`) and `yellowToWood` (`DoorOdds.toWood`: `withDoorOdds` makes a Gold Door, a gold key alone, a Wooden Door whose `gold` is the Gold Door's `doorGold`, by the tile's fixed number with `TO_WOOD_SALT`); GEM's `gemBank` is half a reward a level, its whole part on the bank's cap and its fraction (`adTerms`' `part`) a chance rolled on `stream("gemBank")` at each claim (`GemFinder.claimAd`) and kept as `gemDrop.adBonusSlot`, which raises the next refill's cap by one.
Stats several cards share (`goldBonus`, `freeUtilityChance`, …) add into one card factor in `powerBonus`.
Many `PowerStat`s no power raises now (Spawn Rate, Regen, Spare Change, the color keys' heals and Silver, the traders' Free Utility, …) keep their mechanics for later powers.
GOLD, BLUE and RED KEY's stats are `${colour}${part}` (`ColorKeyStat`, `COLOR_KEY_PARTS`, read with `keyStat`).

**FLYING ENEMY** (Tower V's floor 10 Goal, `GOAL_CARDS`) is planned apart (`flyerPlan` in `cards/cards.ts`, from `HandAt.shotReach`: the Archer's Attack Range and the wrap, which `HandRules` reads from `Game.shotReach`, absent unless the hand holds ARCHER inside a run): with a Flying enemy its arrows can hurt within reach, a plan with no path, which `handTurn` plays as `holdTurn` (the turn's volley, no step, no Regen, no activation); otherwise the plain search's way to the closest such flyer, cut at the first tile with one in reach, its last step activating nothing and never rolling Skip.
While held its `CardBonus` (`shot`, 5% each) adds Shot ATK and Attack Speed, which `handBonus` sums and `volleyTurn` multiplies into each arrow's boost and the Archer's speed (`VolleyTurn.handShot`, from `Game.handBonus.shot`).
**ARCHER** (Tower III's floor 10 Goal, `archerCard` in `GOAL_CARDS`) never moves the hero (`WANTS.archer` is `never`): while the hand holds it (`shootsWith`, read by `Game.isArcher`; in the forest, the next run's hand) the hero is an Archer, so `endTurn` looses its arrows, `shotReach` is given, the HUD names the class (`heroClass`), and the shot Training rows open to Silver and Free Training in the run (`runTrainingOpen` in `progression/loadout.ts`).
Its `CardBonus.boss` (5%) is summed into `handBonus(...).shot.boss`, which `arrows` (volley.ts) adds to the Shot ATK of an arrow at a boss or Greater Boss only.
