# The hand, badges, charges and automation

The planners, Tower automation, the hand of cards, card badges, Focus/Ignore/Target charges, Dash, play/pause, and deadlock.

The planners (`routeTo`, Tower automation's search and Delve Automove) work from a `Position` (`board.ts`: a board and the run standing on it), never the whole `Game`.
`chooseStep(game)` in `automation.ts` is the one entry that reads the rest (the mode and Delve Automove's memory, plan and upgrades), and `tests/planners.test.ts` drives the planners on hand-built boards.
Tower automation (`automation.ts`) runs a bounded breadth-first search (`Search`) with separate destination scoring.
It stops searching at interactions and replans every step rather than assuming future keys or health, never takes lethal fights, and scores progress against the run record so it can backtrack without oscillating.
Inside a run (Tower or Delve, not the forest) the player's **hand** moves the hero: `cards/cards.ts` holds the cards (`CARDS`, each with its name, its text and, for some, a `bonus`, the new profile's `BASE_HAND` (STAIRS, DOOR, KEY, ENEMY) filling the hand's `BASE_HAND_SLOTS`, more with Larger Hand and Gems (`handSlots`), saved as `save.hand`, and `moveCard`, the Deck's reorder) and `planHand`, a pure breadth-first search from the hero over crossable tiles, the whole ground the hero can walk with no row limit (only `MOST_WALKED`, 20,000 tiles, more than a Tower floor or a Delve area holds, so a search can't run on up the endless Delve) (floor, one-way gates and every item; walls, doors, monsters and stairs only as targets) (of equally short paths, the one stepping on the fewest `takesSomething` tiles, so the hand walks round items and lit torches unless they lie on the only shortest way; Charge's search doesn't) that returns the first card in priority order that can act: one that acts in place (`IN_PLACE`: GK SIPHON, asked through the `HandAt`'s `canAct`, which `HandRules` answers; such a plan has no path, and `handTurn` runs it as a turn with no step: `CardActivation.siphon` (`game/card-activation.ts`, the game's `activation`) trades the run's top Max HP training levels, `siphonCost` of them (one more each use: `run.siphoned` counts the uses), out of `siphonLevel`, the hero's ranks plus those bought with Silver less those siphoned, for a gold key, after an undo snapshot; without enough left the HUD greys the card, `unable`), or one with a reachable target on the current floor only (never through stairs) and the shortest path to its closest one (the Delve's STAIRS card, whose text, `cardText`, says so only once Into the depths is owned, climbs to the highest crossable tile it can reach instead, however far from the view, only above the highest row the run has reached, `run.top`, so it never leads back to a row stood on before).
**Card bonuses** (`CardBonus`, read with `cardBonus`) are what a card gives for as long as the run's hand holds it, whichever card leads the hero there: GOLD, BLUE and RED KEY make each key of their colour picked up worth 5% more and pay 1, 2 or 3 more Pickup Gold, and CHEST gives each treasure chest a 1.5% chance of being a Supply Chest and 0.5% of being an Equipment Chest, fixed as the run goes inside and its floors are generated (see progression.md); KEY, heading for a key of any colour, gives none.
Each enemy card but ENEMY (`foe`: WEAK, BASE, STRONG, ELITE, BOSS, NON-BOSS, FAST and FLYING ENEMY, each covering the enemies `FOE_CARDS` in `cards/foe-cards.ts` names, BOSS ENEMY the Greater Boss too) gives `FOE_ATTACK` (5%) more ATK against them and `FOE_GOLD` (5%) more kill Gold from them: `dealHand` puts the hand's ATK percents on the hero as `Player.foeAttack` (by card), which `attackAgainst` (`combat.ts`) adds into one card factor (with an arrow's Shot ATK, `cards`) multiplying the equipment's boss ATK, so fights, arrows, predictions and the planners all count it; `RunPurse.killGold` multiplies a kill's Gold by `foeGold` (5% a covering card in `run.hand`, the cards' percents added into one factor, ×1.1 for two, after Gold Bonus and Mug, multiplying the rest).
GOLD, HEART and WOODEN DOOR (`door`, `DOOR_CARD_DISCOUNT`, 5%) make their doors cost that much less (`DoorDiscounts` in `doors.ts`, by `DoorKind`, read with `doorDiscount`): a single gold door's key (`isYellowDoor`, a Gold Heart Door included), the HP a door draining HP takes (`drainsHp`), and a Wooden Door's key and the HP breaking it takes; DOOR gives none.
`handBonus(hand)` adds them up (`Game.handBonus`, none in the forest; `foe` by card): `Game.stepRules` passes the key percents as `StepRules.keyFind`, which `resolveStep` multiplies a key by (after a badge's scale), and the door percents as `StepRules.doorOff` (`Game.doorOff`), which `resolveStep` multiplies a door's key (with Key Efficiency's `keyCost` and the badge's scale) and its HP toll (with the badge's scale and, for a Heart Door, Heart Door Resilience's `heartToll`) by, each its own factor; `HandRules.doorOff` gives the planners the same (`HandAt.doorOff`: `keyScale` and `tollScale` in `cards/cards.ts`, which `paysRun`, Charge's `chargeable` and `explainStuck` read), so a door card heads for a door the cheaper keys pay for.
`Finds.pickup` adds the key Gold to the key's Pickup Gold, and `Game.dealHand` fixes the chest odds as the run's `chestOdds`, which its boards mark each chest's kind by.
`cardBonusLines` words them for the card's details (`ui/card-reveal.ts`, the chest's chances light blue and purple) and `cardBonusText` for the hand's and Deck's tooltips; `tests/card-bonus.test.ts` covers them.
A run copies `save.hand` into `run.hand` when it goes inside (`game.hand` reads the run's, or in the forest the saved one), so reordering never reaches a run already inside; `deck.arrange` (`DeckEditor`, `game/deck-editor.ts`, the game's `deck`) reorders `save.hand` only in the forest, once Combat Stance is owned, and `deck.add` and `deck.remove` move the deck's cards (`deckCards(upgrades)`: the base hand's, and each card an upgrade row names with `card`, `upgradeCard`) in and out of it once Buildout is owned; STAIRS never leaves.
**Card Groups** (Tower II's floor 90 Goal, `goalUnlocked(save, "cardGroups")`) link cards side by side in the hand: `save.cardLinks` holds each link as the pair of cards it joins (`CardLink`, left then right), `deck.link(i)` (`DeckEditor`, in the forest, `canLink`) toggles the link after slot `i` (`toggleLink`), and every change to the hand (`arrange`, `place`, `remove`, through `DeckEditor.setHand`, and the decoder) keeps only the links still joining neighbours (`keptLinks`), so a card moved out of place breaks its links.
A run copies them into `run.links` with its hand (`dealHand`), and `HandRules.links` (`linkedToNext`: for each card, whether it is linked to the next) is the `HandAt.links` `planHand` reads: it plans each card as before (`planCard`) but takes the shortest plan of a run of linked cards (a card acting in place, with no path, before all; ties to the earlier card), going on past the group only when none of its cards can act; a focused card (`only`) plans alone.
The Deck page (`linksHtml` in `ui/deck-page.ts`, `.with-links`) draws a diamond toggle after each hand card but the last, in a row gap under each row of the grid, and each link as two half brackets, one under each card, so a group wrapping to the next row reads across it.
`tests/card-groups.test.ts` covers it.
**The hand rules** (`cards/hand-rules.ts`, `HandRules`, the game's `handRules`) are what the hand's cards may do now, kept apart from `Game`'s turn: each card's `CardRules` from its badges, Deprioritize's marks and the Stairward and Skip Open Nodes cooldowns by floor, Skip's stream of rolls, the tiles Ignore marked, Card Groups' links, and whether a card that acts in place can act (`canAct`, `cardUses`, `siphonLevel`, `siphonCost`).
It is the `HandAt` the hand plans from (`planHand`, `planTarget`, `explainStuck` and `walkAll` take it), reads the save, the run, its board, the mode, the hand and Key Efficiency's cost from a narrow host (`HandHost`) each time, and keeps everything it changes in the run, so undo and reloads keep it.
`Game`'s turn (`handTurn`, the dash, Target's walk, Focus and the payouts) asks it and applies the effects; the HUD and the renderer read its marks and greying from it.
`tests/hand-rules.test.ts` drives it on hand-built boards through a plain-object host, with no `Game`.
**Card badges** (`cards/badges.ts`, opened by the Courage skill **Badges**) are tokens attached to cards: `BADGES` holds each one's rarity, kind (`reward`, `gate`, `aim`, `scale` or `chance`), symbol and values by level, levels come from copies (`COPIES_FOR_LEVEL`: the first copy opens level 1 and counts toward level 2; 80 reach level 7), and `drawBadges` draws by `RARITY_WEIGHTS` among the rarities with a badge left below level 7 (maxed ones leave the pool, and a badge with an `unlock` joins it only once that upgrade is owned: the key gates wait for Key < Badges and Key ≥ Badges), continuing the stream saved in `save.badges.rng` (seeded once from `stream("badges")`), so a purchase can't be redrawn by reloading.
`save.badges` also holds the copies and a gate's chosen threshold (`pick`) per badge, and `cards`, the badges each card holds, top slot first (a badge on one card at most).
A card has one slot, and one more for each level of the Archives' **Badge Slots** research (`researched(…, "badgeSlots")`, `BadgeDesk.slots`; opened by Tower VI's floor 50 Goal): `attachBadge` fills the next free slot or, every slot full, replaces the bottom one, which swaps into the dropped badge's old slot (or comes off).
Badges that can't work together never share a card (`badgeConflict`, the pairs in `CONFLICTS`: a key colour's `<` and `≥` gates, Effective with Dampen; gates of different things, HP < with GK ≥, may share one): `attachBadge` refuses one, `attachConflict` (`BadgeDesk.conflict`) names the badge in the way so the Deck page can say why in a dialog, and both decoders keep only the first of such a pair.
The Deck page's `BadgeDesk` (`game/badge-desk.ts`, the game's `badges`) draws for Gems (`DRAW_GEMS`), attaches, detaches and picks, in the forest only; a run copies its hand's into `run.badges` as it goes inside (`runBadges`, a list per card, read with `badgesOn` and `badgeOn`).
A gate badge is checked again on every turn of a committed path (`HandRules.gateShut`, unless the card is focused): if it no longer holds (HP rose past HP <, say, from Regen), the path is dropped and the hand chooses a card again.
The hand card's tooltip names each of its badges on a line of its own, top slot first, with the value in effect (`badgeTitle` in `ui/hud.ts`: "HP < 100%"), not its level; the card wears their icons down its top right (`.hand-badges`), and on the Deck page their marks down its top right under the X (`.badge-marks`), its level standing at the top left.
Inside a run `HandRules.cardRules` turns each card's badges into one `CardRules` (each badge's rules merged, `closed` if any closes it) `planHand` takes through `HandAt.cardRules`: a gate's `closed` skips the card (unless it is the focused card, planned with `only`), Stairward's `stairward` picks the target nearest the stairs by walk (the highest in the Delve), Skip Open Nodes' `skipOpen` passes over a door or monster whose open neighbours the hero can all reach already (reporting each into `run.skipped`, which the renderer marks with an X for that floor), and Charge's `charge` searches through up to that many fightable monsters and doors the hero holds keys for (`chargeSearch`).
Stairward and Skip Open Nodes cool down by floor (`run.badgeFloors`, the floor each last worked on, by `HandRules.floor`: the Tower floor, or the Delve's equivalent floor where the hero stands).
**Deprioritize**'s `deprioritize` passes over the target the card would pick, marking it ? (`run.marks`, by `HandRules.floor`, cleared as its tile is stepped on), and the cards after it plan round it; `HandAt.marked` (the ? tiles, `HandRules.marked`) keeps every card's search off them, and once its level's marks are made the card rests `closed` for the floor (`HandRules.cardResting`, which the HUD greys as `unable`).
When no card can act, `handTurn` turns the ? nearest by walk into ! (`raiseBang`) and the card's `bang` heads for it by any path; the renderer draws both marks (`drawBadgeMarks`).
**Effective** and **Dampen** (`scale`) multiply the card's target's effect when it activates: `handTurn` resolves the card's last step with `StepRules.scale` (`scaleOf`: 1.05 … 1.35, or 0.95 … 0.65), so `resolveStep` scales a potion's heal, a key (keys held are fractional, `snap`ped, shown by `keyCount` in `whole.ts` to two decimals when not whole), a shard, each key a door takes (`doorCost(tile, player, scale)` needs that much of each colour held; DOOR's `CardRules.scale` makes the card want only doors it can pay; the door cards want a door run only while the hero can pay for every door of it, `paysRun`), a Heart Door's toll (only ever smaller) and a fight's damage, which the fight plays out as predicted and adds (`StepEffect.extraDamage`, which can fell the hero; given back as a heal below 0) when it settles, with its Silver, Gold and XP (`StepEffect.scale`, through `winFight` and `RunPurse`); GK SIPHON's key too.
**Skip** (`chance`) rolls on the card's last step, drawing the next number of its own stream, started from the run seed (`HandRules.skipHits`, spent by `skipRolled`; `run.skipRolls` counts those drawn, counted once the step is in the undo history, so undo takes a roll back and a run of the same seed played the same way skips the same targets): the target vanishes without effect (`vanish`, a turn of its own, with `render/effects/poof.ts`'s puff at `game.vanished`), never a boss or Greater Boss; on the Tower's stairs `advanceTowerRoom` climbs two floors (the step's `CardStep.floorSkip`; the floor climbed past pays no Spare Change or Wishing Well, and an area climbed past is still judged) with *Floor Skipped*.
It has no limit a floor.
**More Enemies** (`floor`, the one badge that changes the floors themselves, `enemies/more-enemies.ts`) sits only on a monster card (`BadgeDef.cards`, `fitsCard`: `attachBadge` refuses another card, a swap that would carry it onto one takes it off, and both decoders drop it there); `extraEnemies(run.badges)` sums each strength's percent over the cards covering it (MONSTER every strength, each from its own count; the Greater Boss never), and the boards add the extras as a last stage before `tierCells`: `RoomWorld` (its `extras`, through `withExtraEnemies`) and the Delve `World` (its second argument, through `delveExtras`, cached per area, each of the area's ten equivalent floors by tile depth counting and placing its own, bottom first, on one `extraGround` for the area: the tiles as a grid of indices with their neighbours and the shortest walk worked out once; the area's torches come from the cache `World.torches` reads too, so they are placed once).
`addEnemies` adds each strength's generated count times its percent, the fraction rolled as a chance of one more, each a copy of one of the floor's own of that strength, placed by `nextSpot`: corners of reward rooms behind a gate (a door or enemy), corners of other rooms behind one, corners and then edges of the core, any tile that cuts nothing off (Tarjan's articulation points, extras already placed counting as walls), and only then one that does, off the shortest walk from the way in to the target (the stairs, or the area's boss) first; never the way in or beside it, beside the stairs, near the milestone gate or on a torch.
All its rolls come from a stream seeded by the run seed and floor (`extrasSeed`), so a floor comes out the same whenever it is rebuilt; runs without it generate exactly as before, so no layout version moves.
`Game.newRun` rebuilds the board after `dealHand` so a run going straight inside gets its extras.
`tests/more-enemies.test.ts` covers it.
**Dash** (`aim`, rare, opened by the Courage skill Dash Badge) adds its value (1 to 7) to the tiles its card's first step toward a new target may dash across (`dashFor`), on top of Dash and End Rush; it needs no run state, since a dash is one turn and one undo.
Turning on Dev mode (`setDevMode`) owns Combat Stance, Buildout and Badges and every badge at level 7.
`handTurn` activates a card when its path's last step lands (after its fight settles, wrapping the encounter's `settle`) or an in-place card acts (`activation.actInPlace`): `activation.activate` (`CardActivation`, `game/card-activation.ts`) pays the rewards, HP and Silver and XP through the run (so undo takes them back) and Gold through `RunPurse.badgeGold`, gated per target in `lootedTiles`.
**Card levels** (`cards/levels.ts`): each card's XP, `save.cardXp` (by card, none left out), raises its level by `CARD_LEVEL_XP` (60 levels, `MAX_CARD_LEVEL`; 3,541,500 XP in all, `MAX_CARD_XP`, past which a card gains none), read with `cardLevel(xp, cap)` (the level, no higher than the card's max level `cap`; the XP `into` the next, which keeps gathering past the cap; what it costs, `needed`; and `capped`).
A card's max level is `cardMaxLevel` (cards/trees.ts): every level its tree's nodes hold, `BASE_MAX_LEVEL` (10) until card buys open more (and 10 for a card without a tree); `levelIn(save, card)` reads a card's level under it, and `totalCardLevels` every card's, for the Deck tab's dot.
Each level gives a card point (`cardPoints`, `pointsOf`), spent on the card's tree.
**Card trees** (`cards/trees.ts`): `CARD_TREES` holds the trees of STAIRS, HEAL, DOOR, GOLD, HEART and WOODEN DOOR, KEY, GOLD, BLUE and RED KEY, ENEMY, WEAK, BASE, STRONG, ELITE, BOSS, NON-BOSS, FAST and FLYING ENEMY, ARCHER, ATK UP, DEF UP, TORCH and CHEST, each node (`CardTreeNode`) a rarity (`CardRarity`, `cards/rarity.ts`: common, uncommon, rare, epic, legendary), the levels it holds from the start (`levels`: 0 for a node only a card buy of its rarity opens), the most card buys can open on it (`max`), the `TreeStat`s each level raises, the node it follows (`after`; a track's first node has none) and, where it isn't `opensAt`'s default, the levels the nodes after it wait for (`unlocks`: TORCH's Boss Defense 5 of its 10, CHEST's Chest Gold 5 of its 10).
Every tree holds 10 levels from the start and opens to 60 (its nodes' `max` add up to `MAX_CARD_LEVEL`), each track rising a rarity a node; CHEST's one common node forks into two tracks.
GOLD, BLUE and RED KEY share one tree shape (`keyTree`), each raising its colour's own stats, `${colour}${part}` (`ColorKeyStat`, `COLOR_KEY_PARTS`, read with `keyStat`).
Those multiply their KEY counterparts where they are percents (`compounded`: Key Gold in `Finds.pickup`, Key Find in `stepRules`' `keyFind`, `DoorDiscounts.yellowKey`/`blueKey`/`redKey`, its own factor on that colour's key on any door that takes it: `doorKeyScale` gives each colour's part of a key, a `KeyScale` that `doorCost` and `resolveStep` read by `keyNeed`, the step's `keyAmounts` when the colours a door took cost apart) and add where they are flat (`StepRules.yellowKeyHeal` and the rest, Key Silver, `Game.keyChest`'s chance); Max HP (`treeHeroFactor`, in `heroFactor` and `rowValue`, and once in `dealHand`), each colour's Locksmith adds to KEY's (`luckyStep`, one roll with KEY's salt), and its Master Key (`Game.masterKey`, `COLOR_MASTER_KEY_SALT`, the floor's doors with a lock of the colour) have their own.
Stats several trees share (`goldBonus`, `freeUtilityChance`, …) still add into one card factor in `treeBonus`.
A node holds `nodeCap` levels: its `levels` plus those card buys opened (`save.cardBuys.opened`, `CardOpened`).
A point goes on a node once a card buy has opened it and its parent holds `opensAt` levels (all a common parent starts with, or 1 for a node a buy opened) (`nodeOpen`, `raiseRefusal`: `unknown`, `sealed` (no buy has opened it), `maxed`, `locked` or `points`), and `pointsLeft` is the card's points less those spent (`pointsSpent`).
`cardLook` gives how a card looks for its opened nodes: the rarest rarity holding a level, and whether a common buy raised it (`upgraded`).
`save.cardTrees` (`CardTreeLevels`: by card, node → levels) holds them; `decodeCardTrees` keeps known nodes in tree order, whole, within what the node holds, a child only after its parent holds enough, and no more points than the card's level under its max gives.
STAIRS' legendary nodes add two stats: `overhealMax` (HEAL's too), percents of max HP that `treeOverheal` adds to the Overheal ceiling whether or not the skill is owned, and `stairSkip`, a flat chance that `HandRules.rollsSkip` and `skipHits` add to the Skip badge's on STAIRS' last step onto the Tower's stairs (rolling even without the badge), on the same stream.
HEAL's epic and legendary nodes open stats the player may not own, each added to the stat's own and counting from zero without it: `freeDefenseChance` (`freeUpChances`, `rollFreeTraining`'s `chances`, a Defense level with no Free Defense trained) and `parry` (`Player.cardParry`, set in `dealHand`, which `parryCut` adds to Parry %, under `PARRY_CAP`).
Its uncommon and rare nodes add `potionSilver` (flat Silver through `RunPurse.silver` as `Finds.pickup` takes a potion), `regen` (a percent on `StepRules.regen`, after the Regen research) and `shroud` (against every enemy: `Player.foeShroud.monster`, ENEMY's key).
**Card buys** (`cards/buys.ts`, the Deck page's `CardBuyDesk`, `game/card-buy-desk.ts`, the game's `cardBuys`): `CARD_BUY_GEMS` (1 for 20, 10 for 200) each open one level on a deck card's tree; `buyOne` rolls a rarity by `CARD_RARITY_WEIGHTS` (60%, 32%, 6%, 1.5%, 0.5%, in hundredths of a percent), picks a deck card with room in that rarity (`roomIn`; with none, any deck card with room), and `rarityOn` takes the rolled rarity while the card has room in it, else the next rarer with room, else the rarest commoner; it opens a sealed node of that rarity first (at 1 level), else raises one with room, three numbers drawn each.
`buyCards` carries on `save.cardBuys.rng` as `drawBadges` does; `stampProfile` (`save/profile.ts`) seeds it once, from `stream("cardBuys")`, as the profile is made (on start-up for a profile without one, and in `eraseAll`), and a buy seeds it if the profile never was.
`buysLeft` caps a purchase (`refusal`: `full`, or `short` of Gems, which asks for them); buys work inside a run too, since the run keeps the trees it went in with, and a respec leaves what buys opened.
`ui/card-buy-reveal.ts` (`revealBuys`) shows them, reusing the badge reveal's layer, header, Skip and `DrawAgain`; each card glows for its rarity (`.cb-stage`), rare and epic with rays, legendary with stars on a darkened screen.
`tests/card-buys.test.ts` covers them, and `tests/card-buys-browser.mjs` (its own Vite server) buys on the Deck page and screenshots each rarity's reveal and look.
`CardTreeDesk` (`game/card-tree-desk.ts`, the game's `cardTrees`) is the API the tree screen calls: `raise` and `respec` (`RESPEC_GEMS`, 20, every point back; `short` without the Gems, nothing paid with Dev free purchases), both refused `inside` a run, since the trees change in the forest only.
A run copies its hand's trees as it goes inside (`runCardTrees` in `dealHand`, `run.cardTrees`), so a run paused inside while the other mode's forest edits the trees keeps its floors and bonuses; `treeBonus` sums each stat over them (`Game.treeBonus` and `RunPurse`'s, none in the forest), and a percent stat is a card factor (`treeFactor`, the cards' percents added), the `FLAT_STATS` added as they are.
