# Pages and HUD

`main.ts` wiring, `PageGame` and the desks, `Game`'s UI commands, every page in `src/ui/`, the HUD, dialogs, lessons, the frame loop and the UI golden.

**Pages and HUD.** `main.ts` only wires things together: it builds the shell, creates the `Game` and `Renderer`, owns the current tab and `navigate()` (inside a run, meaning the active mode's run isn't `outside`, the tab row `nav` (a solid bar of icons, no words, `NAV_TABS` in `ui/dom.ts`: board, Upgrades, Deck, Gear, Research, Defend, Shop) shows the hand's cards in place of its buttons, so the other pages open only from the forest, but for Settings and Research, from the HUD; leaving the board inside a run pauses the hand (`Game.pauseForPage`, keeping whether it played and its speed) and coming back to it plays on as it was (`resumeFromPage`); the HUD's Settings button opens the Settings page, with a Back button to the board (inside a run it waits in the run's menu, `ui/run-menu.ts`: the hamburger, `#run-menu-toggle`, stands in Settings' place at the top of the actions column, and pressed, a thin white frame round it and the Shop under it (`#run-shop`, wearing the Shop's dot, `renderShopDot`; the Shop's Back returns to the run) slides left over the stats, with a bounce, its buttons fading in, two rows of cells filling right to left (`.run-menu-items`): Settings beside the hamburger with Research under it (the hamburger wearing Research's dot and the Tournament's while folded away) and End Run, `#end-run`, filling both rows at the far left, a new button taking a blank cell or widening the frame leftward; pressed again it fades and slides back in; Settings, the Shop, Research or End Run close it, and `renderRunMenu` moves Settings back to the column in the forest, unless the column would hold more than three buttons, Settings and Missions with the Tournament's and Mail's, `forestMenu`: then the hamburger stays in the forest, alone, holding Settings and Mail in one row of cells, `.run-menu.forest`, and wearing Mail's dot); the Shop tab, at the bottom right of the tab row (and so only outside a run), opens the Shop page; the currencies bar tops the Upgrades, Research, Deck and Gear pages (every currency, Silver first, then Gold, Gems, Ascension Shards and, once held, Medals: Silver the run's, or in the forest what the next run starts with once that is any, each through `currencyAmount` in `hud.ts`: in full below a million, then 1.23M, 4.56B … with two decimals; Training from the start, Courage once Into the depths is), and the Shop's Back returns to whichever page opened it), and passes each page an `AppContext` (`ui/app.ts`: the game, the shared dialog, `save`, `update` for the HUD, `renderPage`, `navigate`, `confirm`).
Pages see the game as a `PageGame`: its mode, run, save and whether the hero lies fallen to read (they may change a setting or a purchase in the save but never replace the save, run or mode), and the commands that change the rest; they never touch the `Renderer`.
`Game` keeps the commands of each page in desks it owns (`src/game/`), each over a `DeskHost` (`game/desk.ts`: the save, free purchases, the clock and the status line): `training` (`TrainingDesk`), `research` (`ResearchDesk`), `deck` (`DeckEditor`), `gear` (`GearDesk`) and `gemFinder` (`GemFinder`); beside them `RunPurse` (`game/run-purse.ts`) pays what a run finds, and `game/hero-sync.ts` keeps the runs' heroes in step with the loadout (`startingHero`, `changeLoadout`, `readyForestRuns`).
`Game` keeps its step and lifecycle stages private, and the UI changes play only through its commands: `stepManually`, `walkTo`, `toggleAuto` (the hand's play and pause inside a run), `enterRun` (the forest's Enter button, `#enter-run` under Goals, where play/pause stands inside a run: straight in through the entrance), `autoTurn` (one Automove step, run by the frame loop), `cancelRoute`, `undo`, `acceptDefeat`, `finish` and `eraseAll`.
`tests/game-commands.test.ts` covers those commands in Node.

`board-lesson.ts` shows the lessons on the Tower's board (`game.boardLesson`, a note over the top of the board): a new game's first run's, `climb`, which holds the hand paused (`playHand`, `toggleAuto` and `changeSpeed` refuse, and the HUD closes play and the arrows) until the note is tapped (`game.dismissLesson`, `save.tutorials.climb`), then plays it; in the forest `enter`, saying to try again and to spend the run's Inspiration, with the tutorials' pointing hand at Enter, until tapped or a run goes inside from the forest (`save.tutorials.enter`); then, once Into the depths is owned, `delve`, the hand at the forest sign, until the Delve is opened (`save.tutorials.delve`).
Nothing else is locked meanwhile; tests that play the hand from a new game start from a save with `climb` set.
`frame-loop.ts` runs the requestAnimationFrame loop (drawing, the Upgrades and Research pages' particles, settling encounters, route steps, Automove timing, the purse's count-up, the once-a-second Archives tick, which saves as soon as research or training completes, autosave; each part runs guarded and the next frame is asked for first, so an error in one is reported to the console and never stops the loop: `tests/frame-loop.test.ts`), and `debug-hooks.ts` installs the `towerDebug`/`delveDebug`/`defendDebug`/`tournamentDebug`/`mailDebug` console helpers.
`tests/ui-golden.mjs` (`npm run test:ui`) loads fixed saves with seeded randomness, clicks through every page, tab, tooltip, board tap and dialog, and hashes the `#app` HTML after each step against `tests/fixtures/ui.golden.json`.
It sets `globalThis.__handStartsPaused` and `__handHeld` (read by `frame-loop.ts`: the frame loop never runs the hand's turns), so pressing play or a card's Focus changes the HUD but no step can land between a snapshot's checks.
As with the render golden, regenerate it with `UPDATE_GOLDEN=1` on the unchanged code before a UI change (HTML goes to `test-results/ui*/`).
