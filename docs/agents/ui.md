# Pages and HUD

`main.ts` wiring, `PageGame` and the desks, `Game`'s UI commands, every page in `src/ui/`, the HUD, dialogs, lessons, the frame loop and the UI golden.

**Pages and HUD.** `main.ts` only wires things together: it builds the shell, creates the `Game` and `Renderer`, owns the current tab and `navigate()` (inside a run, meaning the active mode's run isn't `outside`, the tab row `nav` (a solid bar of icons, no words, `NAV_TABS` in `ui/dom.ts`: board, Upgrades, Deck, Gear, Research, Defend, Shop) shows the hand's cards in place of its buttons, so the other pages open only from the forest, but for Settings and Research, from the HUD; leaving the board inside a run pauses the hand (`Game.pauseForPage`, keeping whether it played and its speed) and coming back to it plays on as it was (`resumeFromPage`); the HUD's Settings button opens the Settings page, with a Back button to the board (it waits in the HUD's menu, `ui/run-menu.ts`: the hamburger, `#run-menu-toggle`, stands at the top of the actions column, and pressed, a thin white frame round it (and inside a run the Shop under it, `#run-shop`, wearing the Shop's dot, `renderShopDot`; the Shop's Back returns to the run) slides left over the stats, with a bounce, its buttons fading in, cells filling right to left (`.run-menu-items`, in the shell's `ACTIONS_MENU` order, hidden ones taking no cell): inside a run two rows, Settings beside the hamburger with Research under it, then the Tournament's (once entered), Missions and Events, and End Run, `#end-run`, filling both rows at the far left, a new button taking a blank cell or widening the frame leftward; in the forest one row (`.run-menu.forest`), Settings, Missions, Relics (once Events are open) and Mail (while there is mail), with the Tournament's and Events' buttons standing in the column under the hamburger, always in sight (`renderRunMenu` moves those two between the column and the menu); the hamburger wears the dots of the buttons folded in it, and while open its bars fold into an X (CSS on its `aria-expanded`); pressed again it fades and slides back in; Settings, the Shop, Research or End Run close it); the Shop tab, at the bottom right of the tab row (and so only outside a run), opens the Shop page; the currencies bar tops the Upgrades, Research, Deck, Gear, Missions and Events pages (`CURRENCY_PAGES` in `main.ts`) (an amount there, or in the purse, can be held at what it read, `holdAmount` in `hud.ts`, while a daily mission's pay, an event mission's Medals or a tapped Gem fly to it: `flyToPurse` in `flourish.ts`) (every currency, Silver first, then Gold, Gems, Ascension Shards and, once held, Medals: Silver the run's, or in the forest what the next run starts with once that is any, each through `currencyAmount` in `hud.ts`: in full below a million, then 1.23M, 4.56B … with two decimals; Training from the start, Courage once Into the depths is), and the Shop's Back returns to whichever page opened it, as the Missions, Events and Relics screens' X does), and passes each page an `AppContext` (`ui/app.ts`: the game, the shared dialog, `save`, `update` for the HUD, `renderPage`, `navigate`, `confirm`).
Pages see the game as a `PageGame`: its mode, run, save and whether the hero lies fallen to read (they may change a setting or a purchase in the save but never replace the save, run or mode), and the commands that change the rest; they never touch the `Renderer`.
`Game` keeps the commands of each page in desks it owns (`src/game/`), each over a `DeskHost` (`game/desk.ts`: the save, free purchases, the clock and the status line): `training` (`TrainingDesk`), `research` (`ResearchDesk`), `deck` (`DeckEditor`), `gear` (`GearDesk`) and `gemFinder` (`GemFinder`); beside them `RunPurse` (`game/run-purse.ts`) pays what a run finds, and `game/hero-sync.ts` keeps the runs' heroes in step with the loadout (`startingHero`, `changeLoadout`, `readyForestRuns`).
`Game` keeps its step and lifecycle stages private, and the UI changes play only through its commands: `stepManually`, `walkTo`, `toggleAuto` (the hand's play and pause inside a run), `enterRun` (the forest's Enter button, `#enter-run` under Goals, where play/pause stands inside a run: straight in through the entrance), `autoTurn` (one Automove step, run by the frame loop), `cancelRoute`, `undo`, `acceptDefeat`, `finish` and `eraseAll`.
