# Currencies, Shop and online features

Gems, the Shop, Gold bonuses, Spare Change and Wishing Well, and the server-backed Tournament, Mail, Missions, Events and relics.

**Gems** (`save.gems`, the premium currency) and where they stand live in `gems.ts` (`save.gemDrop`, never part of a run, so undo can't touch them): `reachFloor` decides, each time `GemFinder.step` (`game/gem-finder.ts`, the game's `gemFinder`, which also holds the Gem on the board and the ad's Gems) runs after a step inside a run (and on entering), whether the hero reached a new floor (the Tower height, or the Delve's furthest equivalent floor, by mode and run seed), misses a Gem left on another floor (another is owed `GEM_MISSED_FLOORS` new floors on, carried between runs; `newRun` misses one its mode's run left out), and whether one appears now (the next new floor, once the 30-minute `GEM_COOLDOWN_MS` after the last collected is over).
`gemSpot` picks its plain floor tile from those the hero can walk to (in the Delve within the view, rows ahead first), out of the way: on the fewest shortest paths between the hero and the floor's targets and from one target to the next, then against the most walls, drawn among the ties from a stream seeded by the run seed and floor, so it is the same every time (it never turns a wall into floor: terrain is baked, lit and decorated as generated).
Stepping on it or tapping it (`gemFinder.collectAt`, from `BoardOverlay.tap`) pays one and sets `gemFinder.sparkle`; the renderer draws the Gem and its sparkle over the darkness (`gem-art.ts`), and the gain popup paints it.
Tapped outside cinematic mode, a Gem and *+1* also fly from its tile to the purse's Gems (`flyToPurse` in `ui/flourish.ts`), which keep their old amount until it lands (`holdAmount` in `ui/hud.ts`).
The ad button (`gemFinder.claimAd`, `AD_GEMS` every `AD_COOLDOWN_MS`, on `game.clock()`; no ad plays yet) stands under the purse in the forest and inside a run, in both modes, and bursts into blue glints when claimed (`gemSparkle` in `ui/flourish.ts`).
Gems buy hand slots (`deck.buySlot`: `HAND_SLOT_GEMS` in `cards/cards.ts`, once Larger Hand is owned; `handSlots(save)` is the hand's size, 4, 5 with Larger Hand, plus `save.handSlots`) and Training resets (`training.reset`, `TRAINING_RESET_GEMS`, through `changeLoadout` like training).
`tests/gems.test.ts` covers them.
**Trial Points** are an independent currency in `save.trialAccess.points`, awarded only by the first clear of a trial difficulty, in an amount equal to that difficulty.
`proving-grounds/progression.ts` saves the payment receipt per trial/difficulty and calls `earn(save,"trialPoints",amount)`; manual, cards and hybrid victories share the same receipt and highest-clear record.
They have no spending options yet and are shown in the trial menu and, once earned, the profile Stats.
The **Shop** (`src/shop/`, `ui/shop-page.ts`; see `docs/SHOP.md`) sells offers and hands what they grant to their owners: `offers.ts` holds the catalog (`OFFERS`: the limited Ascension Shard packs, each a `bundle` of Shards and Gems bought once per `period` of Shop days, `SHARD_PACK_DAYS`, 14, from its last purchase's, the one-time packs, the Daily Free Gems, a `period` of 1, whose Claim button wears a dot while it can be claimed, and the Gem packs; **Ascension Shards**, `save.ascensionShards`, a limited currency nothing spends yet, wear `shardIcon` from `ui/dom.ts`, a green crystal, wherever shown; until the store is connected, `stubServer` confirms the Gem and shard packs' skus unpaid, `stubConfirms`), `items.ts` grants an item to its owner, `currency.ts` reads, spends and credits each currency, `rarity.ts` is the rarity table, `entitlements.ts` the permanent perks bought once (`save.entitlements`, kept by `eraseAll`; the Premium Passes `pass1` to `pass3` among them, sold as the `passes` category, which the Shop page doesn't list): `goldFactor`, which `RunPurse.gold` (`game/run-purse.ts`, through which `Game` pays all a run finds: Gold, Silver, kill loot, treasure and floors) multiplies every Gold banked by (×1.5, ×2 and ×3, multiplied together) and which returns what it banked for the popups, Ad-Disable's `permanentBoost`, which sets `save.trainingBoostUntil` to `BOOST_FOREVER` (on purchase, and on every load; a save without it loses a `BOOST_FOREVER` it held), and the Special and Premium Coin Packs' `trainingRate` (×2 and ×3, multiplied together), which `workLeft` and `doneAt` in `progression/training-jobs.ts` take beside the boost (×12 with all three);
a purchase rescales ranks in training to the new pace (`training.pace` before it, `training.repace` after), and the Training tab shows the permanent speed glowing on the trainers' row (the boost box goes only with Ad-Disable).
`transactions.ts`' `purchase` makes every check (requirements, start and end, limit, the item, payment) before changing anything, then pays, grants, records (`save.shop.history`, the last 100, shown only in Dev mode) and counts (`save.shop.counts`, per Shop day for a daily offer).
`Game.buyOffer(id, serverNow, paid)` runs it on a server time confirmed for the purchase (`clock.ts`: the Shop day is the GMT day on the server's clock, the last confirmed time saved and never moved back; the page estimates between confirmations only for countdowns and the Shop button's dot).
`server.ts` is the server and store interface (`ShopServer`: time, prices by sku, purchase, open the store), stubbed by `stubServer` until they are set up; Dev free purchases grant real-money offers without it.
What a purchase or claim grants rises to the middle of the screen until pressed (`revealReward` in `ui/reward-reveal.ts`, styled like `card-reveal.ts`'s new card: `offerShown`), golden rays turning behind a permanent unlock but not behind currency; the Goals page shows its currency rewards (`goalRewardShown`) and a Premium Pass bought there the same way.
`tests/shop.test.ts` covers it.

**Spare Change** (`floorGold` in `progression/loadout.ts`, from the Gold / Floor training row and research) pays Gold for each floor climbed for the first time in a run (`RunPurse.floorGold`: the Tower's stairs up in `advanceTowerRoom`, each new equivalent floor in `afterDelveStep`), gated by `lootedTiles` (the stairs tile's key, or the Delve's `x = -1` column with the floor) like a kill's Gold, times the research's percent and the tier's Gold bonus; `tests/floor-gold.test.ts` covers it.
Below it, **Wishing Well** pays Silver the same way (`floorSilver`, `RunPurse.floorSilver`; gated by the run's own `maxHeight` in the Tower, which only rises, since Silver is the run's and undo takes both back), **Wealthy** multiplies all Silver a run finds (Silver found, `silverFound` in `gold-multipliers.ts`, which `RunPurse.silver` pays by, keeping Silver's fractions with `snap`, and the Silver Bonus and Silver / Floor rows show) and **Loot**, through Gold Bonus (`goldBonus`), a kill's Gold before the tier's bonus (`RunPurse.enemyLoot`), a treasure chest's (`RunPurse.treasure`) and the Gold every item taken off the floor pays (`RunPurse.pickupGold`); each is its training percent times its research percent, read when paid.
**Pickup Gold:** every item a run takes off the floor pays Gold, raised by Gold Bonus and then the tier's bonus: a key `KEY_GOLD` by its colour (1 gold, 2 blue, 3 red), a keyed door opened the sum of its colours' (`doorGold` in `doors.ts`: a Triune Door 6), anything else `PICKUP_GOLD`, 1 (a potion, a shard, a torch put out, a treasure chest, a Heart, Wooden or Steel Door; `pickupGold` in `doors.ts`); an area reward chest, a one-time reward and no floor item, pays none.
`Finds.pickupGold` (`game/finds.ts`, the game's `finds`) pays it from `finds.pickup`, `finds.door` and `land` (torches, through `breakTorchAt`'s return), gated per tile by `lootedTiles` (`<loot key>:pickup`) like a kill's Gold, so undo can't pay it twice; a treasure chest's `PICKUP_GOLD` is added to its own Gold in `RunPurse.treasure` instead, and Gold Bonus raises the whole of it.
Floor Skip Reward (`RunPurse.skippedFloorGold`) counts the Pickup Gold of the skipped floor's items, doors and lit torches too, marking each looted.
**Warp Loot** (`src/warp-loot.ts`; Archives research `warpLoot`, +2% a level for 50 levels, opened by Tower III's floor 150 Goal) adds to a Tower run going inside above floor 1 (`TowerRun.start`: a Warp, or a Retry from one) `warpLootSilver`, its percent of `skippedSilver`, in whole Silver, beside Pocket Money (`Game.dealHand`, through `Game.warpLoot`).
The skipped floors aren't generated (about 5 ms a floor): `skippedSilver` estimates them from the enemy schedule, floor by floor, `enemiesOnFloor` (7.5 enemies on the first 20 floors, 10.8 after, or 8 × `enemyCountPercent` once that is more, fitted to generated floors) shared by `enemyShare` and paid by `silverForKill`, with a boss's on every tenth floor (`floorKillSilver`), plus the Wishing Well's starting Silver / Floor and its research, both times `silverFound` at the starting Silver Bonus ranks, then Interest at its research's rate on the Silver held from the Pocket Money, at most Max Interest a floor; Shrine boons are left out, a run starting with none.
It is read once, as the run goes inside, so undo keeps it with the run's Silver.
When it adds 1 or more, `Game.warpLooted` has the board play `drawWarpLoot` (`level-up.ts`, `WARP_LOOT_MS`): a silvery blaze over the hero, *WARP LOOT* over the Silver and its coin; undo and a new run clear it.
`tests/warp-loot.test.ts` covers the schedule, the estimate and the run's Silver.
Its Gold rises a row over the item's own popup, at the same time (`drop: { row: 1, with: true }`); Goldback's combo leaves it alone.
`tests/currency-bonus.test.ts` and `tests/fight-playback.test.ts` cover it.
Their rows read as multipliers (`trainingText`: unit `×`); `tests/currency-bonus.test.ts` covers them.

**Gold multipliers** (`gold-multipliers.ts`, `goldMultipliers` over a `GoldContext`: the save, the mode, the tier, the clock and the Shrine boons' Gold factor; `Game.goldContext` builds it for the HUD, `RunPurse` for paying) list every factor on all Gold banked, as the player sees it: the tower's (`TIER_BONUS_TENTHS`), the ads' ×1.5 on one line (*Ad Boost* while the Gold ad's boost runs, else *Disable ads* for Ad-Disable: one slot, so the two never stack), each Coin Pack, the themes owned (*Themes owned (n)*, ×1.025 a theme, `themeGoldFactor` in `theme-collection.ts`; see rendering.md), the mode's equipment's Gold found (*Gold Found*) and, once Shrines are open in a Tower run, the Gold boons (*Shrine Boons*), a multiplier not applied having a null factor, shown as *Inactive*.
Within a kind they add (the themes' 2.5% each, the equipment's Gold found over every piece); the Coin Packs are the exception, each its own factor multiplying the other (×2 and ×3 make ×6), as the Shop describes them, and so are their training rates.
`goldMultiplierTotal` multiplies those applied, and `goldBanked` is what paying banks: Gold found (before any multiplier, the tower's too) times that total, snapped once, so the total shown is exactly what is banked (`tests/gold-multipliers.test.ts` checks every way a run pays Gold against it).
`RunPurse.gold` takes Gold found and banks it through `goldBanked`; its callers never apply the tier themselves, and the kill and chest previews read the same path.
Inside a run the HUD's height column shows the total on a line of its own below a divider under the base enemy's stats, which sit nearer the floor they belong to (`#gold-bonus`, `renderGoldBonus` in `ui/hud.ts`); pressed, it opens the *All Gold Bonuses* box (`ui/gold-bonus-box.ts`), a plain layer rather than the shared dialog, so the game plays on behind it, and a press anywhere closes it.
In the forest, once a second tower is open, `drawEntrance` draws the tier's Gold coin, × and multiplier in a line under the numeral over the arch.
With the Sprites tower (its PNG drawn), the numeral and that line sit up by the board's top edge instead, clear of its taller arch.

The **Tournament** (`src/tournament/`, see `docs/TOURNAMENT.md`, which also holds the build order, all built but Tickets in the purse) is a twice-weekly global competition in the Delve, opened by Tower I's floor 60 Goal (the `tournament` `GoalUnlock`): `schedule.ts` holds when each runs on the server's GMT clock (entry all of each Wednesday and Saturday, `GRACE_MS` more for runs inside, results claimable for `CLAIM_MS` from finalization; `phaseOf` is upcoming, open, ending or results, and a tournament's id is the GMT date it opens; `TournamentDesk.phase` shows a tournament the player never entered, or whose prize they claimed, as upcoming once entry closes, and every tournament once its Results are over, claimed or not, so the page and HUD show only when the next opens; a prize left unclaimed is to come as Mail, `docs/MAIL.md`), `leagues.ts` the five leagues (Copper to Champion, each playing Delve cave 1, 3, 5, 7 or 9) and who moves between them (`nextLeague`: the top `PROMOTE_PERCENT` up, the bottom `DEMOTE_PERCENT` down only from Platinum and Champion; `withinTop` measures a place by the share of entrants ahead of it, whole numbers only), `prizes.ts` the ten prize levels a league by percentile (`PRIZES`, Ascension Shards and Gems; `placeOf` counts ties at their cohort's lowest place; `oscillationFailures` checks that moving up never pays less than holding back), `tickets.ts` the Tickets (one free a tournament, then one for an ad, then `GEM_TICKET_STEP` Gems more each, starting over each tournament), `progress.ts` its save (`save.tournament`, `decodeTournament`), and `server.ts` the `TournamentServer` interface (the live tournament with its league's shared seeds, entering, submitting a score, standings, claiming), stubbed by `stubTournament`, which makes up each league's other entrants and reads the player's own record.
`TournamentDesk` (`game/tournament-desk.ts`, the game's `tournament`) takes in what the server reports (`observe`, which confirms the Shop's server time and grants the free Ticket) and runs the Tickets, standings and claims; the server's answers are fetched by the pages, so the desk never waits on the network.
A tournament run (`run.ts`, a `TournamentRun` on the `DelveRun`) is a Delve run in the league's cave, whether or not the Delve is open: `Game.beginTournament(info, entry)` spends a Ticket, switches to the Delve (`enterMode`, which `switchMode` gates) and the cave's tier (`switchTier`, so its records are the cave's) and goes in on the league's `layout` seed; the Delve `World` makes its enemies as strong as the next cave's, the next cave's step after the tier's factor (`tournamentStatFactor`: ×5 in Copper, ×3 above; `tournamentTile`, through `World.inLeague`); `RunPurse` draws from the run's own streams (`runStream`: the `game` seed for loot, the `equipment` seed for Equipment's drops through its `equipmentRng`), each draw counted on the run so undo and reloads replay them; and `finalizeRun` keeps its score (`tournamentScore`, the deepest depth as the HUD counts it) to send and returns the player's mode and Delve tier (`back`).
`TournamentClient` (`client.ts`) does what waits on the server: refreshing, entering (`begin`), and sending scores (`tick` keeps a run inside's depth as it rises, only while it counts, `TournamentDesk.counts`: none reached after the grace does; `sendRun` as the run's end dialog opens, which shows the place; `tick` retries one that couldn't be sent).
The desk reads the server's report only while it is of the latest tournament to have opened (`live`), so the next one shows open on the clock before the server is asked; past the grace, `main.ts` asks again each minute until the results are final (`awaitingResults`), and `final` holds the final place, prize level and the league it moves the player to.
Its screens: the forest's Tournament Hall (`TOURNAMENT_HALL` and `drawTournamentHall` in `outside.ts`, `OutsideWorld`'s `hall`, `Game.atTournamentHall`), the HUD's Tournament button (`#tournament-button`, `renderTournamentButton` in `ui/hud.ts`: in the forest's actions column under the hamburger, labelled with the phase or the countdown to the next, and in the run's menu once entered; `renderRunMenu` places it) and the Tournament page (`ui/tournament-page.ts`: the league, phase, Tickets, Begin or the ad and Gem Tickets, the standing and prize, *Claim rewards* through `TournamentClient.claim`, and All prizes); `main.ts`'s `refreshTournament` asks the server as the app starts, the page opens and the phase changes, and celebrates a new free Ticket with `revealReward`.
The UI suite fixes the page's clock (`page.clock.setFixedTime`) so the countdown is the same every run.
`tests/tournament.test.ts`, `tests/tournament-run.test.ts`, `tests/tournament-screens.test.ts` and `tests/tournament-ending.test.ts` (a tournament from entry through the grace, the results, the claim and expiry to the next) cover it; `tests/tournament-trace.test.ts` hashes every turn of seeded Copper, Silver and Champion runs, played by the hand from the stand-in's seeds, against `tests/fixtures/tournament-trace.golden.json` (regenerate it with `UPDATE_GOLDEN=1` only for an intended tournament or gameplay change), and checks two players on different game streams play the same run.
The Tournament page redraws whole only when something but its countdown changes, and as soon as the server answers.
**Mail** (`src/mail/`, see `docs/MAIL.md`) is messages the game server pushes to a player, each a subject, body and possibly items to claim (`MailItem`: a currency or Tickets; a kind this client doesn't know decodes as `unknown`, which holds the whole message back from a claim): `message.ts` holds the types, `decodeMessage` (the server's answers and the save are read alike, field by field) and how long one is kept (`kept`: `MAIL_DAYS`, 7, or while its items wait, up to `MAIL_KEEP_DAYS`, 90, the bound on stored mail), `progress.ts` its save (`save.mail`: the inbox the server last reported, the ids read and removed here not yet confirmed, `unsynced`, the ids granted here, `claimed`, so none is granted twice, and the stand-in's mailbox, `stub`; `eraseAll` keeps it all, as Mail is the server's), `server.ts` the `MailServer` interface (the inbox with the server's time, claim, mark read, hide, which refuses a message with items waiting) and `stubMail`, whose mailbox is `save.mail.stub`: Dev mail posted by `postStubMail`, and each Tournament prize left unclaimed past its claim window, posted as the window closes (`tournament:<id>`, worked out by `stubResults` in `tournament/server.ts`, which the Tournament stand-in uses too), and `client.ts` the `MailClient` (refresh, open, hide, claim, and syncing what was read and removed).
`MailDesk` (`game/mail-desk.ts`, the game's `mail`) takes in the inbox (`receive`, keeping what was done here and not yet confirmed), lists what shows (`recent`, newest first), the Mail button's dot (`unread`), and opens, grants and removes.
Its screens: the HUD's Mail button (`#mail-button`, an envelope, `mailIcon` in `ui/dom.ts`, in the forest's menu beside Settings, `renderMailButton` in `ui/hud.ts`: shown only in the forest while there is mail to show, with a dot while any is unread) and the Mail popup (`ui/mail-dialog.ts`, `MailDialog`, in the shared dialog: the list, each row an unread dot, the subject, a gift box, `giftIcon`, while items wait, how long ago, and an X only on a message with nothing to claim; and a message, its date in GMT, body, items, and *Claim*, which closes the dialog for `revealReward`'s `mailShown` and opens it again on the message when pressed away; a phone's Back goes from a message to the list; every word from the server goes through `escapeHtml`).
`main.ts`'s `refreshMail` asks the server as the app starts, as the player arrives in the forest, every `MailClient.POLL_MS` (5 minutes) there, as the popup opens, and as the Tournament's phase changes (a prize left unclaimed comes as its Results end); the `mailDebug.send({ subject, body, items }?)` console helper (`debug-hooks.ts`) posts to the stand-in's mailbox (`OUTAGE_MAIL` when given nothing).
`tests/mail.test.ts` covers it.
**Missions** (`src/missions/`, see `docs/MISSIONS.md`) are daily missions and the week's rewards for completing them: `catalog.ts` holds each kind (`MISSIONS`: its text, target and `offered(save)`, never before what it asks for is unlocked: strong and elite enemies and blue and red keys once some open tower's record, or the Delve's equivalent floor, reaches their first floor, `enemyFirstFloor` and `keyFirstFloor`; Buy 1 badge with the Badges skill and copies left; run training with On the Job; Focus with Focus), what a mission pays (`missionReward`: 3 Gems, Gold by the highest tower open, `MISSION_GOLD`, and its equipment material, `MISSION_MATERIALS`) and the weekly rewards (`WEEKLY_REWARDS`, one every 5 missions claimed up to 35: Gold a multiple of a mission's, Gems, **Medals**, `save.medals`, a limited currency nothing spends yet, a `CURRENCIES` entry, Ascension Shards, and a Training Point, `save.bonusTrainingPoints`, which `trainingPoints` adds to those levels earn), and `progress.ts` the save (`save.missions`: the list, the 8-hour period missions were last given in, `missionPeriod`, from 00:00 GMT, the saved stream they are drawn from, seeded once from `stream("missions")`, the week, `missionWeek`, from Monday 00:00 GMT, with its tally of missions claimed and rewards claimed, and what has counted) and `decodeMissions`.
`MissionDesk` (`game/mission-desk.ts`, the game's `missions`) runs them on the Shop's estimated server time: `refresh` (main's start and once-a-second tick) gives two missions a period begun, the first two at once, while the list holds fewer than 8, those completed and waiting to be claimed included (`MISSION_CAPACITY`, `full`), each of a kind offered and with no incomplete mission of it; `record(type, amount, key)` counts toward the incomplete mission of a kind, a kill or pickup once a tile (`counted`, keyed by mode and the mode's loot key, so undo can't count it twice); `mark` counts what undo takes back (run training ranks, Focus uses) only past the run's most (`marks`, by mode and run seed); `claim` pays a mission and counts it in the week's tally, and `claimWeekly` pays a weekly reward.
`Game` counts kills by strength (`finds` `countKill`, from `winFight`), potions and blue and red keys picked up (`finds.pickup`), floors new to the run (`advanceTowerRoom`, `payDelveFloors`), run training (`trainInRun`) and Focus (`focus`); the Training and Badge desks count ranks and draws through the `DeskHost`'s `missionDone`.
Its screens: the HUD's Missions button (`#missions-button`, `missionIcon` in `ui/dom.ts`, `renderMissionsButton` in `ui/hud.ts`: a check mark over the missions in the list out of 8, complete or not, with a dot while `missions.waiting`; in the menu beside Settings, in the forest and inside a run, the hamburger wearing its dot) and the Missions screen (`ui/missions-page.ts`, `MissionsPage`, the `missions` page: an X back to the page that opened it, the weekly bar with a prize box over every fifth mission, dim, glowing to claim or checked, and the missions, each with its progress bar or *Complete!* and Claim; claims rise through `revealReward`, a weekly reward's currencies one after another, each pressed away to show the next).
`tests/missions.test.ts` covers it.
**Events** (`src/events/`, see `docs/EVENTS.md`) are fortnightly sets of missions paying Medals toward relics, opened by Tower I's floor 70 Goal (the `events` `GoalUnlock`; `EventDesk.unlocked`, or Dev mode): `catalog.ts` holds the missions (`EVENT_MISSIONS`: each kind's text by target, its three tiers' targets, whether it keeps the most reached at once, `best`, and `offered(save)`, the stand-in drawing a kind only once the player can work on it; `FIXED_MISSIONS`, the four every event opens with, and `DRAWN_MISSIONS`), each tier's Medals (`TIER_MEDALS`, 10, 15, 20), the schedule on the server's GMT clock (`eventAt`: 14-day events from a Monday 00:00, `releasedBy` and `nextReleaseAt`: 7 missions the first day and 2 on each of the next 7) and what the server says of an event (`EventInfo`: its name, times and relic milestones, `RelicMilestone`), stood in for by `stubEvent` (`TODO(server)`: named by its first day, with `STUB_MILESTONES`, placeholder relics at 350, 550 premium, 700 and 1,100 premium Medals that give nothing), and `progress.ts` the save (`save.events`: the event's id, its missions, the saved stream the stand-in draws from, seeded once from `stream("events")`, the events boosted, kept by Erase All, the missions the Events page has seen and moved, the day a login last counted, what has counted, `counted`, and the run's streaks) with what is derived from it (`tiersDone`, `baseMedals`, `relicMedals`: the event's own Medals, doubled once boosted, `milestoneReached`, `grantRelics`) and the Event Booster (`boostRefusal`, `boostEvent`, which credits the Medals already paid a second time).
`EventDesk` (`game/event-desk.ts`, the game's `events`) runs them on the Shop's estimated server time: `refresh` (main's start and once-a-second tick, and claiming the Goal) starts the next event as one ends (`roll`, keeping the boosters, and posting the ended event's unclaimed Medals to the stand-in's mailbox, `mailUnclaimed`: `event:<id>`, sent at its end, through `postStubMail`, its tiers then counted claimed for the relics), gives the missions released so far (the fixed ones first, then drawn, each kind once), counts the day's login and completes Buy badges once no badge is left to draw; `record(type, amount, key)` counts toward the event's mission of a kind, a kill or pickup once a tile (keyed as the daily missions' are); `best` keeps a `best` mission's most; `hurt` and `climbed` keep the run's streaks (floors without fight damage, and paying the most Interest, `RunPurse.interestCap`); a tier done waits to be claimed (`raise` only counts): `claim(id)` pays the mission's first tier done and not yet claimed, one a press (`EventMission.claimed`; `unclaimedTiers`, `isFinished`, `unclaimedMedals` in `progress.ts`), doubled once boosted, and grants the relics they reach; `baseMedals` counts only tiers claimed.
`Game` counts kills by strength (`finds` `countKill`), crits (`critHits`, a fight's critical strikes, carried on its `TakenStep`), Equipment from kills, potions, chests (`finds` `treasure`), floors climbed and the Tower floor reached, Free Training's levels, the Daily Free Gems and tournaments entered; Training ranks and badges through `missionDone`, and through the `DeskHost`'s `eventDone` the Gem finder (floor Gems, ad Gems) and the Equipment desk (pulls, merges).
The **Event Booster** is a Shop offer (`eventBoost`, a `ShopItem` of kind `eventBoost`, in the `passes` category, so sold only on the Events page).
**Relics** (`relics.ts`: `RELICS`, empty until relics are defined, `PREVIEW_RELICS` for Dev mode, `decodeRelics`) are kept for good in `save.relics`.
Their screens: the HUD's Events button (`#events-button`, `eventIcon` in `ui/dom.ts`, `renderEventsButtons` in `ui/hud.ts`: the time the event has left, a dot while `events.waiting`; in the forest's actions column under the Tournament's, inside a run in the run's menu) and the Events screen (`ui/events-page.ts`, `EventsPage`, the `events` page: the banner, with the time left and beside it the Medals held (`.event-held`, which a claimed tier's Medals fly to, reading the old amount until they land), the relic track (its bar and both rows of relics spanning the track's whole width with the same margin at each end, so the bar lines up with the relics' places, each row's name at its left under the first relic), the booster's offer, bought as the Goals page buys a pass, and the missions, each with its tiers claimed, the next tier's Medals and its bar, which turns gold and reads *Claim* while a tier waits (`.event-claim`, a button), a finished one moved under the rest 2 seconds after its last tier is claimed), and the Relics button (`#relics-button`, `relicIcon`, in the forest's menu once Events are open) and Relics screen (`ui/relics-page.ts`, `RelicsPage`: the relics owned in a grid, each pressed or hovered raised to full size with rays and sparkles, `.relic-pop`).
`tests/events.test.ts` covers it.
