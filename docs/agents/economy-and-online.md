# Currencies, Shop and online features

Gems, the Shop, Gold bonuses, Spare Change and Wishing Well, and the server-backed Tournament, Mail and Missions.

**Gems** (`save.gems`, the premium currency) and where they stand live in `gems.ts` (`save.gemDrop`, never part of a run, so undo can't touch them): `reachFloor` decides, each time `GemFinder.step` (`game/gem-finder.ts`, the game's `gemFinder`, which also holds the Gem on the board and the ad's Gems) runs after a step inside a run (and on entering), whether the hero reached a new floor (the Tower height, or the Delve's furthest equivalent floor, by mode and run seed), misses a Gem left on another floor (another is owed `GEM_MISSED_FLOORS` new floors on, carried between runs; `newRun` misses one its mode's run left out), and whether one appears now (the next new floor, once the 30-minute `GEM_COOLDOWN_MS` after the last collected is over).
`gemSpot` picks its plain floor tile from those the hero can walk to (in the Delve within the view, rows ahead first), out of the way: on the fewest shortest paths between the hero and the floor's targets and from one target to the next, then against the most walls, drawn among the ties from a stream seeded by the run seed and floor, so it is the same every time (it never turns a wall into floor: terrain is baked, lit and decorated as generated).
Stepping on it or tapping it (`gemFinder.collectAt`, from `BoardOverlay.tap`) pays one and sets `gemFinder.sparkle`; the renderer draws the Gem and its sparkle over the darkness (`gem-art.ts`), and the gain popup paints it.
The ad button (`gemFinder.claimAd`, `AD_GEMS` every `AD_COOLDOWN_MS`, on `game.clock()`; no ad plays yet) stands under the purse in the forest and inside a run, in both modes, and bursts into blue glints when claimed (`gemSparkle` in `ui/flourish.ts`).
Gems buy hand slots (`deck.buySlot`: `HAND_SLOT_GEMS` in `cards.ts`, once Larger Hand is owned; `handSlots(save)` is the hand's size, 4, 5 with Larger Hand, plus `save.handSlots`) and Training resets (`training.reset`, `TRAINING_RESET_GEMS`, through `changeLoadout` like training).
`tests/gems.test.ts` covers them.
The **Shop** (`src/shop/`, `ui/shop-page.ts`; see `docs/SHOP.md`) sells offers and hands what they grant to their owners: `offers.ts` holds the catalog (`OFFERS`: the limited Ascension Shard packs, each a `bundle` of Shards and Gems bought once per `period` of Shop days, `SHARD_PACK_DAYS`, 14, from its last purchase's, the one-time packs, the Daily Free Gems, a `period` of 1, whose Claim button wears a dot while it can be claimed, and the Gem packs; **Ascension Shards**, `save.ascensionShards`, a limited currency nothing spends yet, wear `shardIcon` from `ui/dom.ts`, a green crystal, wherever shown; until the store is connected, `stubServer` confirms the Gem and shard packs' skus unpaid, `stubConfirms`), `items.ts` grants an item to its owner, `currency.ts` reads, spends and credits each currency, `rarity.ts` is the rarity table, `entitlements.ts` the permanent perks bought once (`save.entitlements`, kept by `eraseAll`; the Premium Passes `pass1` to `pass3` among them, sold as the `passes` category, which the Shop page doesn't list): `goldFactor`, which `RunPurse.gold` (`game/run-purse.ts`, through which `Game` pays all a run finds: Gold, Silver, kill loot, treasure and floors) multiplies every Gold banked by (×1.5, ×2 and ×3, multiplied together) and which returns what it banked for the popups, the Special Coin Pack's `permanentBoost`, which sets `save.trainingBoostUntil` to `BOOST_FOREVER` (on purchase, and on every load; a save without it loses a `BOOST_FOREVER` it held), and the Premium Coin Pack's `trainingRate` (×3), which `workLeft` and `doneAt` in `training-jobs.ts` take beside the boost (×6 with both);
a purchase rescales ranks in training to the new pace (`training.pace` before it, `training.repace` after), and the Training tab shows the permanent speed glowing on the trainers' row in place of the boost box.
`transactions.ts`' `purchase` makes every check (requirements, start and end, limit, the item, payment) before changing anything, then pays, grants, records (`save.shop.history`, the last 100, shown only in Dev mode) and counts (`save.shop.counts`, per Shop day for a daily offer).
`Game.buyOffer(id, serverNow, paid)` runs it on a server time confirmed for the purchase (`clock.ts`: the Shop day is the GMT day on the server's clock, the last confirmed time saved and never moved back; the page estimates between confirmations only for countdowns and the Shop button's dot).
`server.ts` is the server and store interface (`ShopServer`: time, prices by sku, purchase, open the store), stubbed by `stubServer` until they are set up; Dev free purchases grant real-money offers without it.
What a purchase or claim grants rises to the middle of the screen until pressed (`revealReward` in `ui/reward-reveal.ts`, styled like `card-reveal.ts`'s new card: `offerShown`), golden rays turning behind a permanent unlock but not behind currency; the Goals page shows its currency rewards (`goalRewardShown`) and a Premium Pass bought there the same way.
`tests/shop.test.ts` covers it.

**Spare Change** (`floorGold` in `loadout.ts`, from the Gold / Floor training row and research) pays Gold for each floor climbed for the first time in a run (`RunPurse.floorGold`: the Tower's stairs up in `advanceTowerRoom`, each new equivalent floor in `afterDelveStep`), gated by `lootedTiles` (the stairs tile's key, or the Delve's `x = -1` column with the floor) like a kill's Gold, times the research's percent and the tier's Gold bonus; `tests/floor-gold.test.ts` covers it.
Below it, **Wishing Well** pays Silver the same way (`floorSilver`, `RunPurse.floorSilver`; gated by the run's own `maxHeight` in the Tower, which only rises, since Silver is the run's and undo takes both back), **Wealthy** multiplies all Silver a run finds (`silverBonus`, through `RunPurse.silver`, which keeps Silver's fractions with `snap`) and **Loot** a kill's Gold before the tier's bonus (`killGold`, in `RunPurse.enemyLoot`); each is its training percent times its research percent, read when paid.
Their rows read as multipliers (`trainingText`: unit `×`); `tests/currency-bonus.test.ts` covers them.

**Gold bonuses** (`gold-bonuses.ts`, `goldBonuses`) list every multiplier on the Gold banked, as the player sees it: the tower's (`TIER_BONUS_TENTHS`), the ads' ×1.5 on one line (*Ad Boost* while the Gold ad's boost runs, else *Disable ads* for Ad-Disable, since only one can be active), and each Coin Pack, a bonus not applied having a null factor, shown as *Inactive*.
`goldBonusTotal` multiplies those applied.
Inside a run the HUD's height column shows the total on a line of its own between two dividers, under the floor and above the base enemy's stats (`#gold-bonus`, `renderGoldBonus` in `ui/hud.ts`); pressed, it opens the *All Gold Bonuses* box (`ui/gold-bonus-box.ts`), a plain layer rather than the shared dialog, so the game plays on behind it, and a press anywhere closes it.
In the forest, once a second tower is open, `drawEntrance` draws the tier's Gold coin, × and multiplier in a line under the numeral over the arch.

The **Tournament** (`src/tournament/`, see `docs/TOURNAMENT.md`, which also holds the build order, all built but Tickets in the purse) is a twice-weekly global competition in the Delve, opened by Tower I's floor 70 Goal (the `tournament` `GoalUnlock`): `schedule.ts` holds when each runs on the server's GMT clock (entry all of each Wednesday and Saturday, `GRACE_MS` more for runs inside, results claimable for `CLAIM_MS` from finalization; `phaseOf` is upcoming, open, ending or results, and a tournament's id is the GMT date it opens; `TournamentDesk.phase` shows a tournament the player never entered, or whose prize they claimed, as upcoming once entry closes, and every tournament once its Results are over, claimed or not, so the page and HUD show only when the next opens; a prize left unclaimed is to come as Mail, `docs/MAIL.md`), `leagues.ts` the five leagues (Copper to Champion, each playing Delve cave 1, 3, 5, 7 or 9) and who moves between them (`nextLeague`: the top `PROMOTE_PERCENT` up, the bottom `DEMOTE_PERCENT` down only from Platinum and Champion; `withinTop` measures a place by the share of entrants ahead of it, whole numbers only), `prizes.ts` the ten prize levels a league by percentile (`PRIZES`, Ascension Shards and Gems; `placeOf` counts ties at their cohort's lowest place; `oscillationFailures` checks that moving up never pays less than holding back), `tickets.ts` the Tickets (one free a tournament, then one for an ad, then `GEM_TICKET_STEP` Gems more each, starting over each tournament), `progress.ts` its save (`save.tournament`, `decodeTournament`), and `server.ts` the `TournamentServer` interface (the live tournament with its league's shared seeds, entering, submitting a score, standings, claiming), stubbed by `stubTournament`, which makes up each league's other entrants and reads the player's own record.
`TournamentDesk` (`game/tournament-desk.ts`, the game's `tournament`) takes in what the server reports (`observe`, which confirms the Shop's server time and grants the free Ticket) and runs the Tickets, standings and claims; the server's answers are fetched by the pages, so the desk never waits on the network.
A tournament run (`run.ts`, a `TournamentRun` on the `DelveRun`) is a Delve run in the league's cave, whether or not the Delve is open: `Game.beginTournament(info, entry)` spends a Ticket, switches to the Delve (`enterMode`, which `switchMode` gates) and the cave's tier (`switchTier`, so its records are the cave's) and goes in on the league's `layout` seed; the Delve `World` makes its enemies ×1.1 after the tier's factor (`tournamentCells`); `RunPurse` draws from the run's own streams (`runStream`: the `game` seed for loot, the `equipment` seed for Equipment's drops through its `equipmentRng`), each draw counted on the run so undo and reloads replay them; and `finalizeRun` keeps its score (`tournamentScore`, the deepest depth as the HUD counts it) to send and returns the player's mode and Delve tier (`back`).
`TournamentClient` (`client.ts`) does what waits on the server: refreshing, entering (`begin`), and sending scores (`tick` keeps a run inside's depth as it rises, only while it counts, `TournamentDesk.counts`: none reached after the grace does; `sendRun` as the run's end dialog opens, which shows the place; `tick` retries one that couldn't be sent).
The desk reads the server's report only while it is of the latest tournament to have opened (`live`), so the next one shows open on the clock before the server is asked; past the grace, `main.ts` asks again each minute until the results are final (`awaitingResults`), and `final` holds the final place, prize level and the league it moves the player to.
Its screens: the forest's Tournament Hall (`TOURNAMENT_HALL` and `drawTournamentHall` in `outside.ts`, `OutsideWorld`'s `hall`, `Game.atTournamentHall`), the HUD's Tournament button (`#tournament-button`, `renderTournamentButton` in `ui/hud.ts`: at the top of the forest's actions column, labelled with the phase or the countdown to the next, and in the run's menu once entered) and the Tournament page (`ui/tournament-page.ts`: the league, phase, Tickets, Begin or the ad and Gem Tickets, the standing and prize, *Claim rewards* through `TournamentClient.claim`, and All prizes); `main.ts`'s `refreshTournament` asks the server as the app starts, the page opens and the phase changes, and celebrates a new free Ticket with `revealReward`.
The UI suite fixes the page's clock (`page.clock.setFixedTime`) so the countdown is the same every run.
`tests/tournament.test.ts`, `tests/tournament-run.test.ts`, `tests/tournament-screens.test.ts` and `tests/tournament-ending.test.ts` (a tournament from entry through the grace, the results, the claim and expiry to the next) cover it; `tests/tournament-trace.test.ts` hashes every turn of seeded Copper, Silver and Champion runs, played by the hand from the stand-in's seeds, against `tests/fixtures/tournament-trace.golden.json` (regenerate it with `UPDATE_GOLDEN=1` only for an intended tournament or gameplay change), and checks two players on different game streams play the same run.
The Tournament page redraws whole only when something but its countdown changes, and as soon as the server answers.
**Mail** (`src/mail/`, see `docs/MAIL.md`) is messages the game server pushes to a player, each a subject, body and possibly items to claim (`MailItem`: a currency or Tickets; a kind this client doesn't know decodes as `unknown`, which holds the whole message back from a claim): `message.ts` holds the types, `decodeMessage` (the server's answers and the save are read alike, field by field) and how long one is kept (`kept`: `MAIL_DAYS`, 7, or while its items wait, up to `MAIL_KEEP_DAYS`, 90, the bound on stored mail), `progress.ts` its save (`save.mail`: the inbox the server last reported, the ids read and removed here not yet confirmed, `unsynced`, the ids granted here, `claimed`, so none is granted twice, and the stand-in's mailbox, `stub`; `eraseAll` keeps it all, as Mail is the server's), `server.ts` the `MailServer` interface (the inbox with the server's time, claim, mark read, hide, which refuses a message with items waiting) and `stubMail`, whose mailbox is `save.mail.stub`: Dev mail posted by `postStubMail`, and each Tournament prize left unclaimed past its claim window, posted as the window closes (`tournament:<id>`, worked out by `stubResults` in `tournament/server.ts`, which the Tournament stand-in uses too), and `client.ts` the `MailClient` (refresh, open, hide, claim, and syncing what was read and removed).
`MailDesk` (`game/mail-desk.ts`, the game's `mail`) takes in the inbox (`receive`, keeping what was done here and not yet confirmed), lists what shows (`recent`, newest first), the Mail button's dot (`unread`), and opens, grants and removes.
Its screens: the HUD's Mail button (`#mail-button`, an envelope, `mailIcon` in `ui/dom.ts`, under Settings in the forest's actions column, `renderMailButton` in `ui/hud.ts`: shown only in the forest while there is mail to show, with a dot while any is unread) and the Mail popup (`ui/mail-dialog.ts`, `MailDialog`, in the shared dialog: the list, each row an unread dot, the subject, a gift box, `giftIcon`, while items wait, how long ago, and an X only on a message with nothing to claim; and a message, its date in GMT, body, items, and *Claim*, which closes the dialog for `revealReward`'s `mailShown` and opens it again on the message when pressed away; a phone's Back goes from a message to the list; every word from the server goes through `escapeHtml`).
`main.ts`'s `refreshMail` asks the server as the app starts, as the player arrives in the forest, every `MailClient.POLL_MS` (5 minutes) there, as the popup opens, and as the Tournament's phase changes (a prize left unclaimed comes as its Results end); the `mailDebug.send({ subject, body, items }?)` console helper (`debug-hooks.ts`) posts to the stand-in's mailbox (`OUTAGE_MAIL` when given nothing).
`tests/mail.test.ts` covers it.
**Missions** (`src/missions/`, see `docs/MISSIONS.md`) are daily missions and the week's rewards for completing them: `catalog.ts` holds each kind (`MISSIONS`: its text, target and `offered(save)`, never before what it asks for is unlocked: strong and elite enemies and blue and red keys once some open tower's record, or the Delve's equivalent floor, reaches their first floor, `enemyFirstFloor` and `keyFirstFloor`; Buy 1 badge with the Badges skill and copies left; run training with On the Job; Focus with Focus), what a mission pays (`missionReward`: 3 Gems, Gold by the highest tower open, `MISSION_GOLD`, and its equipment material, `MISSION_MATERIALS`) and the weekly rewards (`WEEKLY_REWARDS`, one every 5 missions claimed up to 35: Gold a multiple of a mission's, Gems, **Medals**, `save.medals`, a limited currency nothing spends yet, a `CURRENCIES` entry, and Ascension Shards), and `progress.ts` the save (`save.missions`: the list, the 8-hour period missions were last given in, `missionPeriod`, from 00:00 GMT, the saved stream they are drawn from, seeded once from `stream("missions")`, the week, `missionWeek`, from Monday 00:00 GMT, with its tally of missions claimed and rewards claimed, and what has counted) and `decodeMissions`.
`MissionDesk` (`game/mission-desk.ts`, the game's `missions`) runs them on the Shop's estimated server time: `refresh` (main's start and once-a-second tick) gives two missions a period begun, the first two at once, while the list holds fewer than 8, those completed and waiting to be claimed included (`MISSION_CAPACITY`, `full`), each of a kind offered and with no incomplete mission of it; `record(type, amount, key)` counts toward the incomplete mission of a kind, a kill or pickup once a tile (`counted`, keyed by mode and the mode's loot key, so undo can't count it twice); `mark` counts what undo takes back (run training ranks, Focus uses) only past the run's most (`marks`, by mode and run seed); `claim` pays a mission and counts it in the week's tally, and `claimWeekly` pays a weekly reward.
`Game` counts kills by strength (`countKill`, from `winFight`), potions and blue and red keys picked up (`collect`), floors new to the run (`advanceTowerRoom`, `payDelveFloors`), run training (`trainInRun`) and Focus (`focus`); the Training and Badge desks count ranks and draws through the `DeskHost`'s `missionDone`.
Its screens: the HUD's Missions button (`#missions-button`, `missionIcon` in `ui/dom.ts`, `renderMissionsButton` in `ui/hud.ts`: a check mark over the missions in the list out of 8, complete or not, with a dot while `missions.waiting`; in the forest's actions column under Settings and Mail, inside a run in the run's menu) and the Missions screen (`ui/missions-page.ts`, `MissionsPage`, the `missions` page: an X back to the page that opened it, the weekly bar with a prize box over every fifth mission, dim, glowing to claim or checked, and the missions, each with its progress bar or *Complete!* and Claim; claims rise through `revealReward`, a weekly reward's currencies one after another, each pressed away to show the next).
`tests/missions.test.ts` covers it.
