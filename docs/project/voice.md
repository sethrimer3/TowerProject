# Voice guide

How the game's text should sound: help panels, tutorials, the Codex, tooltips, messages and the DEVLOG.
Read it before writing or editing anything the player reads.

## The voice

Write like a person explaining the game to a friend sitting next to them.
Short, plain and concrete: say what happens and what the player does, then stop.
Vary sentence length; a short sentence after a long one reads as human, while a page of identically shaped sentences reads as generated.

## Rules

- **One idea per sentence.** If a sentence needs a colon or a semicolon to carry a second idea, make it two sentences.
  Use at most one colon in a paragraph, and only where it introduces a label or a list (`Rates: …`, `Next level: …`).
- **No em dashes (—) in player text.** Use a full stop, a comma, a colon for a label, or brackets.
  A lone "—" meaning "no value" in a table cell is fine.
- **Put the reader first.** Prefer "Each level adds 2%" to "a share of the Silver, 2% a level, up to all of it at level 50".
  Lead with the effect; give the numbers in their own short sentence.
- **Natural word order.** Write "Relics you own are on the Relics screen", not "The relics you own wait on the Relics screen".
  If a phrase sounds like a translation, rewrite it.
- **No filler or hype.** Drop "unique", "powerful", "special", "mighty", "seamless", "unleash", "embark", "journey", "realm", "Enjoy!", "Don't miss it!".
  Say what the thing does instead.
- **No cheering.** Keep game feedback ("LEVEL UP!", "Research Complete!"), but don't praise the player for using the interface ("Nicely done!").

## Stock phrases to avoid

These crept in through repetition and now read as a tic.

| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| for good | permanently, you keep it, never lost |
| at once (meaning immediately) | right away, instantly, on the spot |
| beside the board | next to the board |
| X opens in the Archives: research it, and … | X opens in the Archives. Once researched, … |
| waits on, waits in | is on, is in |
| in the Tower and the Delve alike | in both the Tower and the Delve |
| whatever / however it is raised | no matter how it's raised |

"At once" meaning "together" ("research several projects at once") is fine.

## Before and after

> Warp Loot opens in the Archives: research it, and a climb that starts above floor 1 (a Warp, or a Retry from one) goes inside with a share of the Silver its skipped floors would have paid, 2% a level, up to all of it at level 50.

> Warp Loot opens in the Archives. When a climb starts above floor 1, from a Warp or a Retry after one, it brings some of the Silver the skipped floors would have paid.
> Each level adds 2%, so level 50 brings all of it.

> Provisions are bought with Gold and kept for good: each one you own is one use in every climb, pressed from its button beside the board when you need it.

> Provisions cost Gold and you keep them permanently. Each one you own gives one use in every climb, from its button next to the board.

## Checking your text

Read it aloud.
If you run out of breath, split the sentence; if it sounds like a manual or an advert, simplify it.
Search what you wrote for "—", ": " and the phrases above before committing.
