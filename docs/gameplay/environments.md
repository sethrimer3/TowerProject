# Floor Environments

A floor may be given an environment: a rule for the whole floor, which changes how it looks, what it holds or what it pays.
In the Delve, each equivalent floor rolls its own.
Each environment has its own chance, fixed for a run as it goes inside, and is rolled once for each floor, so a floor has its environments from the moment it is generated and undo can't roll them again.
Environments overlap: a floor can have several at once.
A floor with none is a plain floor.

## Treasure Floor

Chest's epic upgrade, **Treasure Floor**, gives each floor +0.5% chance a level (up to 5 levels) to be one.
Every ATK and DEF shard and potion on a Treasure Floor is a treasure chest instead.

## Golden Floor

A **Golden Floor** multiplies every Gold found on it by **Golden Floor Multiplier**: a kill's, a treasure chest's, an item's, door's or torch's, a badge's (Gold Touch), a smelt's, Spare Change for climbing onto it and what Floor Skip Reward pays for skipping it.
The multiplier counts once for each find, whatever else raises it, and the All Gold Bonuses box shows it as the Golden Floor line while the hero stands on one.
A Golden Floor that is also a Treasure Floor makes its many chests worth all the more.

**Golden Floors** (3 Breakthrough, under More Wealth, above Rehearsed steps) opens two Archives projects:
- **Golden Floor Chance**: +0.5% chance each floor is a Golden Floor a level, for 60 levels, up to 30%. A climb keeps the chance it went inside with, so a level done mid-climb counts from the next.
- **Golden Floor Multiplier**: ×2 at first, +×0.1 a level, for 80 levels, up to ×10. A level done mid-climb counts at once.

Both are among the dearest research there is: their first levels cost 5,000 Gold and take ten minutes, their last 2.51 billion Gold and 30 days (Chance) and 5.02 billion and 40 days (Multiplier), before Research Speed and Research Cost Discount.

A Golden Floor's ground is gold.
Gold belongs to Golden Floors: on the Game themes whose ground is black or without hue, the walls and their edges turn gold instead.

## The enemy stats box

Once any environment has a chance, the enemy stats box ends with an **Environments** table: each environment the run can roll, its chance, and a mark in its **This Floor** column when the floor the hero stands on has it.
