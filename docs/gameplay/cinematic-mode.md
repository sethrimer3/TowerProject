# Cinematic mode

**Cinematic mode** hides every control, stat, bar and menu inside a run, leaving the board alone on black, as large as the screen allows, the hero playing on through the floors.
Turn it on with **Cinematic mode** on the Settings page; two options appear under it.
For now the pinch and the mouse wheel zoom the board instead (below), so outside Battery saver it enters only by idling.
In Battery saver, inside a run, pinch the board in with two fingers (drawn together, as when zooming a photo out) to enter it; on a computer, hold Ctrl and scroll down over the board.
With **Cinematic mode after 2 minutes idle** on, it also enters on its own after two minutes without a tap, key or scroll while the hand plays, and dims the board against screen burn-in; any input lights it again.
With **Show the Gem button in cinematic mode** on, the ad's Gems stay in the top left corner while they can be claimed.
Tapping a floating Gem on the board collects it without leaving.
Any other tap shows a **Back** button in the top right corner for a few seconds; pressing it leaves cinematic mode, back to the whole page.
To leave, pinch the board out (fingers spread apart), tap three times quickly, press Escape, or hold Ctrl and scroll up.
It also leaves when anything takes the board away: the run ending, the hero falling, a dialog or a lesson, or another page.
It is never kept: a reload shows the whole page.

## Zooming the board

Scroll the mouse wheel over the board, or spread two fingers on it, to zoom in on the point under the pointer or the fingers.
Each step keeps the art's pixels crisp, and the last is the first such step past ×4.
While zoomed, drag the board to look around it; a drag never taps a tile.
Scroll back or pinch the fingers together to zoom out.
