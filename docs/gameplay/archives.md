# The Archives

## Archivists

**Archives** (5 Inspiration, after Larger Hand; Into the depths, 3 Inspiration, follows it) adds an Archives tab to the Research page, after Training: research that lasts between runs, paid in Gold and real time.
An archivist works on one project at a time, a level at a time: pay the level's Gold, and it completes on the clock, whether the game is open, on another page, or closed.
A busy archivist shows the time its research has left down to the second (*7h 41m 33s*), ticking each second.
You start with one archivist and can hire up to four more for Gems (100, 400, 1,400 and 3,000).

## The first projects

Two projects open with the Archives themselves, listed first: **Research Speed** makes all research 2% faster a level, for 100 levels (at level 50, 200% speed: a 48-hour level takes 24), shortening research already under way the moment a level completes; **Research Cost Discount** takes 0.3% off the Gold of all research started later, a level, for 100 levels (30% off at the end).
Both cost 40 Gold and 1 minute at level 1, up to 20 million Gold and 100 days at level 100 (before Research Speed shortens it).

## Switching and Rush

Research once begun can't be stopped, only switched to another project.
Pressing a busy archivist opens Select Research too, its own research marked Researching; choosing another asks to switch, and switching gives the old research's Gold back, keeps the time already spent on it for when you start it again (paying again), and starts the new.
**Rush**, beside the time left, completes the research at once for Gems: 1 Gem for each 10 minutes left, and 1 more for any part of 10 minutes.
Each project completed (in a gold box) makes Rush 1.5% more efficient for good: with 10 completed, Rush's Gems are divided by 1.15 and rounded up, so 100 Gems become 87.
Once any project is completed, the Archives heading shows it at its left, opposite History: *+1.5% to Rush* for one.
With **Auto-continue** ticked (the box with the looping arrow, as on the Training tab), an archivist starts the project's next level as soon as one completes, if you have the Gold; otherwise it waits idle.
With Dev **free purchases** on, research completes the moment it starts, so auto-continue waits instead: each press researches one level.

## Cells and Speed Up

**Cells** (a yellow triangle) drop from every Greater Boss: 1 in Tower I, and from 1 to the tower's number on later towers, evenly (an Effective or Dampen badge on the card that took the fight scales the drop, like its Gold); the Archives tab shows the Cells held beside its History button.
**Speed Up**, after a busy archivist's time left, spends them: its dialog picks how long (1, 4, 8 or 24 hours) and how fast (1.5x, 2x, 3x or 4x, at 15, 75, 450 and 3,150 Cells an hour), shows the Cells held over the price, and **Queue Speed Up** buys the same Speed Up again each time it ends while the archivist is busy and the Cells are there (in the dialog of a Speed Up in progress, only that box can change).
While a queued Speed Up is in progress, its dialog shows it (*SPEED UP IN PROGRESS · 2x 1h*) and lets you pick another length and speed: **Next Speed Up** keeps that choice, which is bought in its place as it ends (its Cells paid then, if you have them) and queued again after that; unticking Queue forgets it.
While one lasts the research left counts down that many times as fast (at 4x, a second every quarter of a second), with the factor and the time the Speed Up has left in green beside it (*2x 49m 57s*); it belongs to the archivist, so the next level auto-continued keeps it, and only one runs at a time.
It counts down only while the archivist researches: an idle archivist's Speed Up is paused (its row shows it, *2x 40m · paused*) until its next research starts, and a queued one is never bought again while idle.
Switching away keeps the research done, not the time passed, and Rush prices the research left.

## The library

Each level completed pops up at the top of the screen, over everything, as "Research Complete!" with the project and level, for four seconds before it slides up out of sight; levels that complete together (such as while the game was closed) follow one another in order.
The library lists only research whose requirements are met (Dev mode lists it all), and can be searched and filtered by category and status, and its History button opens a list of the levels completed lately.
Research at its most levels stays in the list in a gold box, marked Complete; once any is, a **Hide Completed** box at the top leaves those out.
Each project's box is kept short: its name, categories and level on one line, its description, then what its next level changes, how long it takes and its Research button, which shows only its Gold price, on one line.
