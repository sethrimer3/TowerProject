# Sound

The game is silent until the first press or key, which is when browsers let a page make sound.
**Sound effects** on the Settings page (on by default) turns every sound on or off; every UI theme sounds the same.
**Weather sounds** (on by default) turns the forest's wind, chimes and thunder on or off on its own.

## Effects

- Buttons knock, the tab row's tabs sound like stone, and a dialog's confirming button has its own sound.
- Picking up an ATK shard, a DEF shard, a potion or a key each has its sound, and so does a chest creaking open.
- A door sounds by the keys it takes: one, two, or three and more.
- A Gem, a Golden Gem and a Stellar Gem each sound as they are collected.
- Fights sound their blows, arrows and falls; a Bomb explodes; Phoenix Spark sounds as it shrugs off a defeating hit; the Lightning Slime's burst sounds as it shocks.
- Using the Traveler's elixir, or any other provision, has its sound.
- The hero levelling up, a card levelling up and badges drawn each sound.
- Walking into the Tower and into the Delve's cave each sound, and a Warp has its own.
- The hand sounds when it finds nothing it can do, the infinity loop as a run begins again, and the story as the game begins.
- Opening the Equipment screen, claiming a Tournament prize and the Gem bank filling (with its chime setting on) each sound.

## Ambience

- Outside, the wind blows through the trees, and on a cloudy or stormy day wind chimes ring with it.
- In the forest clearing, the Blacksmith works at his forge, louder the closer the hero stands.
- At the lake, soft meditative music plays, and water laps at the shore, louder the closer the hero is to the fisherman.
  The music plays on at sea, sailing with the fisherman.
- On floor 999999, the [Divine Portal](first-steps.md#the-divine-portal)'s room, only the Divine Relic's hum plays, louder the closer the hero is to its chest; opening the chest plays its own fanfare.
- Inside the Tower and the Delve there is no ambience; Battery saver plays none.
