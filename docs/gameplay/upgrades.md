# Upgrades

Each Inspiration or Breakthrough skill that can be bought wears its next rank's price in a circle on its corner, filled when the currency is there and outlined when not, so a path's cost shows at a glance.
A **?** beside the Inspiration and Breakthrough trees' headings explains how that currency is earned: Inspiration by a Tower run (and, once you have climbed past floor 100 or 1,000, the slower rates beyond them), and a Breakthrough point from each Tower Goal claimed.
The Breakthrough tree's skills, like the Inspiration tree's, are each bought once, so they show no ranks.
**Critical** (2 Inspiration, after Trainers, before the Archives) opens **Crit %** and **Crit x** training and their research: each strike of the hero may be a critical hit (see Critical hits in [CONTEXT.md](../../CONTEXT.md)), its ATK multiplied by the critical factor before the enemy's DEF comes off.

The Upgrades page shows a tab only once what unlocks it is owned: Inspiration from the start, then Training, Breakthrough (with the Breakthrough skill), the Archives, and Legacy, Wisdom and Renown (Dev mode only for now).
The Inspiration and Breakthrough tabs read the points held under their names (over 9,999 shortened), as the Research page's Training tab reads its points, so the row of tabs shows even while Inspiration is the only one.
What opens Wayfinding, the Delve Automove skills, is still to be decided, so for now only Dev mode shows it; Dev mode shows them all.
