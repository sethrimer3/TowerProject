# Towers, Areas and Goals

## Higher towers

Beating the boss on floor 100 makes floor 100's Goals checkpoint claimable, and claiming it opens **Tower II**; floor 100's checkpoint there opens Tower III, up to Tower IX.
Each tower opened opens the Delve's cave of the same number.
Each one's monsters have more HP, ATK and DEF than the one before: five times in Tower II, four times Tower II's in III (20 times Tower I's), and three times the one before in each tower after, so a hero needs about that many times the ATK, DEF and HP to climb as far.
Each pays more Gold: ×2 in Tower II, ×3.1 in III, ×4.3 in IV, then ×5.6, ×7, ×8.5, ×10.1 and ×11.8 in IX; and its kills pay XP times the same factor as the monsters' stats (×5 in Tower II, ×20 in III …), so the hero levels faster too.
Silver stays the same, so a run buys about the same run training, while potions stay the same too (only upgrades will raise those).
ATK and DEF shards raise their stat by 1 in Tower I and by 2.75 times as much in each tower after (2.75 in II, 7.56 in III, …).
The floors are laid out as in Tower I, but blue, red and Heart Doors (and blue and red keys) come earlier in each tower.
Once a second one is open, arrows on either side of the board in the Tower's forest choose which the next run enters; its numeral glows over the doorway, and the board's subtitle names it with its Gold & XP bonus.
The Delve's forest has no arrows: it always shows the cave its tournament run will play, its league's (*Delve 3+* in Silver).
Each keeps its own records: its highest floor, the Inspiration its new floors pay (again from its first floor) and its areas mastered and cleared, and the Goals screen shows each tower's checkpoints apart.

## Areas

Each ten Tower floors ending at a checkpoint (1–10, 11–20 …) are an area, judged as the hero climbs onto the next area's first floor.
An area climbed without taking damage in a fight (a Heart Door's toll doesn't count) is **mastered**, which lets runs warp to the next checkpoint once Warp is owned; one whose every floor you leave with no monster on it is **cleared**, which pays its floors' Inspiration again: 10 to floor 100, 1 an area to floor 1,000, then 1 for each area ending at a hundredth floor to floor 10,000; an area that would pay nothing (the rest past floor 1,000, and every one past 10,000) isn't cleared at all, with no chest (areas past floor 100, which have no checkpoint yet, can be cleared but not mastered).
Each pays the moment it is earned, once per area in each tower, and a chest for each (gold for mastered, silver for cleared) then stands directly in front of the hero; opening it pays nothing more but bursts into gold with *AREA MASTERED* and *Checkpoint Warp Unlocked*, or *ENEMIES CLEARED* and the Inspiration it paid.
Undo never takes an area's reward back.

## Goals

In the forest, the Tower's **Goals** button (with a red dot while a reward in an open tower waits to be claimed) opens the Goals screen: the tower drawn as a stone column rising from the ground, a line up its middle lit to the highest floor completed in it (shown at the bottom, *Floor 35*; a floor counts as completed once its stairs are climbed, so a tower not yet climbed shows *Floor 0*), and a checkpoint every ten floors to 100, every 50 floors to 300, then at 400, 500, 750, 1,000, 1,250, 1,500, 2,000, 2,500 and 4,500.
Each checkpoint has a reward on the left, claimed by tapping it once its floor is completed (floor 10's once floor 11 is reached), and a premium reward on the right, claimed the same way with that set's **Premium Pass**: Pass 1 opens Towers I–III ($9.99), Pass 2 IV–VI ($19.99), Pass 3 VII–IX ($29.99); the pass button at the top right, or a premium reward tapped without it, offers the pass with the Gems and other rewards it opens.
Claiming a checkpoint's reward on the left also pays one **Breakthrough point**, unsaid on the screen: a Breakthrough icon and *+1* rise off the reward as it is claimed (a premium reward pays none).
A reward whose floor isn't completed yet shows faded with a lock, a claimed one faded with a check, one ready to claim bright.
With **Dev: free purchases** on, tapping a locked reward claims it anyway (a premium one still needs its pass).

## Checkpoint unlocks

Tower I's checkpoints at floors 10, 20, 30, 50, 60 and 70 unlock tools (floor 40 pays 20 Gems, floor 80 25 Gems, floor 90 2,000 Gold, floor 150 opens **Research Speed** in the Archives, floor 200 pays 30 Gems, floor 250 opens **Note Taking** in the Archives, for [Run Notes](runs.md#run-notes), and floor 1,500 pays 5 Ascension Shards, 15 with the pass).
Before floor 10's **Damage Prediction**, a monster's inspect panel shows only its HP, ATK and DEF; after it, also the HP the fight would cost and whether the hero survives it ("26 damage · Survivable", "0 damage · Harmless" when it costs no HP, "LETHAL", or "Instakill" when the hero's first strike fells it).
Floor 20's **Combat Forecast** adds a line saying how many of the hero's hits defeat it ("5 hits to defeat", or "Instakill" when Damage Prediction isn't owned to say so); floor 30's **Attack Lore** adds another with the fewest whole points of ATK more that would defeat it in one hit fewer ("+3 ATK: 4 hits"); and Tower II's floor 40 **Warp**: tapping the floor number of a checkpoint whose area is mastered, along with every checkpoint area below it in that tower (they glow, with a golden swirl at their left; a mastered checkpoint above one not yet mastered keeps its swirl greyed out and can't be warped to) then asks to begin a new run at once on the floor just above it (floor 31 for the floor 30 checkpoint), at full HP; tapping one not mastered says what warping takes.
Floor 50's **Damage Visual** draws that cost in the lower-left corner of every monster on the board, without tapping it, shortened to fit (1.2K): red when lethal (∞ when the hero can't hurt it), a gray 0 for an Instakill, a white 0 when the monster strikes but takes no HP, a blue +N when Lifesteal would heal the hero more than the fight hurts (counted in full even at max HP, so it shows what the fight would heal), gold otherwise.
It also draws, in the same corner, what each item gives, rounded down to a whole number: a potion's HP in blue, an ATK or DEF shard's stat in white, and a treasure chest's Gold (what it pays on average; none once its Gold is paid) in yellow.
The Settings page's Toggles menu turns each half off: *Damage on enemies* (with the Wooden Doors') and *Item amounts*.
Floor 60's **Equipment** opens the Blacksmith (see [Equipment](gear-and-equipment.md#equipment)).
Tower II's floor 10 checkpoint, **Relative Damage Color**, then colours those numbers by the share of the hero's current HP each fight would cost: bright green below 1%, sliding through yellow at 10% and orange at 25% to red at 50% or more (an Instakill stays gray, a lethal fight red).
Tower II's floors 20 and 30 open the Archives' **Shard ATK** and **Shard DEF** research: every ATK shard a run picks up gives 2% more ATK a level, and every DEF shard 3% more DEF a level, for 100 levels each, priced and timed like Potion HP, counting from the next shard the moment a level completes.
Tower II's floor 50 pays 25 Gems.
Tower II's floor 80 opens the Archives' **Training Discount** research: every rank a trainer trains costs 0.5% less Gold a level, for 99 levels, up to 49.5% off (see [Trainers](training.md#trainers)).
Tower II's floor 90 opens **Card Groups** on the Deck page (see [Card Groups](hand-and-cards.md#card-groups)).
Tower III's floor 10 adds the **Archer** card to the deck: while the hand holds it, the hero is an **Archer**, its arrows shooting the closest monster in reach every 10 turns, and floors 20 and 30 open the **Attack Range** and **Attack Speed** Training rows that improve them ([Fights](fights.md#the-archer)).
Floor 50 opens the **ATK / m** Training row (arrows strike harder the further off their target), floor 60 the Archives' **Charged Shot** research (an arrow that waits ready grows stronger), floor 70 the **Multishot Chance** and **Multishot Targets** Training rows (a shot may fire at several enemies at once), and floor 80 the **Rapid Fire Chance** and **Rapid Fire Duration** Training rows (a shot may make the arrows come four times as fast for a few turns); floor 90 pays 30 Gems.
Tower III's floor 150 opens the Archives' **Warp Loot** research: a run that starts above floor 1 (a Warp, or a Retry from one) goes inside with a share of the Silver its skipped floors would have paid, 2% a level for 50 levels, up to all of it, and *WARP LOOT* bursts over the hero with the Silver added.
The skipped floors' Silver is estimated as the run begins, from the training and research it starts with: each floor's monsters by the usual mix of strengths (and the boss on every tenth floor), the Wishing Well's Silver / Floor, both raised by Silver found, and Interest on the Silver held, up to Max Interest a floor.
Its levels cost Dash's Gold for the first 25 (250 Gold for the first, 105 billion for the 25th), then 40% more each (about 470 trillion for the 50th), and take Dash's time in all, spread over the 50 (26 minutes for the first, 4 days for the 25th, 76 days for the 50th).
The name under the hero's portrait follows: a Wayfarer, a Pathfinder once Tower II is open, an Archer while the hand holds the Archer card.
Tower V's floor 10 adds the **Flying Enemy** card to the deck ([The hand](hand-and-cards.md)).
Tower V's floor 30 opens the Archives' **Parry %** research (once the Parry skill is owned): +0.2% Parry % a level for 50 levels (5,000 Gold and 53 minutes for the first, about 6 billion Gold and 87 hours for the last), taking effect the moment a level completes; however it is raised, a parry cuts a strike's damage by at most 98%.
Floor 100's checkpoint in Towers I to VIII unlocks the next tower (and the Delve's cave of the same number); beating floor 100's boss makes it claimable.
Tapping a claimed unlock shows what it does again.
A green check beside a checkpoint marks its area cleared.
Entering the tower from the forest always starts on floor 1.
Every tower has its own checkpoints; once a second is open, arrows at the bottom pan between them.
Tower I's premium rewards, floor by floor: 100 Gold, 10 Gems, 10 Gems, 300 Gold, 15 Gems, 20 Gems, 600 Gold, 30 Gems, 30 Gems, 50 Gems (floor 100), 15,000 Gold, 35 Gems, 30,000 Gold, 25 Gems, 100,000 Gold, then 30, 35, 40 and 45 Gems (floors 500 to 1,250), 10 Ascension Shards (floor 1,500), 50 Gems (2,000), 15 Ascension Shards (2,500) and 20 Ascension Shards (4,500).
The other towers' rewards past their unlocks are placeholders for now (Gold on the left, 5 Gems a checkpoint on the right, rising by 5 with each); the store isn't connected yet, so only Dev mode (or its free purchases) unlocks a pass.
The Delve has no Goals yet.
End Run shows only inside a run.

With Settings → **Sprites** on, Goal rewards and their locked, claimed, mastered and cleared marks use matching pixel-art PNGs.
The option defaults to off, and non-default UI themes supply their own look; reward names, amounts and floor numbers stay live text.
