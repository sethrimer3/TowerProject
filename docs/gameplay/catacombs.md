# The Catacombs

The Catacombs are an idle area under the forest, where your hero, as the Magician, fights undead a turn a second ([plan](../CATACOMBS.md)).
The Codex's Catacombs tab lists each undead once you reach a hall it can appear in.
Tower VI's floor 20 Goal, **Catacombs**, reveals the Inspiration skill **Reveal Catacombs** (40 Inspiration).

**The Mausoleum.** Once Reveal Catacombs is owned, a stone Mausoleum stands in the lower-right corner of the Tower's forest.
Tap it to enter the Catacomb of the tower the forest shows: Catacomb III from Tower III's forest, and so on.
Entering the Catacomb your run is in carries on with that run.
Entering another starts a new run there at Hall 1, and asks first when your run is past Hall 1.
You keep everything you have earned either way.

**The Catacomb button.** Once a Catacomb run is under way, the menu has a Catacomb button, in the forest and during a climb.
It shows the Catacomb your run is in and its hall, whatever tower you are in.
It is not offered in the Proving Grounds.

**The Catacomb screen.** Entering, or pressing the Catacomb button, opens the Catacomb your run is in.
Your climb waits behind it, paused, and **Switch Back** (or Escape) returns to it, playing on from where it was.
The Magician walks each hall left to right, a turn a second: a dash to the next undead, then a round of spells, the Magician first; then the Lore tablet in the middle, any Ingots in the treasure rooms, the rest of the undead, and the stairs down.
Deeper halls bring stronger undead: Skeletons from Hall 11, Ghouls from 25, Vampires from 49, Liches from 80 and Demons from 200.
Some halls are always the same, such as Hall 45's six Wisps and Hall 395's one of each from Ghost to Demon.
A fall starts the run again at Hall 1 with full MHP; MXP, Lore, Spirit and Ingots are kept.
The hall shows "The hero rises and tries again..." as it does.
The HUD shows the Magician's MHP, MAtk and MDef, the Magic level and its MXP, unspent magic skill points in the portrait's corner, Lore in place of Silver, the hall in place of the floor, and the Spirit you hold in place of the Gold bonus.
The enemy box shows a Wisp here; tap it for every undead the hall can hold, its chance, stats and rewards, and the Lore bonus.
An undead's HP bar shows over it once it is struck, as in a climb.
The Catacombs play on while you are elsewhere in the game.

**Skills.** The Catacomb screen has four menus, tabs along the bottom in place of the hand: Skills, Spirit, Forge and Academy.
Each Magic level past the first gives a magic skill point.
Level 2 takes 240 MXP, and each level after takes a fifth more than the one before.
In Skills, tap a skill to open it: − and + move a point out of it or into it, at any time, and its lines show what a point gives and what the skill gives in all.
Each skill takes up to its cap of points, and each point gives three things:

| Skill | Each point | Cap |
|---|---|---|
| Might | +1 MAtk, +1% MAtk, +3% MCrit factor | 50 |
| Weaving | +50 Max MHP, +1% MCrit chance, +0.2% Speed Drop chance | 50 |
| Resonance | +4% Spirit, +0.3% Loot Drop chance, +2 Armor Penetration | 25 |
| Focus | +5% MXP, +0.3% MXP Drop chance, +3% Armor Penetration | 25 |
| Fortune | +2% MCrit chance, +0.2% to every Drop chance, +0.5% Gold Crosshair chance | 25 |

An MCrit multiplies what gets past MDef by the MCrit factor, ×1.5 to start.
MAtk at or under an undead's MDef does nothing, however it crits, until Armor Penetration lowers the MDef.
Armor Penetration ignores a share of an undead's MDef, then a flat amount.
A point moved counts from the next turn; taking points out of Weaving can lower your MHP, but putting them back never heals.
The ≡ button lists every Catacomb stat in one table, with each Drop's chance and what it gives; tap anywhere to put it away.
Forge and Academy open in later updates.

**Drops.** As a hall is made, each undead may roll Drops, shown as native crystal, pouch, boot or heart marks beside it, and beating it pays them all.
An MXP Drop (purple) gives ×3 to ×5 MXP from that undead, and a Loot Drop (green) ×2 to ×5 Spirit.
A Speed Drop (yellow) gives 10 to 110 attacks at double speed, which add up and are kept when you fall.
An MHP Drop (red) gives back 30 to 100 MHP, never past your maximum.
Every Drop's chance starts at 0% and grows with Skills.

**Crosshairs and potions.** While you watch, you can tap the hall for a little extra.
Neither appears until Unlock Ability opens it: potions with Spirit Potion, crosshairs with Zap.
Each unlock shows a short lesson over the hall; tap it to put it away.
Each turn, an undead may gain a crosshair, which turns slowly over it.
Tap it (or the wall just above or below the undead) for a free hit: a thunderbolt strikes it, which can be an MCrit and spends no turn.
A Gold Crosshair hits three times as hard; Fortune gives the chance of one.
Each turn, a potion may also appear on a tile beside the corridor.
Tap it for 10 MHP; it is lost if you leave the hall first.

An undead's Drops show as small icons in a row over it, each edged in light green.
The Lore tablet and the Ingots are edged in gold, as treasure is in a climb.
Numbers, heals and drops rise off the hall as they do in a climb, with the same sounds.

**What a tile holds.** Tap any tile of the hall to see what is there, in the same box a climb shows.
An undead's box shows its MHP, its MAtk with what each of its strikes takes off you, its MDef with what each of your strikes takes off it, and the MXP and Spirit it pays.
Under them come its Drops, its crosshair, and the forecast: what the fight would cost you, whether you survive it, and the hits it takes, without MCrits.
Tap the same tile again to put the box away; a wall's or floor's goes by itself.

**Spirit.** The Spirit menu spends Spirit on upgrades, each level priced in one rarity of Spirit.
Unlock Ability opens something new each level: Spirit Potion for 10 Common Spirit, Spell Surge for 20 Uncommon, Zap for 100 Common, Renewing Rush for 75 Rare and Arcane Barrage for 150 Epic.

| Upgrade | Each level | Levels | Spirit |
|---|---|---|---|
| MHP +10 | +10 Max MHP | 35 | Common, from 25 |
| Magic ATK +1 | +1 MAtk | 25 | Uncommon, from 50 |
| Magic Armor Pierce +1 | the undead you strike have 1 less MDef | 25 | Uncommon, from 75 |
| MXP Gain +2% | +2% MXP | 25 | Uncommon, from 100 |
| MCrit Chance +0.25%, MCrit Damage +1% | +0.25% MCrit chance, +1% MCrit factor | 25 | Uncommon, from 200 |
| Max MHP +20, MHP Drop Chance +0.05% | +20 Max MHP, +0.05% MHP Drop chance | 20 | Rare, from 200 |
| Magic ATK +2 | +2 MAtk | 20 | Rare, from 300 |
| Loot Drop Gain +0.3× | ×0.3 more Spirit from each Loot Drop | 10 | Rare, from 450 |
| Spell Surge: MAtk & MCrit Damage +2%, Cooldown -1 turn | Spell Surge's own +20% MAtk and +100% MCrit damage each 2% more, its cooldown a turn shorter | 15 | Rare, from 600 |

MXP Gain multiplies Focus's MXP bonus; the others add to what Skills give.
Magic Armor Pierce adds to Armor Penetration, so past an undead's MDef it does nothing more.

Most upgrades open only once you have reached a hall, in any Catacomb: Magic Armor Pierce at Hall 5, MXP Gain at 9, MCrit at 13, Max MHP +20 at 17, Magic ATK +2 at 21, Loot Drop Gain at 23 and Spell Surge's at 25.
The menu shows the next ones to open, saying the hall that opens them, and under them *Reveal More With Next Unlock* while more wait.
Once an upgrade is maxed, a Hide Maxed box appears at the top of the list; tick it to hide the maxed ones.
The ≡ stats table shows the deepest hall you have reached in any Catacomb as Max hall reached.

**Abilities.** Each ability unlocked has a button over the menus.
Tap it to cast; then the number under it counts down the turns until it is ready again.
Hover over it, or hold it, to see what it does.
The slim Auto button above it casts it by itself whenever it is ready.

| Ability | For the next 5 attacks | Cooldown |
|---|---|---|
| Spell Surge | +20% MAtk and +100% MCrit damage | 60 turns |
| Renewing Rush | +100% attack speed, and 50 MHP when cast | 120 turns |
| Arcane Barrage | every undead in the hall also takes 20% of a hit past its MDef | 90 turns |

**Banked time.** Time the game is closed is banked, a second at a time, and the Welcome Back dialog says how many turns, and shown over the hall's top right with a speed choice: ×0 (Pause), ×1, ×2, ×5, ×10, ×25 or ×100.
Above ×1, each extra turn spends a banked second; when the bank runs out the speed drops back to ×1.
At ×0 no turn is played, and each second goes to the bank instead.
Leaving the Catacomb screen while paused sets it playing at ×1; a faster speed keeps spending the bank while you are away.

**Spirit and Ingots.** Each undead beaten drops Spirit of its rarity, and treasure rooms hold Ingots; both are kept for good and listed in the currencies table once the Catacombs are open.

The Mausoleum, undead, Spirit, Ingots and menu tabs use native pixel artwork matching the main game.
An undead's active Drops have distinct marks beside it: a purple crystal for MXP, green pouch for Loot, gold boot for Speed and red heart for MHP.
