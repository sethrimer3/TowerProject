# The Catacombs

The Catacombs are an idle area still being built ([plan](../CATACOMBS.md)).
Tower VI's floor 20 Goal, **Catacombs**, reveals the Inspiration skill **Reveal Catacombs** (40 Inspiration).

**The Mausoleum.** Once Reveal Catacombs is owned, a stone Mausoleum stands in the lower-right corner of the Tower's forest.
Tap it to enter the Catacomb of the tower the forest shows: Catacomb III from Tower III's forest, and so on.
Entering the Catacomb your run is in carries on with that run.
Entering another starts a new run there at Hall 1, and asks first when your run is past Hall 1.
You keep everything you have earned either way.

**The Catacomb button.** Once a Catacomb run is under way, the menu has a Catacomb button, in the forest and during a climb.
It shows the Catacomb your run is in and its hall, whatever tower you are in.
It is not offered in the Proving Grounds.

For now the Catacomb shows only its number and hall: the Magician waits at the entrance until the Catacomb screen opens in a later update.
