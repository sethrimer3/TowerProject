# The Aether Shield

A second pool of health that takes enemy strikes and shots before your HP, and regrows after it breaks.

## Unlocking it

**Aether Shield** (5 Breakthrough, between Rehearsed steps and Echoes of time) reveals four Inspiration skills, split off Torch to the right.

| Skill | Inspiration | Opens |
|---|---|---|
| **Shield** | 10 | The shield itself, and Shield HP training |
| **Shield Thorns** (below Shield) | 15 | Shield Thorns research |
| **Regrow Shield** (right of Shield) | 10 | Regrow Shield training |
| **Shield Regen** (below Regrow Shield) | 15 | Shield Regen research |

**Shield Limit Break** (5 Breakthrough, off Limit Break 2) raises Shield HP's most levels from 800 to 1,800.
It stays hidden until Aether Shield is owned.

## How it works

A climb starts with the shield full, in the Tower and the Delve alike.
It keeps what it holds from fight to fight and floor to floor, and undo takes back what it lost with the rest of the step.

Each enemy strike or shot comes off DEF, then Parry %, then Shroud, then the shield, and only what is left comes off HP.
Door tolls go straight to your HP: a Heart Door's price is the same with a shield.
A fight the shield takes whole costs no HP, so it doesn't spoil an area's mastery.
Predictions, the inspect panel and Damage Visual all count the shield you have now.

A blue bubble surrounds the hero while the shield stands.
When the shield reaches 0 the bubble pops, rapidly swelling and fading.

## Regrowing

A broken shield counts down turn by turn, then reforms full: the bubble grows out from the hero's center to full size, and blue sparkles flash once around it.
While it counts down, its counter reads *X turns to rebuild*, and its bar is a hollow outline that grows longer each turn, filling in solid as it reforms.
The count is fixed as the shield breaks, so what lowers it counts from the next break.

## The counters

The shield's counter stands beside the HP counter, under it on a phone, and its bar directly under the HP bar.
A paler layer on the bar shows anything the shield holds over its most, which only happens when its most falls during a climb.

## Numbers

**Shield HP** (Defense training) sets the shield's most as a percent of the hero's max HP: 20%, and 0.1% more a level, 100% at its 800 levels and 200% at 1,800.
Raising it during a climb adds to the shield too, unless the shield is down.

**Regrow Shield** (Defense training) sets the turns it takes to regrow: 1,200, and 2 fewer a level, 600 at its 300 levels.

| Research | Opens with | Levels | Each level |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Shield HP** | Shield, and Tower VIII's floor 10 Goal, *Unlock Aether Shield* | 50 | +2% of the trained value |
| **Regrow Shield** | Regrow Shield, and the same Goal | 20 | 10 fewer turns |
| **Shield Regen** | Shield Regen | 5 for now | A standing shield regains 1% more of your Regen each step, up to its most |
| **Shield Thorns** | Shield Thorns | 5 for now | A strike on the shield costs the enemy 1% more of your Thorns |

Whatever lowers it, a shield always takes at least 150 turns to regrow.

## Thorns

A strike or shot that lands while the shield stands does no Thorn damage by default; Shield Thorns research gives it a share of your Thorns.
A strike that breaks the shield still counts as landing on it.
Once the shield is down, your Thorns work in full.
An enemy's ATK rises with each strike or shot as usual, on the shield or not.
