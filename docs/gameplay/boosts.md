# Golden Scarabs and boosts

A **Golden Scarab** turns up on a floor every so often, for the player who is watching.
Claiming one starts a **boost**: something about your progress goes faster for a while of real time.
Boosts never make the hero stronger in a fight: they speed up Gold, XP, research, training and the climb itself.

## Finding a Golden Scarab

Once 15 minutes of real time have passed since the last one was claimed (or, on a new profile, since the first floor was reached), the next floor new to the climb has a Golden Scarab on it, in both the Tower and the Delve.
It lies on a plain floor tile out of the hero's way, like a floor Gem, and never on a Gem's tile.
Walk onto it, or tap it from anywhere on the board, to claim it.
A scarab left behind when the hero leaves its floor is missed, and another comes three new floors on.
Claiming one starts the 15 minutes again.
No Golden Scarab turns up in the Proving Grounds, though boosts running count there.

## The boosts

A claimed scarab gives one boost, picked at random among those you have unlocked:

| Boost | Effect | Offered once |
|---|---|---|
| Gold | All Gold found ×1.5 | always |
| XP | All the hero's XP ×1.5 | always |
| Card XP | All card XP ×1.5 | always |
| Research Speed | Archives research ×1.5 as fast, research under way included | the Archives are open |
| Training Speed | Trainers ×1.5 as fast, ranks in training included | a trainer is hired |
| Game Speed | +1 step a second while the hand plays (never past 15) | the speed arrows can still go faster |
| End Rush | +3 tiles STAIRS dashes toward the stairs | End Rush is owned |
| Equipment Drops | Equipment and material drop chances ×1.5 | Equipment is open |
| Gem Bank Refill | The ad Gem button's rewards bank twice as fast | Gem Card is owned |
| Rubies | Rubies found (bosses' and STAIRS' floors') ×1.5, rounded | a Ruby has been earned |
| Cells | Cells found ×1.5 | a Cell has been earned |

A boost lasts 12 minutes of real time, counting on while the game is closed and between climbs.
Another scarab of a boost already running adds its time on top of what is left.
Each boost is a factor of its own: the Gold boost multiplies the Level-up Boost and the Gold ad's boost rather than adding to them, and it shows as *Golden Scarab* in the *All Gold Bonuses* box while it runs.

## The boost table

The boosts running show in a small bordered table over the board's top-left corner, in the forest and inside a climb: a row each, its icon and the time left (*11:55*).
It also holds the run's [Level-up Boost](runs.md) and the [Gold ad's boost](currencies-and-shop.md).
A claimed scarab's boost drops from its tile into its row, showing its icon, its value and the time it added (*×1.5 +12:00*).

## Golden Scarab Research

**Golden Scarab Research** (2 Breakthrough, under Gem Card) opens three Archives projects:
- **Golden Scarab Effect** (100 levels): each level +2% to every boost's effect, up to three times it (×1.5 becomes ×4.5; Game Speed +3, End Rush +9).
- **Golden Scarab Duration** (100 levels): each level +2% to a boost's time, up to 36 minutes.
- **Golden Scarab Cooldown** (90 levels): each level 2 seconds off the time between scarabs, down to 12 minutes.

Their prices follow Offline Gold Multiplier's and Offline Gold Duration's.
