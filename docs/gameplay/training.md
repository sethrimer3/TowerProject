# Training

## Silver

Every monster beaten also pays **Silver**, a currency that lasts only for the run, spent on training for that run.
A weak monster pays 2 Silver on floor 1 and 0.2 more every floor (4 on floor 11, 42 on floor 201), then 0.1 more every floor (the Delve counts every ten depth as a floor); a normal monster pays twice that, a strong one three times, an elite four times, and a boss five times.
The stats show it beside the Gold.
Undo takes back the Silver of the kills it undoes, and a new run starts with none.

## Training for the run

Once **On the Job** is owned (1 Inspiration, after Buildout, right of it), a bar under the hand holds a button for each Training group: Offense, Defense and Utility (greyed until something in it is unlocked).
A closed group's button shows only its icon; the open one widens to add its name.
Pressing one shows that group's Training rows in the hand's place (the Archer's rows only while the run's hand holds the Archer card), a card each, scrolled sideways: its name, where it stands now (the hero's stat, the percentage, or the Gold a new floor pays), and a button with the Silver price of the next level.
What a value counts, on the Training tab and the run's cards alike: everything lasting that changes the row directly (Archives research, provisions, and the equipment the mode's hero wears), and inside a run what the run has changed too (the hero's own stats as they stand, with what it found and its Max HP badge's multiplier, its ranks bought with Silver, and its shrine boons).
Silver / Floor counts Silver Bonus (with the equipment's Silver found), since that multiplies it; a global Gold multiplier (the tower's, the Shop's packs, the ad's boost, themes, Gold found, the Gold boons) counts in no row, only in the HUD's Gold bonus total, and shows in the Gold each kill or item actually pays.
Pressing the button pays the Silver and raises it at once by what the Training tab shows (equipment's percent and research counted), for the rest of this run only (more max HP comes with the HP to fill it, as it does when a trainer finishes a Max HP rank mid-run); while the Silver isn't there the button's outline and face fade, its price still clear, and the card stays readable.
A card at its most turns gold, its button reading *Max*.
Pressing a card, or hovering over it, shows its description and its level of the most it can reach; pressing the card again hides it.
The cards are exactly as tall as the hand's, so nothing else on the screen moves as they swap.
Pressing the group's button again brings the hand back.
A run starts at the hero's own Training level, so more Training between runs lets a run reach higher for the same Silver: the first level bought costs 3 Silver for max HP, 5 for ATK, DEF and Regen, 10 for Shroud, Crit % and the currency rows (Gold / Floor, Silver / Floor, Silver Bonus, Gold Bonus), 12 for Crit x, 20 for Potion % and Find Potion, 30 for Attack Speed, 50 for Attack Range and ATK / m, 60 for Multishot Chance, 120 for Rapid Fire Chance and Duration and 450 for Multishot Targets (rising as their trainers' Gold does: Attack Range 1,165 for the 12th, 8,573 for the 36th, 64,075 for the 78th; Attack Speed 1,166 for the 12th, 8,870 for the 34th, 116,464 for the 92nd; ATK / m 589 for the 38th, 60,929 for the 98th, 1 million for the 130th and 998 million for the 200th; Multishot Chance 1,163 for the 11th, 9,893 for the 32nd, 99,554 for the 85th; Multishot Targets 2,000, 5,000, 12,500, 40,000, 120,000 and 350,000 for the rest of its seven; Rapid Fire Chance and Duration 1,002 for the 9th, 10,013 for the 30th, 100,199 for the 82nd), 40 for Parry %, 60 for Thorn Damage and 100 for Revive (rising as its trainers' Gold does: 1,277 for the 7th, 9,870 for the 13th, 95,724 for the 23rd, about a million for the 41st) (the Free Upgrade rows 75, rising as their trainers' Gold does: 1,582 for the 11th, 136,855 for the 100th).
Each level after costs more than the last by a step plus the levels already bought, the step growing every five levels: +1, +3, +5 … for max HP (3, 5, 8, 12, 17, 23, 32 …), +1, +4, +7 … for the 5-Silver rows (ATK: 5, 7, 10, 14, 19, 25, 35 … 85 for the 11th), +2, +5, +8 … for the 10-Silver rows, and +4, +8, +12 … for the 20-Silver rows.
A row goes no higher than its most on the Training tab (1,000 for ATK, DEF, Regen, Max HP and Shroud, raised by 500 for each **Limit Break** owned, to 6,000 with all ten, 5,000 for Shroud, which only the first eight raise; 300 for Potion %, 150 for Gold / Floor, Silver / Floor, Silver Bonus, Gold Bonus and Crit x, 80 for Crit % and Attack Range, 99 for Revive and Thorn Damage, 100 for Attack Speed, 200 for ATK / m, 99 for Multishot Chance, 7 for Multishot Targets, 85 for Rapid Fire Chance, 99 for Rapid Fire Duration, 100 for Parry %, 80 for Lifesteal, 72 for Find Potion, 100 for the Free Offense, Defense and Utility Up rows).
A row at its most shows no values, only its name and level, a Max where the trainer's price stood, and its reset at the right edge.
Each purchase is a turn of its own: undo takes it back, Silver and all.
A level can be bought while a fight plays out too (not one shown as a summary): it counts from the fight's next strike, so ATK bought makes the hero's next strikes harder, DEF softens the monster's next strikes, and max HP raises the HP left at once, which can turn a losing fight around.
The first run after On the Job is bought teaches the bar: a note over the hand explains spending Silver on upgrades for the run, and a pointing hand bounces over a group's button until the player opens a group and presses it again to close it.

## XP and levels

Every monster defeated pays XP, more for stronger monsters and higher floors, and XP carries over between runs.
Each level needs more XP than the last (with the cube of the level), faster than the XP a kill rises with height, so levels come more slowly the higher you climb, even replaying the floors below.
Past level 25 each level costs 25% more, past 50 another 50% more on top (×1.875), past 75 another 75% more (×3.28), past 100 double that (×6.56), and each 25 levels after that another 25% more (×8.2 past 125, ×10.25 past 150, …).
The level shows as a large number at the front of the XP bar under the hero's portrait, which fills with the current level's progress; hovering it shows the XP gained toward the next level and what that level needs.
Reaching a new level bursts fire outward from the hero, surrounds the hero with a glow for two seconds (fading over the last half second), and flashes "LEVEL UP!" over the board; once it fades, "+3" and the training points' golden arrow rise off the hero.

## The Training tab

Each level earns 3 **training points**, and each weekly mission reward 1 more, spent on the Research page's Training tab, open from the start (each row names its level, *Max HP - Lv 5*, and *Lv 5 → 6* while a trainer trains it; the Research tab wears a dot while there are points to spend, and the Training tab, seen from the Archives, after each level-up until Training is shown).
Inside a run, the Upgrades button in the run's menu (the menu button wears its dot) opens the Research page with the run paused: ranks bought there reach the run's hero at once (and undo keeps them, taking back only steps), and its Back returns to the board, the hand playing or paused at the speed it had: a table in three groups, Offense (ATK), Defense (max HP, DEF, then Regen, Shroud, Potion %, Find Potion, Revive, Thorn Damage and Parry % once the Regen, Shroud, Recovery, Find Potion, Revive, Thorns and Parry skills are owned; Regen gives HP back with every step taken in a run, n ranks 0.1n + n²/120, a Dash turn counting as one step) and Offense also holds Crit % (+1% a rank to 80%, the chance each strike is critical) and Crit x (×1.2, +0.1 a rank, what a critical strike multiplies ATK by), once Critical is owned, and Attack Range (3.00 tiles, +0.05 a rank to 7.00, how far the Archer's arrows reach) and Attack Speed (1.00 shot every 10 turns, +0.05 a rank to 6.00) once Tower III's floor 20 and 30 Goals are claimed, ATK / m from its floor 50, and Multishot Chance (0%, +0.5% a rank to 49.5%, the chance a shot fires at several enemies) and Multishot Targets (2 targets, +1 a rank to 9, how many) from its floor 70, and Rapid Fire Chance (0%, +0.4% a rank to 34%, the chance a shot starts Rapid Fire) and Rapid Fire Duration (1.00 turns, +0.05 a rank to 5.95, how long it fires four times as fast) from its floor 80; Utility (Gold / Floor, Silver / Floor, Silver Bonus and Gold Bonus once their skills are owned, the three Free Upgrade rows with Free Training, and Overheal with Overheal), each row with the training time its next rank still needs (less its time credit; counting down while a trainer trains it), its current value and the value one more rank makes it (Attack Range and Attack Speed as bare numbers, such as 3.00 → 3.05; counted as the cards' are, above; inside a run, the run's hero moved by the rank), and two ways to buy the next rank: training points (the arrow button) buy it at once, 1 point for a rank of any row; or, once the **Trainers** skill (1 Inspiration, after Buildout, below it) is owned, Gold (the coin button) pays a **trainer** to train it, which takes time but no points, at 20 Gold for each point the rank would cost times the trainer's rank number and by a curve that rises faster the dearer the row's first rank is with Silver in a run: times 1 + ranks / 10 for the rows starting at 20 Silver (20, 44, 72, 104 … 380 for the tenth rank a trainer trains in that stat), 1 + ranks / 20 at 10 Silver (… 290), 1 + ranks / 40 at 5 Silver (… 245) and 1 + ranks / 60 for max HP and Shroud (… 230); the Free Upgrade rows ask what their Silver does, from 75 Gold (75, 1,368 for the tenth, 9,771 for the 32nd, 136,855 for the 100th).
The two keep separate schedules: ranks bought with points never make a trainer's next rank dearer or slower.
Until Trainers is owned the tab shows no trainers, timers, boost or auto-continue boxes.
Buying a rank with points while a trainer trains it leaves the trainer training: the rank counts at once, and the trainer carries on as before.
Points can buy a row up to its most even while a trainer's ranks would reach it; should they leave the trainer's ranks no room, it trains only the ranks still under the most: the Gold of those it drops comes back, their training time comes off, and the training already done counts, so it finishes that much sooner (at once, if what it has done covers what is left).
Points that fill every rank the trainer had stop it, as stopping it by hand does: its Gold comes back and the time it spent becomes time credit; the Gold and the time rise over the Gold held and the row's time to its next rank.
Potion % adds 0.25% of max HP to what a percent potion restores, and Find Potion 0.25% to the chance that a potion is a percent potion (from 2% up to 20%, at 72 ranks), both the same at every level.
Overheal raises how far potions, GK TO HP and badge heals may heal above max HP: ×1.00 of max HP, and 0.031 more a rank, up to ×16.50 at its 500 ranks.
Its ranks cost the same in Silver in a run and in a trainer's Gold, 1,000 + (n⁴ − 1) × (800 + n) / 400 for the n-th: 1,000 for the first, about 21,000 for the 10th, 5.4 million for the 40th, 1.2 billion for the 150th, 10.3 billion for the 250th, 128 billion for the 450th and 203 billion for the 500th.
A stat's ranks add up on its own curve, each rank worth more than the one before, whatever the hero's level (levels only earn the points): n ranks of max HP give 10n + n², of ATK n + n²/5, of DEF n²/12 and of Regen 0.1n + n²/120, and Shroud's grow steeper still, 5.1 at the first rank, 60 at 10, about 2,100 at 100, 15,000 at 200 and 62,000 at 300, up to about four billion at its 5,000 ranks (with eight Limit Breaks), fractions and all.
Once the Archives are open, their **ATK**, **DEF** and **Max HP** research multiply all of the hero's ATK (+2% a level), DEF and max HP (+3% a level) a run starts with, for 100 levels each, priced and timed like Pocket Money.
A new hero starts with 100 max HP, no DEF and no shroud.
Training applies at once to a run still in the forest.
A stat can be reset for 2 Gems (its row's reset button), only between runs (the button is closed inside a run), one way at a time, the other way's ranks staying: either its ranks bought with training points, returning those points to spend on any stat, or its ranks trainers trained, stopping a rank in training and returning their Gold and all the training time spent on the stat (its trainers' ranks, a rank in training, and its own time credit) into the **time bank**, shown beside a clock by the training points while there is any: any stat's next ranks trainers train start that far along, after that stat's own **time credit** (the time a stopped or dashed rank gave back to it), until it is used up.
Both are taken off the time each row shows.

## Trainers

A trainer's rank takes time, on the clock like the Archives' research: a stat's first four ranks take 15 seconds, 1 minute, 5 minutes and 10 minutes, and each after ten minutes more than the last (10 minutes, 20, 30 …, 16 hours by the 100th), less with Faster Trainers research.
ATK / m and Multishot Chance skip the 15 seconds, starting at 1 minute, and Multishot Targets' ranks take an hour more each (1 hour, 2, 3 … 7).
**Training Discount** research (opened by Tower II's floor 80 Goal) takes 0.5% a level off the Gold every rank a trainer trains costs, rounded up, for 99 levels (30 Gold and 30 seconds for the first level, about 10,000 Gold at level 12, 100,000 at 26, a million at 59 and 4.1 million at 99, which takes 49 days); training points and a run's Silver cost the same as ever.
One stat trains at a time, with one more for each trainer bought with Gems beside the trainer count (100, 250, 450, 700, 1,000 and 1,400 Gems, up to seven at once).
A rank in training shows its countdown (press it to stop, get the Gold back and keep the time spent as credit) and a Gem button that finishes it at once: 1 Gem for each 10 minutes left, and 1 more for any part of 10 minutes.
Each stat at its most (Max, in a gold box) makes finishing 1.5% more efficient: with 10 at their most, the Gems are divided by 1.15 and rounded up, so 100 Gems become 87.
Since a reset or a Limit Break can take a stat off its most, this can go down as well as up.
Once any stat is at its most, the Training heading shows it at its left: *+1.5% to Rush* for one.
The countdown reads to the second (*7h 41m 33s*), ticking each time it shows another second, sooner as training goes faster (the boost, the Shop's packs), but at most ten times a second: faster than that, each tick takes off more than a second.
A stat a trainer is training moves up under the trainer count, in the order they started, and goes back to its group once its training ends.
While it trains, a box with a looping arrow before its name sets it to **auto-continue**: while ticked, each rank its trainer finishes starts the next at once, as long as the Gold is there, ranks finished while the game was closed included.
A row a trainer is training puts its auto-continue box, its name and its levels (*Lv 0 → 10 / 1,000*) on a line of their own, and under it a batch's speedup (*×3*, large), its values, its countdown (as wide as its time needs, on one line), the points, and the Gem button in the reset's place: it has no reset while it trains, and its time to go shows only on the countdown.
Each rank a trainer finishes, whatever page shows or while the game was closed, announces "Training Complete!" at the top of the screen and counts at once, a run inside included.

## The training boost

The box at the top of the tab is the **training boost**: "×2" with its time left, or "Inactive", and an ad button, "+1h" (no ad plays yet: it is granted on the press), each press banking an hour more, up to four (the button closes while more than 3 hours 50 minutes are banked, until the time runs down).
While it lasts, ranks in training go twice as fast, their countdowns dropping two seconds a second, on the clock like the rest.
With Permanent Ad-Disable the boost runs for good, no ad needed, and in the box's place a glowing "×2" stands on the trainers' row; the Special Coin Pack and the Premium Coin Pack make training ×2 and ×3 as fast besides, all three multiplying (×12 with all three), the speed glowing there too.
**Faster Trainers** (2 Inspiration, after the Archives, right of them) opens Faster Trainers research: +2% training speed a level for 100 levels (+200%, a rank then taking a third of its time), the n-th level costing 250 × n + 2 × n³ Gold and taking Research Speed's time or 1.75 × n + n²/20 hours, whichever is shorter (1 minute first, Research Speed's to level 27, then 88 hours at 28; 2 million Gold and 28 days at level 100), about 2.9 years in all.
