# Skills and stats

The upgrade map offers only the Inspiration and Breakthrough trees, including in developer mode.
Their buttons put each icon above its name and points, keeping the text centered and on single lines.
The taller buttons wrap into rows on phones so labels and balances stay readable.

A new game's Inspiration tree shows one line of skills, from Door & Key down to **Trainers**; buying Trainers reveals the paths that branch out after it (Larger Hand, On the Job, ATK Up and everything under them).
A skill you can't yet afford, once pressed, says how many more Inspiration points it needs: *You need 2 more Inspiration points. Climb higher in the tower to get more.*

## Below the Archives

Greater Heal and the skills under it stay hidden until the Breakthrough skill **Physician** (1 Breakthrough, below Movement Speed) is bought, and Pocket Money and the skills under it until **Path to Wealth** (1 Breakthrough, left of Physician).
Below Free Training, **Interest**, with **Max Interest** and **Mug** either side under it (10 Inspiration each), and Chest, Torch and Floor Skip Reward under them, wait besides on **More Wealth** (1 Breakthrough, under Path to Wealth).
**Golden Floors** (3 Breakthrough, under More Wealth) opens the [Golden Floor](environments.md#golden-floor) research.
**Thorns** stands below Buy Quantity, a row below Torch and Floor Skip Reward, and leads three ways, each hidden until a Mastery under **Key Mastery** (1 Breakthrough, under Overheal) in the Breakthrough tree is bought: down the middle Wooden Door and the door cards (**Door Mastery**, 2 Breakthrough, under Key Mastery), down to the left Find Gold Key and Key Efficiency (revealed by Key Mastery itself), then GK Siphon, BK Siphon, the key trades and the key badges (**Card Mastery**), and to the right Weak Enemy and the enemy cards (**Beast Mastery**), those two 1 Breakthrough each, split off under Key Mastery.
**Bombs** (5 Inspiration, left of Critical Prediction, after it) opens the Bomb provision (see gear-and-equipment.md).
Below the Archives in the Inspiration tree, which grows taller than the screen and scrolls (drag it, or turn the mouse wheel; Ctrl and the wheel, or a pinch, zooms), **Greater Heal** (3 Inspiration, after the Archives) opens the Archives' **Potion HP** research,, **Recovery** (10 Inspiration, after Greater Heal) brings **percent potions** onto the floors (2% of potions), **Find Potion** (10 Inspiration, below Recovery) opens **Find Potion** training, which makes more of them percent potions, and **Shroud** (2 Inspiration, after Trainers, above Critical) wraps the hero in a **shroud** that blocks the first 1 damage of every fight and opens **Shroud** training and the Archives' **Shroud** research (+4% of the shroud a level, for 100 levels, up to ×5, priced and timed like Pocket Money), **Parry** (10 Inspiration, below Regen Research) lets the hero parry, **Revive** (10 Inspiration, below Parry) may raise a felled hero at full HP, and **Thorns** (10 Inspiration, on the middle path between Buy Quantity and GK Siphon) makes every monster pay for striking (see below).

## Silver and Gold

**Pocket Money** (1 Inspiration, after the Archives) opens the Archives' **Pocket Money** research: every run goes inside with 5 more Silver in hand a level, for 100 levels, priced and timed like Potion HP.
**Spare Change** (3 Inspiration, below Pocket Money) makes every floor climbed for the first time in a run pay Gold: in the Tower each staircase taken up for the first time that run, in the Delve each new ten depth.
It pays 3 Gold, and 1 more for each rank of the **Gold / Floor** training it opens (Utility, 1 training point a rank, or Silver inside a run), times the tower's Gold bonus; undo never makes a floor pay twice.
It also opens the Archives' **Gold / Floor** research (listed last), +5% of that Gold a level for 100 levels, priced and timed like Potion HP.
Below it, three more skills (2 Inspiration each) each open a Utility training row (1 point a rank, or Silver inside a run) and Archives research of the same name (100 levels, priced and timed like Gold / Floor): **Wealthy** opens **Silver Bonus**, which multiplies all Silver a run finds (×1, +1% a rank, and +3% a research level, the two multiplied: 30% of each makes ×1.69); **Loot** opens **Gold Bonus**, which multiplies the same way but +3% a rank, before the tower's Gold bonus, each kill's Gold, a treasure chest's Gold, the Gold every item taken off the floor or door opened pays, and what Floor Skip pays for them (not Gold / Floor or Interest; see [Floors](floors.md#what-the-floor-pays)); **Wishing Well** opens **Silver / Floor**: every floor climbed for the first time in a run (above the run's highest, in the Tower) pays 4 Silver a rank (none before the first), +5% a research level, then Silver Bonus (its training and research).

## Free Training

**Free Training** (20 Inspiration, under Wishing Well) opens three Utility rows, **Free Offense Up**, **Free Defense Up** and **Free Utility Up** (100 ranks each): each rank adds 0.5% to the chance that each floor passed in a run (a new floor in the Tower, the one Skip climbs past included; each new ten depth in the Delve) trains a random stat of that group, open and not yet at its most, one level for free, for the run (Free Utility can pick the Free rows themselves).
A free level costs no Silver but raises that row's Silver price a step, as if it were bought, so a level bought before one comes free is cheaper than after.
The levels given show for two seconds at the bottom left of the board, a line each ("+1 ATK"), fading over the last half second.
Silver keeps its fractions, like Gold, and the purse shows it rounded down.

## The shroud and potions

The shroud is whole again at the start of each fight and takes the enemy's strikes until it is spent; only then does HP fall.
What it blocks rises off the hero in silver, beside the red of any damage that got through, the HUD shows it beside DEF once owned, and every damage forecast (the inspect box, route totals, the hand and Automove) counts it.
Without Recovery every potion is a regular one; with it, the striped potions the Tower's floors roll (half of them) and half of the Delve's corridor-guard potions become percent potions, from the next run on: each restores the regular 35 HP plus 1% of max HP, and 0.25% more for each rank of **Potion %** training.
A regular potion is drawn red, like the HP bar, and a percent potion blue, like the Overheal bar.

## Overheal

**Overheal** (2 Breakthrough, between Physician and Key Mastery in the Breakthrough tree) lets HP rise above max HP, and opens Overheal training.
Potions, regular and percent, the smelts (Smelt GK, BK and RK) and the badges' heals then heal past max HP, up to the Overheal ceiling: max HP times the Overheal training row's value (×1.00 until it has ranks).
Regen, Lifesteal, Dash Regen, equipment's victory and floor heals still stop at max HP, and none of them lowers HP held above it.
The HP readout shows HP over max (1,240 / 1,000), and its tooltip the ceiling.
The HP bar stays full red, and a blue bar over it shows the HP above max, full at the ceiling.
While it shows, a fight's purple and a potion's growing heal are drawn on the blue bar instead of the red.
Max HP changing in a run (training, a Shrine boon, the Max HP badge, a siphon) keeps HP above max, within the new ceiling; Revive still rises at max HP.

**Regen** research (after **Regen Research**, 10 Inspiration, below Shroud, beside Recovery; listed after Potion HP) makes every step regain 3% more of what Regen training gives a level, for 100 levels, priced and timed exactly like Potion HP, and counting at once.

## Buy Quantity

**Buy Quantity** (5 Inspiration, below GK Siphon) adds a quantity dropdown to Training (drawn by the game, a short list that opens beside it, on phones too), at the right of the trainers' row on the Training tab and of the group buttons under the hand inside a run: one press of a training-points button, or of a run card's Silver price, buys that many ranks at once (fewer only at a row's most), showing their total price, and the Training tab shows the value they reach.
It offers x1 at first; the Archives' **Buy Quantity** research opens x5, x10, x100 and Max, a level each (1,000, 5,000, 25,000 and 100,000 Gold; 4, 12, 24 and 48 hours).
Max buys as many as the points or Silver held pay for, its count ("x12") shown on the button beside their price.
Trainers still train one rank at a time.

## Potion HP

**Potion HP** (listed first in the Archives) makes every potion restore 20% more HP a level, for 100 levels (up to 21 times as much), the Cinder Tonic included but not the percent potion; each level counts the moment it completes, even mid-run.
The first four levels are quick (15 seconds for 10 Gold, 1 minute for 25, 5 minutes for 50, 10 minutes for 75); then the formula starts over, so the m-th level after those takes m/4 + m²/100 hours and costs 100 × m + m³ Gold (about 16 minutes and 101 Gold at level 5, up to about 4.8 days and 894,000 Gold at level 100).

## Revive

**Revive** (10 Inspiration, below Parry) gives each monster's strike that would fell the hero a 0.5% chance to raise it at full HP instead: the skull of a defeat burns over the hero first (its HP shown at 0), then it rises in a smaller golden blaze like a level-up's, under a beam of white and golden light that a few small white angel wings flutter down and fade from, and once that is over the fight goes on from the next round, the monster as hurt and as fierce as it was, and a later strike that would fell the hero again rolls again.
It opens Revive training, 1 training point a rank, each adding 0.5%, up to 50% at 99 ranks.
Each strike's roll is fixed for that run, floor and tile, so undoing a fight and fighting it again ends the same way.
Forecasts, Automove and the hand still treat such a fight as lost.

## Thorns

**Thorns** (10 Inspiration, below Buy Quantity) opens Thorn Damage training, 1% a rank up to 99%: every monster strike the hero lives through (parried or not, blocked by the shroud or not) costs the monster that share of its maximum HP, half against a boss or Greater Boss, whatever its DEF, so at 50% a monster falls to its second strike however much HP it has.
Its maximum HP is what it had before the hero's arrows wounded it, so a wounded monster pays as much as a fresh one.
A Ranged or Flying monster's shot the hero lives through costs it the same.
The amount rises in yellow over the monster as the strike's damage rises over the hero, and a monster felled this way counts as any other kill (though never as an Instakill).
Thorns are certain, so every forecast, Automove and the hand count them.

## Parry

**Parry** (10 Inspiration, between Regen Research and Revive) opens Parry % training (0.5% a rank, 100 ranks): once the hero has any Parry %, every monster strike is parried, the damage it does after DEF cut by Parry %, before the shroud and Lifesteal's heals (the monster's ATK still rises after the round as usual).
A Ranged monster's shots are parried the same way.
In an animated fight the hero gives way a little from a strike it parries; in a summary only the damage shows it.
Parries are certain, so forecasts, the inspect panel, the monster's damage number, Automove and the hand all count them.
