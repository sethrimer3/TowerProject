# The Codex

The **Codex** is your reference to the game.
Open it with the ⓘ **Codex** button in the menu (the hamburger at the top of the actions column), in the forest or inside a climb; inside a climb the hand pauses while it is open.
Its X returns to where you opened it.

Each category is a tab.
A category you haven't reached shows as ??? and can't be chosen, and inside an open category anything you haven't come across yet shows as ???.
With the Dev setting Access to Everything on, every category and entry shows.
Pressing a section's heading collapses or expands it, the chevron at its right showing which.
An entry with more to tell expands under a + (a − once open); one with a single stat or rank shows it after its name instead.

- **General**: the game's big ideas (the Tower, its floors, cards and ascension, keys and doors, Goals, new towers, the Delve, the Proving Grounds, Missions, Events, Relics, the Shop), Free Daily Gems, with a button to that offer in the Shop, and How to progress, a list of tips for when you're stuck.
- **Story**: the tale so far, a chapter a collapsible section, opened once the first climb has ended: its **Intro** tells of the towers and dungeons, the young adventurer delving to raise their family out of debt, and the Chrono Anchor.
  Its **Training** chapter, the adventurer hiring a trainer with the family's help, joins it once the Trainers skill is owned, and its **Archives** chapter, the adventurer's first look at the Royal Archives, once the Archives skill is; the adventurer's thoughts are in italics.
- **Currencies**: every currency you've earned, what it's for and, after its name, how much you hold, as *(Have 120)* (Silver only inside a climb).
- **Tower Elements**: stairs, each colour of key beside its door's coloured tile, Wooden and Multi Doors, potions, shards, chests, torches, Gems, then Heart Doors.
  Blue, red and Heart Doors show once you've climbed to them, and Multi Doors with red.
  A Torch's link to the Greater Boss is told only once you own the Torch card.
- **Card Deck**: every card in your deck with a line on what it's for; expand one for its level, XP, the XP to its next level and its powers, up to the next rarity's only, so higher ones stay a surprise (every rarity's with Access to Everything). A card ready to ascend is marked, with a button showing its Rubies and Gems that opens it on the Deck page.
- **Card Badges**: each badge owned with its level, copies toward the next level and what it does now and at the next level, then those still to draw.
- **Inspiration** and **Breakthrough**: every skill in the tree, grouped as Owned, Available (its price after its name), Locked (what reaching it costs after its name, it and every skill above it) and Hidden.
- **Training**: how Training works (free training points from levels and weekly missions, training with Gold, training several ranks at once, rushing with Gems, auto-continue, stopping), then each Training row open to you, by group, each with its rank and what that adds (*Rank 3 (+33)*).
- **Arcana**: each arcanum you've been offered, owned or been lent by a Shrine boon; an owned one shows its attributes' levels and values.
- **Equipment**: the Unique pieces you've found, by category.
- **Familiars**: the five familiars, shown once Events are open, each hatched one with its levels.
- **Enemies**: how fights work (who strikes first, how damage is counted, rising ATK), the stats that win fights, which stats help most against which enemies, then enemies by power and by attribute (each once a tower you've opened can have it), by build, and the Proving Grounds bosses.
- **Combat**: the powers that turn fights your way, each once you own its skill.
- **Shrines**, **Environments** and **Archives**: the boons, floor environments and research projects (the Archives with how they work: Gold, Rush, auto-continue, switching, archivists and Speed Ups), each project with its rank and what that adds.

Buttons lead to the page they name; one for a page only the forest opens (Deck, Upgrades, Gear, Goals, Relics, Shrine Boons) can't be pressed inside a climb.
