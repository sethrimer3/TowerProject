# The Codex

The **Codex** is your reference to the game.
Open it with the ⓘ **Codex** button in the menu (the hamburger at the top of the actions column), in the forest or inside a climb; inside a climb the hand pauses while it is open.
Its X returns to where you opened it.

Each category is a tab.
A category you haven't reached shows as ??? and can't be chosen, and inside an open category anything you haven't come across yet shows as ???.
With the Dev setting Access to Everything on, every category and entry shows.

- **General**: the game's big ideas (the Tower, its floors, cards and ascension, keys and doors, Goals, new towers, the Delve, the Proving Grounds, Missions, Events, Relics, the Shop), Free Daily Gems, with a button to that offer in the Shop, and How to progress, with tips for when you're stuck.
- **Currencies**: every currency you've earned, what it's for and how much you hold.
- **Tower Elements**: stairs, keys and doors (blue, red and Heart Doors once you've climbed to them), potions, shards, chests, torches and Gems.
- **Card Deck**: every card in your deck with a line on what it's for; expand one for its level, XP, the XP to its next level and its powers. A card ready to ascend is marked, with a button showing its Rubies that opens it on the Deck page.
- **Card Badges**: each badge owned with its level, copies toward the next level and what it does now and at the next level, then those still to draw.
- **Inspiration** and **Breakthrough**: every skill in the tree, grouped as Owned, Available (with its price), Locked (with what reaching it costs, it and every skill above it) and Hidden.
- **Training**: how Training works (free training points from levels and weekly missions, training with Gold, training several ranks at once, rushing with Gems, auto-continue, stopping), then each Training row open to you, by group.
- **Arcana**: each arcanum you've been offered, owned or been lent by a Shrine boon; an owned one shows its attributes' levels and values.
- **Equipment**: the Unique pieces you've found, by category.
- **Enemies**: how fights work (who strikes first, how damage is counted, rising ATK), the stats that win fights, which stats help most against which enemies, then enemies by power and by attribute (each once a tower you've opened can have it), by build, and the Proving Grounds bosses.
- **Combat**: the powers that turn fights your way, each once you own its skill.
- **Shrines**, **Environments** and **Archives**: the boons, floor environments and research projects (the Archives with how they work: Gold, Rush, auto-continue, switching, archivists and Speed Ups).

Buttons lead to the page they name; one for a page only the forest opens (Deck, Upgrades, Gear, Goals, Relics, Shrine Boons) can't be pressed inside a climb.
