# Currencies and the Shop

Inspiration uses a small blue-white spark, while Breakthrough uses a larger ivory-and-brass starburst.
These distinct symbols appear wherever their balances, skill trees and rewards are shown.

The [relic collection](../EVENTS.md#relics-and-the-track) uses individual pixel artwork for every named relic, including legendary tower rewards.
The same artifact appears in its reward display and enlarged preview.

## Gems

**Gems** are the premium currency, kept between runs like Gold.
The stats show the height in their right-hand column (in the forest, the tower or delve the entrance leads to, such as Tower 2), and beside it, once a run has earned any, the Inspiration a Tower run has earned, its icon and number flashing each time it rises; under them the Inspiration a new best would pay (the Delve's depth pays none: it only scores a tournament), and below that the currencies held, one a line: Gems, in the forest Arcanite, Gold and, inside a run, its Silver (amounts over 9,999 shortened, such as 123K; the exact amount on hover).
Inside a run a tap on the currencies switches Gold and Silver to the rates the run finds them at, such as *12/min*, over the last minute of play: time the hand waits (paused, or with no card able to act) or a page or dialog covers the board doesn't count, so a pause leaves the rates as they were.
**Arcanite** buys and upgrades [Arcana](arcana.md).
The pages that spend currencies (Upgrades, Research, Deck, Gear, Missions and Events) show a single row at their top: Gold, Gems and Arcanite, each its icon and amount (amounts over 9,999 shortened), and a down chevron.
The chevron opens a list of every currency held, a row each with its icon, name and amount (shortened the same way), which scrolls or swipes, and closes with the X at its top right: Gold, Gems, Rubies, Arcanite, Medals, VIP Points, Silver, Training Points, Inspiration and Breakthrough, then at the bottom Cells, Tournament Tickets and the Equipment upgrade materials, each listed once what earns it is open.
The Upgrades page's Inspiration and Breakthrough tabs also read the points held, and the Events page's banner the Medals held, beside the time the event has left.
Gold prices on buy buttons read in full up to 99,999 and shortened from 100,000 (123K, 4.56M); Silver prices on a run's training cards, and trainers' Gold prices on the Training page, are shortened from 10,000 (12.3K).
Shortened amounts, wherever they appear, use K (thousand), M (million), B (billion), T (trillion), q (quadrillion), Q (quintillion), s (sextillion), S (septillion), O (octillion), N (nonillion) and D (decillion), always rounded down; past 999D they read as a power of ten.

## Gems on the floors

A Gem now and then lies on a floor: on the next new floor a run reaches (a Tower floor not reached before in that run, or each 10 depth of the Delve, within view ahead of the hero), on a plain tile the hero can walk to, out of the way where there is one (off the routes between the hero and the floor's items, monsters, doors and stairs, tucked into a corner or nook), so the hand's cards seldom walk the hero over it, shining over the darkness.
Stepping on it, or tapping it anywhere on the board, collects it in a sparkle for 1 Gem, and the next can't appear for 30 minutes (on the real clock).
A tapped Gem flies to the Gems in your purse, which count it as it lands.
A Gem left on a floor the hero leaves, or in a run that ends, is gone, and another appears 3 new floors later, again and again until one is collected (that count carries into the next run).
The [Gem card](hand-and-cards.md#the-gem-card) adds more Gems while either hand holds it: Golden and Stellar Gems, Gems of its own on new floors, from bosses and in chests.

## Rubies

**Rubies** are kept between runs and spent [ascending cards](hand-and-cards.md#ascending).
Each boss and Greater Boss drops as many as its tower's number when felled (1 in Tower I, 9 in Tower IX; in the Delve, its cave's), each kill once, rising off its tile; Boss Enemy's Boss Rubies and Stairs' Ruby Chance add more.
They show in the currencies dialog once you have had one, and over the Deck section of the Deck page.

## Ad rewards

Under the currencies, in the forest and inside a run, in the Tower and the Delve alike, a Gem button offers **6 Gems**, marked Claim (for watching an ad, once ads are hooked up; for now pressing it pays them, in a burst of blue glints); it hides once claimed and comes back 10 minutes later.
With the Gem card's **Gem rewards banked** power (half a reward a level: a whole one always, a half one on a refill whose roll comes up, rolled at each claim), the button keeps banking a reward each cooldown while it goes unclaimed, until its bank is full, and shows them all together (*12* for two rewards of 6); a claim takes every reward banked, and never resets the time already tallied toward the next (with a 10-minute cooldown, a claim 5 minutes after the button appears brings it back 5 minutes later).
While it banks, a blue fill rises across the button, from empty as it appears to full as the bank fills, and once full its outline turns gold.
Settings then offers **Notification chime when the Gem bank is full**, on by default (silent for now: the game has no sound effects yet).
Inside a run a **×1.5 Gold** ad button stands beside it: each claim (again, no ad plays yet) stores 20 minutes of real time in which all Gold found is multiplied by 1.5, claimable again and again up to two hours stored (the button closes while it holds more than an hour and fifty minutes); while it lasts the button glows and shows the time left, and with none stored it reads *Inactive*.
Permanent Ad-Disable's ×1.5 Gold takes the same slot: while the boost runs, the boost counts instead, so the two never stack.
Inside a run the height column shows every Gold multiplier multiplied together, and pressing it lists them in the *All Gold Bonuses* box: the tower's, the ads' slot, the [Level-up Boost](runs.md) while it lasts, each Gold bundle, the Event Collection, the Relics (every Gold Bonus relic owned, their percents added), the equipment's Gold found, the Shrine boons and, once Golden Floors is owned, the Golden Floor line (the hero's floor's [Golden Floor Multiplier](environments.md#golden-floor), Inactive off one); every Gold the run banks is what it found times that total.
When the boost ran during a run, its end dialog adds a line for it, such as *50 Gold × 1.5 boost = 75 Gold*.

### Ads Watch

Until Permanent Ad-Disable is owned, pressing any ad button (the Gems', the Gold ad's, Training's boost or the tournament's ad Ticket) first opens the **Ads Watch** dialog.
At its top a bar of prizes scrolls sideways, an ad's play mark at its left end, and every ad watched moves it on; a prize waits every 5 ads:

| Ads | Prize |
|---|---|
| 5 | Gold ×2 for 1 hour |
| 10 | 30 Gems |
| 15 | Gold ×3 for 1 hour |
| 20 | 40 Gems |
| 25 | Gold ×3 for 2 hours |
| 30 | 50 Gems |
| 35 | 10 Rubies |
| 40 | Permanent Ad-Disable 50% off in the Shop for 24 hours |

Under the bar, a shining box shows what the pressed button offers (*6* and a Gem, or every reward the Gem Bank holds), with a golden **Watch** button: it closes the dialog, plays the ad (none plays yet: it pays at once) and pays the offer.
An ad that reaches a prize opens the dialog again, its Watch button closed, since the ad was just watched.
A prize reached glows until pressed; pressing it claims it, celebrated in the middle of the screen until a press anywhere, then the dialog comes back.
The Gold prizes are their own *Ad Reward* line in *All Gold Bonuses*, multiplying with every other bonus; won while another runs, the stronger runs first and the other waits, so no time is lost.
Buying Ad-Disable or a Gold bundle while one runs keeps it running until its time is up.
Under the offer, Permanent Ad-Disable is sold as on the Shop page, with its buy button; during its discount both show the old price struck through and the time left.
The bar never resets until it is full, so prizes left on it wait as long as needed; once every prize is claimed it stays full, reading *The prize row resets in 2D:05H:13M*, until Monday 00:00 GMT, and the next ad starts it again.

## Themes

Themes are bought only in the Store of limited-time [Events](../EVENTS.md#the-store), each event featuring one UI theme (250 Medals) and one Game theme (450 Medals); Classic is every hero's from the start.
Each theme owned adds to all Gold banked for good, worn or not: a UI theme +1%, a Game theme +2%, in the *Event Collection* line with the bonus tracks' +0.8% each.
Settings' UI Themes and Game Themes menus show Classic and the themes owned, to wear.
With Access to Everything on, both menus list every theme to wear, owning none: a theme worn that way comes off when it is turned off.

## Offline Gold

Each Tower climb, as it ends, keeps the Gold it found and the real time it took (less any time the app was closed during it); Delve runs don't count.
The last ten give the **Recent Gold per Hour**: their Gold over their time, so a long climb weighs more than a short one.
The Settings page's Stats dialog shows it after the Gold earned, shortened (*12.3K*).
**Offline Gold** (2 Breakthrough, straight under Golden Floors, beside Esoteric Training, with Gem Card under it) pays Gold for the time the app was closed.
Starting the app again after a minute or more away (less is no time away) opens the *Offline Gold* dialog, pausing a climb in progress: the time away and the most that pays (*03h:10m offline (of 02h:00m max)*), and the sum, *Recent 12.3K × 02h:00m × 10.0% = 2.46K*.
Time away that pays nothing (no Recent Gold per Hour yet) opens no dialog.
A Champion is paid Offline Gold even without the skill, for twice the time, at the Champion's Club's rate added, and **Offline Gems** besides, listed in the same dialog.
**Claim** banks the Gold, rains coins down the screen, and plays the climb on.
The skill opens two Archives projects: **Offline Gold Multiplier**, the share of the Recent Gold per Hour each hour away pays, 10% at first and 0.5% more a level for 100 levels (60%), on Critical Chance's prices and times; and **Offline Gold Duration**, the most time away that pays, 2 hours at first and 10 minutes more a level for 60 levels (12 hours), each level's Gold and time Offline Gold Multiplier's at the same level, times 1.02 for each level before it.
The time away is on the device's clock for now.

## In the forest

In the forest the purse also shows the Silver each run starts with, once that is any, and the stats show only the keys a run starts with.
In the forest Goals stands in End Climb's place, with **Enter** under it.

## The Shop

Gems buy hand slots (with Larger Hand), Training resets and Arcana respecs.
Once **Training Respec** is owned, each Training row has a reset button that asks to spend 5 Gems on one of two resets, each button naming the ranks it returns (those bought with training points, or those trained with Gold), and allows them only when the Gems are there.
The **Shop**, from the Shop tab at the bottom right of the tab row outside a run (its Back button returns to the page it was opened from), gives 25 Gems free once a day (the day turning at 00:00 GMT on the server's clock; the Shop button and the offer's Claim button show a dot while they wait) and sells, for real money, two limited packs of **Arcanite** (a limited currency with a green crystal icon, shown after Gems on the pages' currency bars and under Gems in the forest; nothing spends them yet): 300 Shards and 250 Gems for $29.99, and 750 Shards and 600 Gems for $59.99, each once every two weeks; Gem packs; and three one-time packs: Permanent Ad-Disable (no ads: the Gem button just pays and the Gold ad button goes, the trainers' ×2 boost for good, ×1.5 Gold), the Special Gold Bundle (×2 Gold, training ×2 for good) and the Champion's Bundle (×3 Gold, training ×3 for good; the three packs' training speeds multiply, ×12 with all three); the Gold bonuses multiply each other, and packs already bought move to the bottom of the page.
Under the limited packs, **Premium Offers** sells one-time offers at $9.99, each with a unique legendary relic: Extra Researcher (one more archivist, beside those Gems hire, and the Archivist Badge, +5% research speed), Extra Trainer (one more trainer, and the Training Accolade, trainers 5% faster and +10% XP) and the Stellar Bundle (500 Gems and the Astral Ribbon, +1% Stellar Floor chance and +1% Stellar Gem chance, each added as points: the Stellar Floor chance with or without Aurum Bloom, and the Stellar Gem chance to a Golden Gem's).
What they give is kept by Erase All.
Every real-money purchase also pays **VIP Points** (a brass crown), 16 for each dollar of its price: 80 for a $4.99 Gem pack or the half-price Ad-Disable, 160 for a $9.99 offer, 480 for a $29.99 one; each card shows its points beside what it grants.
They are kept for good, Erase All included; nothing spends them.
The Champion's Bundle can be bought once Permanent Ad-Disable and the Special Gold Bundle are both owned (until then its button is greyed out and says so).
It makes you a **Champion**: a green *Champion Status* banner tops the Shop, VIP Points show (on the cards, in the banner and in the currencies list), the next two Premium Offers show instead of one, and you get ×2 the offline time paid (Offline Gold even without its skill), +25% XP, the legendary Champion's Cape (+5% ATK and DEF), 20 seconds off the Gem button's cooldown and **Daily Training Points** (1 a day on the Training page, 2, 3 or 5 when a Club tier's chance multiplies it, from 00:00 GMT).
The **Champion's Club** button under the banner lists its tiers, each opened by VIP Points earned in all: Offline Gold and Offline Gems with the bundle, then chances to multiply the daily points, a shorter Gem button cooldown, more Gold, Silver, Golden Gems, a bigger Gem bank, more Gems a reward, Stellar Floors, Dash and End Rush tiles, Gem chance a floor and a chance a reward pays 100 Gems; each grows with every tier past its own, up to its cap, and counts even before what it adds to is open.
From Tier 14, each tier gives a **One Piece Ticket** to claim in the Club and redeem on the Equipment page for a free five-Star Unique of your choice.
See [docs/SHOP.md](../SHOP.md#champion-status-and-the-champions-club) for the tiers.
The server and store are not connected yet, so for now the daily Gems can be claimed and the Gem and Arcanite packs bought without payment (the two weeks counting on the device's clock), and nothing else.
See [docs/SHOP.md](../SHOP.md).
