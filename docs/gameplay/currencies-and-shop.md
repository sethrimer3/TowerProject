# Currencies and the Shop

## Gems

**Gems** are the premium currency, kept between runs like Gold.
The stats show the height in their right-hand column (in the forest, the tower or delve the entrance leads to, such as Tower 2), and beside it, once a run has earned any, the Inspiration (Courage in the Delve) it has earned, its icon and number flashing each time it rises; under them the Inspiration or Courage a new best would pay, and below that the currencies held, one a line: Gems, in the forest Ascension Shards, Gold and, inside a run, its Silver (amounts over 9,999 shortened, such as 123K; the exact amount on hover).
Inside a run a tap on the currencies switches Gold and Silver to the rates the run finds them at, such as *12/min*, over the last minute of play: time the hand waits (paused, or with no card able to act) or a page or dialog covers the board doesn't count, so a pause leaves the rates as they were.

## Gems on the floors

A Gem now and then lies on a floor: on the next new floor a run reaches (a Tower floor not reached before in that run, or each 10 depth of the Delve, within view ahead of the hero), on a plain tile the hero can walk to, out of the way where there is one (off the routes between the hero and the floor's items, monsters, doors and stairs, tucked into a corner or nook), so the hand's cards seldom walk the hero over it, shining over the darkness.
Stepping on it, or tapping it anywhere on the board, collects it in a sparkle for 1 Gem, and the next can't appear for 30 minutes (on the real clock).
A Gem left on a floor the hero leaves, or in a run that ends, is gone, and another appears 3 new floors later, again and again until one is collected (that count carries into the next run).
Undo never brings a collected Gem back or takes one away.

## Ad rewards

Under the currencies, in the forest and inside a run, in the Tower and the Delve alike, a Gem button offers **7 Gems**, marked Claim (for watching an ad, once ads are hooked up; for now pressing it pays them, in a burst of blue glints); it hides once claimed and comes back 10 minutes later.
Inside a run a **×1.5 Gold** ad button stands beside it: each claim (again, no ad plays yet) stores 20 minutes of real time in which all Gold found is multiplied by 1.5, claimable again and again up to two hours stored (the button closes while it holds more than an hour and fifty minutes); while it lasts the button glows and shows the time left, and with none stored it reads *Inactive*.
When the boost ran during a run, its end dialog adds a line for it, such as *50 Gold × 1.5 boost = 75 Gold*.

## In the forest

In the forest the purse also shows the Silver each run starts with, once that is any, and the stats show only the keys a run starts with.
In the forest Goals stands in End Run's place, with **Enter** under it.

## The Shop

Gems buy hand slots (with Larger Hand) and Training resets: each Training row has a reset button that asks to spend 2 Gems on one of two resets, each button naming the ranks it returns (those bought with training points, or those trained with Gold), and allows them only when the Gems are there.
The **Shop**, from the Shop tab at the bottom right of the tab row outside a run (its Back button returns to the page it was opened from; the Upgrades, Research, Deck and Gear pages show every currency held at the top (Training points from the start, Courage once Into the depths is), Silver first, then Gold, a million or more in millions, billions … with two decimals, such as 1.23M), gives 25 Gems free once a day (the day turning at 00:00 GMT on the server's clock; the Shop button and the offer's Claim button show a dot while they wait) and sells, for real money, two limited packs of **Ascension Shards** (a limited currency with a green crystal icon, shown after Gems on the pages' currency bars and under Gems in the forest; nothing spends them yet): 300 Shards and 250 Gems for $29.99, and 750 Shards and 600 Gems for $59.99, each once every two weeks; Gem packs; and three one-time packs: Permanent Ad-Disable (no ads: the Gem button just pays and the Gold ad button goes, the trainers' ×2 boost for good, ×1.5 Gold), the Special Coin Pack (×2 Gold, training ×2 for good) and the Premium Coin Pack (×3 Gold, training ×3 for good; the three packs' training speeds multiply, ×12 with all three); the Gold bonuses multiply each other, and packs already bought move to the bottom of the page.
The server and store are not connected yet, so for now the daily Gems can be claimed and the Gem and Shard packs bought without payment (the two weeks counting on the device's clock), and nothing else.
