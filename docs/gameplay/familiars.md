# Familiars

Familiars are young beasts that hatch from eggs and grow as you spend Event Medals on them.
Each one hatched flies, hops or runs over the board in every climb, Tower and Delve alike, and acts by itself.
They take no notice of walls.
They don't come along to the Proving Grounds.

## Hatching and growing

The Events screen's **Familiars** tab, after the Store, has a row for each familiar.
Press a row's name for what it does, and each stat now and after its next upgrade.

- **Hatch** costs 150, 300, 600, 900 and 1,200 Medals, by how many you already have, whichever egg you choose.
- A hatched familiar shows its four stats. Press one and confirm to raise it a level: the first level costs 100 Medals, and each after it 40 more than the one before.
- A stat at its last level wears a gold box and reads *Max*.
- A stat you can't afford has a grey outline, and an egg you can't afford a red price. Pressing either offers the event's Event Booster, until you have it.
- The switch beside a hatched familiar's name turns it on and off at any time, in a climb or out. Off, it leaves the board, its effect ends and its cooldown waits where it was.

A familiar hatched during a climb joins it at once.
Cooldowns count from one activation to the next, and the first is a whole one.
A new floor interrupts nothing, but burns and lingers stay behind with the floor's enemies; a new climb starts every cooldown over.

An effect happens where its familiar was as the turn began, and reaches every enemy whose tile's center is in its range, Flying enemies too.

### Fire Dragon

It flies five slowly turning petals round the floor, 100 turns a circuit.
When it fires, flames burst out from it and every enemy in range is struck at once and set burning.

- The strike is your ATK times Damage, your critical and Super Critical chances averaged in as Arcana's damage does, less the enemy's DEF. It never heals by Lifesteal or breaks armor.
- A burned enemy takes that again every turn until it falls or you leave the floor, and its ATK is cut by Damage Reduction, in its fights and shots alike.
- A burned enemy wears a small flame at the top of its tile.
- Your Attack Range doesn't widen the Dragon's.

### Lightning Slime

It follows a slowly turning triangle, 60 turns a circuit.
When it fires, a crackling circle stuns every enemy in range for Duration.

- A stunned enemy wears dizzy stars, can't move or shoot, and in a fight skips that many of its strikes before it hits you.
- As the stun ends, Linger slows the enemy for 3 turns: its strikes, shots and steps alike, never by more than 90% with other slows.
- Bosses and Greater Bosses take half the stun and half the slow, rounded down.

### Hunting Wolf

It trots after you, then every Cooldown dashes out to a spot four tiles in from the floor's edge, in a random direction, and stays there for Duration.
Around it, your damage is multiplied by Bonus: fights, arrows, Bombs, damaging Arcana, Lifespring's field, and the Dragon and its burn.
Thorns, Takedown and Voidwell Damage, which take a share of the enemy's HP, aren't multiplied.
Damage predictions count you without it, as the worst case.

### Golden Falcon

It circles the floor, 50 turns a circuit, its circle slowly drifting round the middle.
While its effect lasts, a golden circle follows it, and an enemy killed inside pays Bonus times the Gold, however it fell.
Silver, Lore and drops aren't multiplied.

### Support Wisp

It drifts a slowly turning figure-eight, 80 turns a circuit.
While its effect lasts, it multiplies these values of any familiar inside its circle: the Dragon's Damage, the Slime's Linger, the Wolf's Bonus and the Falcon's Bonus.

## Research

Tower V's floor 60 Goal, **Familiar Research**, opens five projects in the Archives' Familiars group, one for each familiar's Cooldown.
Each takes a turn off a level, for 25 levels: the Dragon's down to 5 turns at its highest level, the others' to 50.
All five share one schedule: 30 million Gold and 1d 14h 53m for the first level, 398.88 billion and 85d 8h 22m for the last, about 1.81 trillion Gold and 577 days each before Research Speed and discounts.

## Settings

Settings → Toggles → Familiars turns the Fire Dragon's damage numbers off.
The damage is dealt all the same, and the burn icon still shows.
