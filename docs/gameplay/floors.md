# Floors

A boss, glowing red and gold with three chevrons, several times a normal monster's HP and the hardest strikes on its floor, guards the way up every ten floors: it stands in front of the stairs on Tower floors 10, 20, 30 and so on, and in the shaft below each Delve milestone gate, so it must be beaten to climb on. A Tower floor also hides a secret: put out every torch on it and a **Greater Boss** appears in a puff of smoke on the open floor nearest the stairs (once a floor each run). Ringed in violet and gold with four chevrons, it has twice a boss's HP, ATK and DEF and pays twice a boss's Gold, Silver and XP. On other Tower floors the stairs may stand open, behind a monster, or behind a yellow door. In the Tower, blue doors and keys first appear on floor 51 of Tower I and five floors earlier in each tower after (floor 11 in Tower IX), red ones on floor 101 and ten floors earlier each tower (floor 21 in Tower IX), and Heart Doors on floor 201 and ten floors earlier each tower (floor 121 in Tower IX). Each starts at about one door in ten floors and grows: blue by a hundredth of a door a floor every five floors, to about two a floor by floor 1,000; red and Heart Doors by as much every ten floors, to about one a floor. Later on, a door often takes two or three colours of key at once (an Amber + Azure, Azure + Crimson or Triune door), and a Heart Door's drain may come on top of a coloured door's keys (an Azure Heart Door takes a blue key and drains HP to 1). **Wooden Doors** take any one key, the cheapest held; a hero with none breaks one down, losing HP equal to its durability (twice the floor's normal monster's ATK, more in each tower; DEF doesn't help, and it never takes the last HP: with too little HP it stays shut). Every yellow-key door on Tower I's first floors is wooden, one in a hundred fewer every ten floors (none past floor 1,000), and each later tower starts ten in a hundred lower (Tower IX's are gone past floor 200). Breaking one down is a toll, not fight damage, so it never costs an area's mastery. Each key colour starts with more keys than its doors need and grows scarcer floor by floor and tower by tower, red the scarcest; the first floor where blue keys appear holds one by the entrance, and the same for red. On side branches, a fork may offer more than one blue or red door, a chance for a hero who comes prepared. In Tower I a blue or red door never stands alone on the way to the stairs: it guards an optional room, or stands in a fork beside a lane that needs neither colour (a yellow door, a monster), so a hero without the key can still climb; later towers may put them on the way up. In Delve I, blue keys and doors first appear on equivalent floor 21 (depth 200) and red ones on 51, and Delve II and the rest have them from their first floors. The first floor's stairs can always be reached without a fight, so the starting hand's Stairs card climbs straight to floor 2 (and its Inspiration); floors 2 to 5 always have a yellow door in front of the stairs, a yellow key lying free near the entrance, and a key behind every door, so the starting hand can open its way up. On Tower I's first ten floors, about a third of the doorways on the way to the stairs hold a health potion where a monster would have stood. No monster on floors 1 to 10 can beat a new hero (100 HP, 12 ATK) in a single fight, but the floor 10 boss can. Strong monsters appear only from floor 11 and elites (from the next zone) only from floor 41, and in the Delve Heart Doors only past equivalent floor 100 in the first cave (from 31 in later ones); the Delve counts its equivalent floors the same way.

Matching open left/right world edges wrap to each other. Locked doors and enemies at the destination still apply. Openings have no solid frame across them and show continuation chevrons. Viewport cropping is not a wrap boundary; the world is 30 tiles wide. Vertical movement remains continuous upward.
