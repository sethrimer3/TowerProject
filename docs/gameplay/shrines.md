# Shrines

**Shrines.** Tower II's floor 150 Goal opens the Archives' **Unlock Shrines** (1.5 million Gold, 4,999 minutes).
Once it is researched, every Tower run earns a **boon** each time its highest floor reaches the next shrine: floor 200, 400, 600 … (200 floors apart for the first twenty boons, then 250, 300 and from the 41st 350), the floors left shown under the floor number.
Under the hero's XP bar, the blue-purple Shrine bar fills as the run climbs from the floor of its latest boon toward the next, with the floors left to the next boon under it.
A diamond over its left end shows how many boons wait to be picked, flashing as each arrives; the bar refills toward the next boon meanwhile.
Pressing the bar offers two boons (up to four with Boon Option Quantity research) to pick one, which lasts the rest of the run, and with none waiting lists the boons taken, copies added up.
Standard boons can be taken several times (×1.20 Max HP, ×1.15 ATK, All Gold Bonuses, Shroud and Silver Bonus, ×1.75 Regen, Interest ×1.50, +5% Free Up Chance, +4 Parry %, −20% Boon Floor Requirement, +1 Max Play Speed): a multiplier's copies add up as (1 + value × copies), an addition's as value × copies, both then raised by Standard Boon Bonus research (+1% a level).
Trade-offs, once each, give a benefit at a cost (×1.50 ATK but bosses ×8 HP, ×1.80 Gold but −70% max HP, −50% enemy HP but −90% Regen and Lifesteal, and more); Improve Trade-off Boons research raises the benefit.
Boons of different types multiply each other: a trade-off's ×1.50 ATK on five ATK copies' ×1.75 makes ×2.625.
A boon applies at once, to the hero's stats as they stand and to what it gains later; undo never takes one back.
Taking a Boon Floor Requirement boon shortens the whole schedule, so several boons can come due at once.
More research, each opened by a later Goal: Floors Required (one floor fewer a level), First Boon Choice (a standard boon heading each run's first offer), Auto Pick Boons (the run takes the top boon itself; toggled in the Shrine bar's list), Ban Boons and Auto Pick Ranking, set on the forest menu's **Shrine Boons** page.
The Delve has no shrines.
